The Anime Mindset moves to animemindset.com

joinkaminari.com keeps the entertainment side (filler lists, watch orders, quotes, recommendations) and takes on ads and affiliate links. The Anime Mindset, the archetype quiz and the Arc Tracker get their own home with no ads. Below are three ways that home could look.

What moves and what stays

The new site is everything that helps someone change their habits. The current site is everything people come to read and watch.

Moves to animemindset.com

  • The Anime Mindset page, which becomes the new home page
  • The archetype quiz and the 9 archetypes
  • The Arc Tracker: sales page, app and its API (sync, photos, reminders, push)
  • “Lessons” blog posts such as Think Like Shikamaru, which become the Codex
  • Gumroad products: the Mindset System templates and the tracker
  • Discord / community waitlist

Stays on joinkaminari.com

  • Filler lists, watch orders, quotes, “anime like…” and character lists
  • Ads and affiliate links, kept off the mindset site
  • About and the team page
  • One banner pointing readers to “Find your anime archetype”
  • The Substack newsletter, unless you split it (see open questions)

The three concepts

Each mockup is a full homepage at animemindset.com, using the real archetypes, ranks and quotes from the current site. Every mockup shows the “moved from joinkaminari.com” notice members would see.

The Arc
Concept 1

The home page is a season of episodes

One long scroll with a title card for each part: The Call, The Training Arc, The Shadow, Power Up, then a finale that sends people to the quiz. Cinematic and dark, keeping Kaminari's cyan.

Best forFirst-time visitors from TikTok and Instagram who need the pitch
Trade-offReturn visitors have to scroll past the story every time

Concept 2

The nine archetypes are the home page

Quiz call-to-action next to a 3×3 grid of archetype “doors”. Picking one (standing in for a quiz result) re-tints the whole site in that archetype's colour and opens its profile page with its shadow, characters, starter habits and rank ladder. Try tapping a few doors.

Best forSearch traffic (one page per archetype) and making each visitor's site feel like theirs
Trade-offLeans hard on the quiz, so the quiz needs to stay excellent

The site is the app

A dashboard with four areas: Train (the Arc Tracker), Codex (lessons), Scrolls (products) and Guild (community and Seasons). Visitors land in a demo account with example data. Tick the habits and switch tabs.

Recommendation

Launch with Concept 2 as the structure and use Concept 1's episode storytelling for the part of the home page below the doors. That gives you a home page that works for new and returning visitors, plus nine archetype pages that can rank on their own.

Grow into Concept 3 once the Arc Tracker is on sale: logged-in members land on their dashboard instead of the marketing page.

Before the move

  • Protect Arc Tracker progress MustProgress is saved in each member's browser for joinkaminari.com. A new domain starts empty, so confirm every member's data is synced to the server (or add an export button) before switching.
  • Set up the 301 redirects MustEvery path in the table above, so search rankings and shared links carry over.
  • Keep the old API routes running MustInstalled home-screen apps keep calling joinkaminari.com until members reinstall from the new domain.
  • Update Gumroad, Substack and social linksProduct pages, receipt emails, link-in-bio and the newsletter footer.
  • Add animemindset.com to Google Search ConsoleThen submit the new sitemap and use the change-of-address tool where it applies.
  • Add the banner on joinkaminari.comThat's where your traffic is today, so it's how people will find the new site.

Open questions for you and Devin

  1. Newsletter: one Substack for both sites, or a separate mindset newsletter?
  2. Look: keep Kaminari's cyan on the new site, or let each archetype's colour lead (Concept 2)?
  3. Branding: does “by Kaminari” stay next to the logo, or does Anime Mindset stand on its own?
  4. Seasons: do you want a shared 30-day challenge at launch? It shapes the Guild and the email plan.
