Green hair sits somewhere between rare and iconic in anime, usually reserved for characters with a bit of an edge: cool-headed swordsmen, chaotic espers, and heroes with a deep connection to nature. We rounded up twenty of the best green hair anime characters, male and female, and ranked them on the personalities that make the color stick.

Green tends to signal calm, growth, or something a little unconventional, which is why it lands on such a wide range of characters. Some of these are famous green anime characters everyone knows on sight, others are cool green anime characters pulled from cult favorites, and a few wear deep dark green while others go bright and vivid. Green haired anime characters really do run the full spectrum.

Fierce warriors, gentle heroes, and at least one tiny hurricane of an esper, this list has them all. We kept it to characters who are green from root to tip, with the two-tone and tipped picks waiting down in the honorable mentions. Let's get into it.

The 20 best green hair anime characters

Gathered from every corner of anime and ranked loosely by how iconic they are. Every one of these green anime characters is green all the way through, no highlights or gradients required.

1. Izuku Midoriya

Anime: My Hero Academia

Izuku is the green-haired heart of modern shonen. A quirkless kid who idolized heroes and refused to give up, Deku claws his way to the top through pure grit, analysis, and an almost reckless need to save everyone. His messy dark green hair is now one of anime's most recognizable silhouettes. Proof that heart, not luck, makes a hero.

2. Roronoa Zoro

Anime: One Piece

Zoro is the moss-headed swordsman fans have loved for decades, and yes, that green hair earned him the nickname. Loyal to a fault, absurdly strong, and completely incapable of finding his way anywhere, he is the Straw Hats' unshakable backbone. His dream to become the world's greatest swordsman drives some of One Piece's very best moments.

3. Tsuyu Asui

Anime: My Hero Academia

Tsuyu, better known as Froppy, is the calm, sensible glue of Class 1-A. With a frog-based Quirk and a habit of saying exactly what everyone is thinking, she is dependable in a crisis and refreshingly honest the rest of the time. Her deep green hair suits a hero who is quietly one of the most capable in her whole class.

4. C.C.

Anime: Code Geass

C.C. is mystery wrapped in pale green hair and centuries of secrets. An immortal witch who strikes the deal that kicks off Code Geass, she is aloof, sardonic, and far more emotionally scarred than she ever lets on. Her long green hair and infamous pizza obsession made her an instant icon. Cool, cryptic, and impossible to forget.

5. Tatsumaki

Anime: One Punch Man

Tatsumaki is a tiny green-haired hurricane with a colossal attitude. The S-Class esper known as Terrible Tornado can toss buildings around with her mind and has absolutely zero patience for anyone who wastes her time. Her curly green hair matches an ego that is, honestly, mostly justified. Small, furious, and terrifyingly powerful.

6. Maki Zenin

Anime: Jujutsu Kaisen

Maki is the powerless powerhouse who refuses to be written off. Born into a sorcerer clan with almost no cursed energy, she trains her body and her weapon skills into something that leaves full sorcerers eating dust. Her muted green hair suits a fighter defined by sheer, stubborn will. One of Jujutsu Kaisen's most satisfying underdogs.

7. Maomao

Anime: The Apothecary Diaries

Maomao is a poison-obsessed apothecary with a mind like a scalpel. Dragged into palace intrigue against her will, she solves mysteries out of pure scientific curiosity while staying gloriously indifferent to court politics. Her green hair suits a heroine who cares more about a good experiment than anyone's opinion of her. Deadpan, brilliant, and a big modern favorite.

8. Nelliel Tu Odelschwanck

Anime: Bleach

Nel is one of Bleach's most beloved Arrancars, bouncing between a childlike form and a powerful adult warrior. Kind-hearted and fiercely protective of her friends, she pairs a gentle spirit with genuinely fearsome combat skill. Her bright green hair makes her instantly recognizable in a massive cast. All heart, plenty of power.

9. Fubuki

Anime: One Punch Man

Fubuki, the Blizzard of Hell, leads her own team of heroes and spends half her time trying to step out of her sister Tatsumaki's shadow. Ambitious, proud, and secretly insecure, she is far more sympathetic than her ice-queen exterior suggests. Her green hair suits a character quietly working to prove she belongs. Complicated in the best way.

10. Sailor Neptune

Anime: Sailor Moon

Michiru, better known as Sailor Neptune, might just be the most recognizable green-haired character in anime history. Elegant, refined, and deeply devoted to her partner Sailor Uranus, she fights with a grace that matches her sea-green waves. A talented violinist and a fierce guardian in equal measure. Timeless in every sense of the word.

11. Freed Justine

Anime: Fairy Tail

Freed is Fairy Tail's rune mage, a stylish, intensely loyal fighter devoted to Laxus and his Thunder Legion. Refined and a little dramatic, he backs up all the flair with genuinely clever, trap-based magic. His long green hair suits a character who fights with his mind as much as his blade. Elegant, loyal, and quietly formidable.

12. Amaimon

Anime: Blue Exorcist

Amaimon is the King of Earth, a demon prince who treats world-ending battles like a bored afternoon hobby. Childish, unpredictable, and forever snacking on something, he brings a creepy, playful energy to every scene he crashes. His spiky green hair suits an antagonist who is equal parts menace and mischief. Unsettling and weirdly fun.

13. Envy

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist

Envy is one of the nastiest, most memorable villains in Fullmetal Alchemist, a shapeshifting homunculus who delights in cruelty. Spiteful and gleefully manipulative, Envy embodies the sin they are named for, resenting the very humans they torment. That palm-tree of green hair caps off one of anime's most hateable, and most fascinating, antagonists.

14. Jae-ha

Anime: Yona of the Dawn

Jae-ha, the Green Dragon, is all charm, freedom, and impossible legwork. A former pirate who values his independence above almost everything, he joins Yona's group strictly on his own terms and slowly learns what loyalty really means. His long green hair suits a character who is equal parts flirt and fierce protector. Effortlessly cool.

15. N

Anime: Pokemon

N is one of Pokemon's most complex characters, a gentle idealist raised to believe humans and Pokemon should be separated for the creatures' own good. Sincere, brilliant, and genuinely conflicted, he challenges the whole premise of the series with real conviction. His long green hair suits a would-be king whose heart is in the right place, even when his methods are not.

16. Lubbock

Anime: Akame ga Kill

Lubbock is the wisecracking heart of Night Raid, a wire-wielding fighter hiding real depth behind constant jokes and flirting. Loyal to his friends and quietly braver than he lets on, he brings some rare levity to one of anime's darkest series. His green hair suits a character who jokes his way through a war he knows he might not survive.

17. Bartolomeo

Anime: One Piece

Bartolomeo is a foul-mouthed, chaotic pirate who also happens to be the single biggest Straw Hat fanboy in the world. Rude to everyone except his idols, he pairs an obnoxious attitude with a genuinely clever barrier power. His spiky green mohawk suits a character who is far more capable, and far more loyal, than he first appears. Chaos with a soft center.

18. Ranka Lee

Anime: Macross Frontier

Ranka is a bright-eyed aspiring idol whose voice ends up mattering to the fate of the entire series. Sweet, earnest, and a little clumsy, she chases her dream with a sincerity that is impossible not to root for. Her vivid green hair suits a character who lights up every stage she steps onto. Pure, hopeful, and full of heart.

19. Jolyne Kujo

Anime: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

Jolyne is the first female JoJo, and she carries the torch with style. Tough, quick-witted, and fiercely protective of the people she loves, she fights her way through a prison sentence and a heavy family legacy with equal determination. Her green hair, twisted into signature buns, suits a heroine who simply refuses to break. A worthy heir to the Joestar name.

20. Shintaro Midorima

Anime: Kuroko's Basketball

Midorima is a genius shooter whose name literally contains the word for green. Obsessed with horoscopes and daily lucky items, he pairs bizarre superstition with flawless three-point accuracy from anywhere on the court. His green hair and taped fingers make him unmistakable. Rigid, prideful, and secretly a big softie for his teammates.

Honorable mentions

A few more with green in the mix, just not head to toe: Gon Freecss, whose black hair only greens at the tips in Hunter x Hunter, Senku Ishigami, whose two-tone hair splits white and green in Dr. Stone, and Mitsuri Kanroji, whose signature locks fade from pink into green in Demon Slayer. All iconic, all a little more than simply green.

Green never blends in

Warrior, esper, villain, or idol, green hair anime characters have a way of standing out even in the most crowded cast. The color reads as calm, natural, and just a little unconventional, which is exactly why writers hand it to characters with a real edge. Pick a favorite and you are choosing one of anime's most distinctive design choices.

FAQs

Who is the most famous green hair anime character?

Izuku Midoriya and Roronoa Zoro are the most iconic green hair anime characters, topping nearly every fan ranking. Tsuyu Asui, Gon Freecss, and C.C. sit close behind, each instantly recognizable for their green locks. If you want one answer to who is the anime character with green hair most people picture, it is usually Deku.

Who are the best female green haired anime characters?

Some of the best female green haired anime characters are C.C., Tatsumaki, Tsuyu Asui, Nelliel, and Sailor Neptune. They range from immortal witches to tiny psychic powerhouses, which proves green suits just about every kind of heroine.

Which anime characters have dark green hair?

For dark green hair, Izuku Midoriya, Tsuyu Asui, and Maki Zenin lead the way, all sporting a deep, muted green rather than a bright shade. Fans also search for characters with short green hair, and Tsuyu and Fubuki fit that bill nicely too.

Are there green and black haired anime characters?

Yes, though the most famous ones read as more black than green. Gon Freecss is the classic anime character with green and black hair, his black spikes tipped in green, while Senku Ishigami splits his between white and green. Both just miss a strictly green list.

What does green hair mean in anime?

Green often symbolizes calm, nature, growth, or a character who stands a little apart from the crowd. It tends to land on cool-headed types, nature-connected heroes, and the occasional chaotic wildcard. More than anything, green hair signals a character who was designed to be noticed.
