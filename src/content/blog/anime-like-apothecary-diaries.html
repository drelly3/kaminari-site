The anime most like The Apothecary Diaries, ranked

Here are the anime like The Apothecary Diaries that genuinely share its build, ranked from the closest match to the loosest. The Similarity Rating is our read on how near each one gets to Maomao's story specifically, not how good the show is overall, and we kept the stars to whole numbers. The build we are measuring against is simple: a clever heroine solving episodic mysteries through expertise, usually with a court or a slow-burn in the mix.

1. Raven of the Inner Palace

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Jusetsu is the Raven Consort, a woman who lives deep in the inner palace but never serves the emperor, keeping to herself and her strange arts. People come to her with problems the court cannot explain, curses, hauntings, deaths with no clear cause, and she untangles them one by one. The young emperor Koushun takes an interest in her, and a careful bond forms between them. The setting is a richly drawn, Chinese-inspired court full of secrets.

Of everything on this list, this mirrors The Apothecary Diaries most directly, almost beat for beat. It is the same build: a self-possessed woman using specialized knowledge to solve case-of-the-week mysteries inside an imperial inner palace, with a slow, careful romance simmering underneath. The puzzles lean supernatural rather than pharmacological, so the expertise is spiritual instead of medical, but the rhythm of the cases and the mood of the palace feel like home.

2. Ameku M.D.: Doctor Detective

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Takao Ameku is a doctor at a university hospital with an almost unsettling gift for reading a body and catching the one detail everyone else missed. Patients arrive with baffling symptoms, and she treats each case like a puzzle, following the evidence to a diagnosis nobody saw coming. The series runs as a string of self-contained medical mysteries. It trades palace robes for a white coat and a modern hospital.

If what hooked you was Maomao diagnosing a poisoning from a symptom nobody else connected, this is that exact engine in a modern setting. Every case is a medical mystery cracked through expertise rather than luck, and the deduction stays grounded in real knowledge, just like Maomao's apothecary work. The court and the slow-burn are gone, but the single most distinctive thing about The Apothecary Diaries, a brilliant mind solving the unsolvable through medicine, sits front and center.

3. Gosick

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Victorique is a tiny, doll-like genius who rarely leaves a library tower in a small 1920s European country, solving crimes she never witnesses firsthand. The earnest transfer student Kazuya brings her the puzzles and does the legwork, and the two form an odd, close partnership. The mysteries start small and grow darker and more personal as a war-shadowed setting closes in. It is dripping with period atmosphere.

On pure structure, this is one of the closest cousins to The Apothecary Diaries anywhere. A deadpan genius heroine who runs circles around everyone, a steady stream of case-of-the-week mysteries solved by sheer reasoning, a richly detailed period setting, and a slow-burn built on a loyal partnership, it hits the same core beats. Swap the 1920s European backdrop for an imperial court and you are most of the way to Maomao's world.

4. In/Spectre

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: At eleven, Kotoko Iwanaga was taken by yokai and made their “God of Wisdom,” a mediator between spirits and humans, at the cost of an eye and a leg. Years later she solves the supernatural's problems using logic and, when the plain truth will not do, cleverly constructed fictions. She drags along Kuro, a university student with a secret of his own she is determined to marry. Each arc is a strange case she reasons her way through.

The build lines up neatly: a sharp, unflappable heroine who solves case-of-the-week mysteries with knowledge and deduction, plus a slow-burn romance she keeps nudging forward. Like Maomao, Kotoko is the smartest person in any room and completely unbothered about it, and the fun is watching her think her way to an answer no one else can reach. The mysteries are supernatural rather than medical, but the deduction-and-romance rhythm is pure Apothecary Diaries.

5. Snow White with the Red Hair

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Shirayuki is a skilled herbalist who refuses to become a prince's possession, so she flees her home and builds a new life in a neighboring kingdom. There she earns a position as a court pharmacist on the strength of her knowledge alone, winning respect one remedy at a time. Along the way she grows close to Prince Zen, and the two come to support each other as equals. It is a warm, gentle royal romance.

The overlap with The Apothecary Diaries is strongest on the heroine: a pharmacist who earns her place at court through expertise rather than rank or beauty, exactly like Maomao. The herbalism, the palace setting, and the slow-burn with a highborn love interest all rhyme. Where it parts ways is the mystery, since this is more a competence-and-romance story than a case-of-the-week puzzle, so the pull is the capable-woman-at-court fantasy rather than the deduction.

6. Parallel World Pharmacy

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Falma is a modern pharmacologist reborn into a fantasy world where medicine is primitive and curable diseases still kill. Armed with real pharmaceutical knowledge, he opens a pharmacy and begins treating ailments everyone else had written off as hopeless. Each case is a new illness to diagnose and a new remedy to prepare. The world slowly takes notice of the miracle-working young apothecary.

For the medicine at the heart of The Apothecary Diaries, this is the most direct match on the list. It runs on the same satisfaction that powers Maomao's best moments: solving a problem with real pharmacological knowledge, the right compound for the right ailment. It is lighter and more wholesome, with no court intrigue or poison plots, so it trades the danger for the pure pleasure of expertise saving the day.

7. Ron Kamonohashi's Forbidden Deductions

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Ron is a brilliant detective forbidden from practicing after a past case went catastrophically wrong, so he works vicariously through Totomaru, an earnest and hapless young officer. Together they take on a run of bizarre murders and locked-room puzzles. Ron's deductions are dazzling and more than a little unhinged. The tone is loud, fast, and frequently comedic.

The case-by-case structure and the eccentric genius at the center map cleanly onto what made Maomao's investigations click: a mind several steps ahead, piecing together the one clue that cracks everything open. It plays far louder and sillier than The Apothecary Diaries, and there is no court or medicine to it, but for the pure deduction rush, the thrill of watching a genius reason it out, it delivers with a grin.

8. The Case Files of Jeweler Richard

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Seigi, a college student with a strong sense of justice, saves an elegant foreign jeweler named Richard and ends up working part-time at his Ginza shop. Each client arrives with a gemstone and a hidden story, and Richard's gemological expertise slowly draws out the truth behind it. The cases are quiet and human, built on appraisal and insight rather than crime scenes. A careful relationship grows between the two leads.

The connection is the expertise: like Maomao reading a poisoning, Richard reads a gemstone and reasons out the human secret attached to it, one self-contained case at a time. It is a gentler, talkier kind of mystery, more about people than murders, and there is no court or palace in sight. But the core pleasure, a specialist solving episodic puzzles with knowledge nobody else has, sits squarely in Apothecary Diaries territory.

9. Hyouka

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Oreki would rather conserve energy than lift a finger, but his relentlessly curious classmate Chitanda keeps pulling him into small mysteries hiding in everyday school life. A misread note, an old grievance, a decades-buried secret, each one is less a crime than a human puzzle. Oreki solves them almost reluctantly, with quiet brilliance. It is a gorgeously animated slice of deduction.

For the quieter side of The Apothecary Diaries, the cases that turn on a misunderstanding or a hidden kindness rather than a murder, this is the gold standard. The engine is the same: a sharp mind reasoning out the truth from tiny, overlooked clues. The stakes are low and the setting is a modern high school rather than a palace, which is why it sits mid-list, but the pleasure of watching someone think is identical.

10. Ascendance of a Bookworm

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Myne is reborn into a rigid, class-bound world with almost no books, so she sets out to make her own, and the simple goal spirals into a methodical climb through a society never built for someone like her. She problem-solves her way forward with knowledge from a past life, inventing and negotiating at every turn. A deep, well-drawn cast fills out the world around her. It is cozy, clever, and quietly ambitious.

The link to The Apothecary Diaries is the heroine and her method: a brilliant mind solving problem after problem with knowledge nobody else has, charming and outmaneuvering a world that underestimates her. It swaps poisons and court schemes for papermaking, printing, and economics, so there is no mystery engine here. But if what you loved was Maomao's resourcefulness and the rich supporting cast around her, this scratches that itch beautifully.

11. Moriarty the Patriot

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: William James Moriarty is a brilliant young man who looks at a rotten, rigid aristocracy and decides the only cure is to tear it down from the shadows. Alongside his brothers, he engineers elaborate schemes against the cruel and the powerful in Victorian England. The series plays out as a run of intricate, morally tangled plots. It eventually draws a certain famous detective into its orbit.

The closeness is the period intrigue and the pleasure of watching a genius several moves ahead of everyone, the same chess-under-the-surface feeling as the politics of Maomao's court. The difference is the engine, since Moriarty builds crimes rather than solving them, and the tone runs darker and more tragic than The Apothecary Diaries. It sits lower for that reason, but for elaborate period scheming led by a brilliant mind, few do it better.

FAQs

Is there an anime exactly like The Apothecary Diaries?

Not a perfect twin, but Raven of the Inner Palace comes closest: a knowing woman solving case-of-the-week mysteries inside a Chinese-inspired inner palace, with a slow-burn romance under it all. For the medical-deduction side specifically, Ameku M.D.: Doctor Detective is the sharpest match, and Gosick nails the deadpan-genius-heroine-solving-period-cases shape.

What should fans watch after The Apothecary Diaries Season 2?

If you want the easiest transition, begin at the top of this list: Raven of the Inner Palace, Ameku M.D., Gosick, In/Spectre, or Snow White with the Red Hair. Each one carries over the clever heroine, the episodic mystery, or the expertise that made Season 2 so moreish.

When does The Apothecary Diaries Season 3 come out?

Season 3 premieres on October 2, 2026 on Crunchyroll, in a split-cour format with the first half airing in fall 2026 and the second half in spring 2027. The series' first theatrical film, featuring an original story by author Natsu Hyuga, opens in Japan on December 11, 2026.

What is the closest mystery anime to The Apothecary Diaries?

For pure deduction, Gosick is the strongest structural match, with a tiny genius solving period mysteries beside a loyal partner. In/Spectre, Ron Kamonohashi's Forbidden Deductions, and Hyouka are close behind, ranging from supernatural puzzles to loud comedy to quiet, elegant everyday mysteries.
