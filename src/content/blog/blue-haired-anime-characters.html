Blue haired anime characters have a way of standing out, and not by accident. In a medium where hair color doubles as personality shorthand, blue tends to land on the calm ones, the cool ones, the fiercely loyal ones, and the occasional lovable disaster. We pulled together twenty of the best, spanning every shade and every kind of character.

Blue comes in every version here. We have got light blue hair anime characters like Aqua and Kuroko, dark blue hair anime characters like Hinata and Rin, more than one anime character with long blue hair, and even a blue spiky hair anime character in Grimmjow. Heroes, villains, goddesses, and disasters, male and female alike.

Some are the most famous blue hair anime characters in the medium, the ones you picture the second the topic comes up. Others are quiet favorites worth discovering. Every pick earns its spot on personality, presence, and the moments that made it matter, not just the color of its hair. Let's get into it.

The 19 best blue haired anime characters

These are our favorites from every corner of anime, the characters who made blue hair unforgettable through who they are, not just how they look. No strict ranking, just twenty who wear the color best.

1. Rem

Anime: Re:Zero

Rem is the character who turned blue hair into a full-blown fandom moment. The devoted maid of the Roswaal mansion went from background player to the center of the “best girl” debate on the strength of one unforgettable confession scene. Fiercely loyal, quietly heartbroken, and impossible not to root for. The short blue bob is iconic, but it is her devotion people remember.

2. Rimuru Tempest

Anime: That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime

Rimuru starts as a slime and ends up running a nation, which is a career glow-up nobody saw coming. In humanoid form, the sky-blue hair and gold eyes are instantly recognizable, but the real draw is a leader who is equal parts kind, ruthless, and weirdly relatable. Few isekai protagonists carry a series this effortlessly.

3. Hinata Hyuga

Anime: Naruto

Hinata spent most of Naruto as the shy girl watching from the sidelines, then quietly became one of its most beloved characters through sheer heart. Her dark indigo hair frames a Hyuga heiress who found her courage the hard way, standing her ground when it counted most. Gentle, brave, and steadier than anyone gives her credit for.

4. Juvia Lockser

Anime: Fairy Tail

Juvia is devotion turned all the way up. Her deep blue hair suits a rain-summoning water mage whose entire personality reorganized itself the moment she met Gray Fullbuster. Obsessive, dramatic, and genuinely powerful once a fight breaks out, she is one of Fairy Tail's most entertaining guild members. Nobody on this list loves harder, for better or worse.

5. Grimmjow Jaegerjaquez

Anime: Bleach

Grimmjow is the blue spiky hair anime character every Bleach fan has a soft spot for. As an Espada, he is pure feral pride, a villain who fights because losing to anyone is unthinkable to him. The electric blue hair matches the energy exactly: unpredictable, aggressive, and always hunting the next challenge.

6. Franky

Anime: One Piece

Franky is the cyborg shipwright who runs on cola and pure enthusiasm, blue hair shaped into whatever ridiculous style the moment calls for. Loud, sentimental, and endlessly loyal to the Straw Hats, he builds the crew everything from warships to weapons, striking a pose the entire time. All heart and horsepower, with a real genius hiding under the muscle.

7. Rei Ayanami

Anime: Neon Genesis Evangelion

Rei is quiet in a way that gets under your skin. The pale blue hair and blank stare made her an instant icon, but the mystery of who she actually is powers some of Evangelion's most haunting moments. Withdrawn, enigmatic, and far more central to the whole story than she first appears.

8. Esdeath

Anime: Akame ga Kill

Esdeath is proof that a villain can walk off with the entire show. A general with light blue hair and a chilling worldview (the strong live, the weak die), she is terrifying and magnetic at the same time. The scariest part is how much fun she clearly seems to be having.

9. Aqua

Anime: KonoSuba

Aqua is a literal goddess who is also a complete disaster, and that is precisely why fans adore her. Flowing light blue hair, a water-molecule hair clip, and absolutely no impulse control. She is supposed to be the party's divine support and spends most of KonoSuba crying, scheming, or making everything worse. Comedy gold.

10. Kuroko Tetsuya

Anime: Kuroko's Basketball

Kuroko is the phantom sixth man, the player nobody notices until the ball is already gone. His pale light blue hair suits a personality so low-key he practically vanishes on the court, which is the whole point of his game. Calm, sharp, and quietly lethal through misdirection.

11. Nagisa Shiota

Anime: Assassination Classroom

Nagisa looks like the gentlest kid in class, right up until he reveals a talent for assassination that unsettles everyone around him. The light blue hair and soft smile hide a genuinely frightening focus. He is the quiet one you absolutely do not want to underestimate.

12. Jellal Fernandes

Anime: Fairy Tail

Jellal is Fairy Tail's tortured antihero, his blue hair marked by that unmistakable red tattoo across one eye. His arc runs from brainwashed villain to a man desperate to atone, and the guilt he carries gives every scene real weight. Complicated, haunted, and always trying to do better than his past.

13. Ami Mizuno

Anime: Sailor Moon

Sailor Mercury was rocking blue hair before half this list existed. Ami is the brains of the Sailor Guardians, a shy honor student who fights with water and ice and out-thinks every enemy she meets. Kind, studious, and quietly one of the most dependable characters in the entire series.

14. Nefertari Vivi

Anime: One Piece

Vivi is the princess who sailed with the Straw Hats and earned every bit of their loyalty. Her long blue hair suits a leader willing to risk everything for her kingdom of Alabasta. Brave, selfless, and one of the most quietly heroic allies Luffy's crew ever picked up.

15. Rin Okumura

Anime: Blue Exorcist

Rin is the son of Satan who decides to become an exorcist anyway, which is about as complicated as a heritage gets. His dark blue hair and blue flames mark him as something dangerous, but he leads with a hot temper and an even bigger heart. A troublemaker you cannot help but root for.

16. Nejire Hado

Anime: My Hero Academia

Nejire is one of UA's Big Three, and her long, flowing periwinkle hair matches a mind that bounces from question to question at light speed. Endlessly curious and cheerful, she hides serious power behind all that bubbly energy. Underestimate her and you will regret it fast.

17. Himmel

Anime: Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

Himmel is the hero whose legend hangs over all of Frieren, the blue-haired leader of the party that once saved the world. Even in flashback he radiates warmth and quiet wisdom, and his kindness echoes through the whole series long after he is gone. Proof a character can define a story without much screen time.

18. Sayaka Miki

Anime: Puella Magi Madoka Magica

Sayaka is the magical girl whose story quietly breaks your heart. Bright blue hair, a hero's ideals, and a wish made for someone else set her on one of the most tragic arcs in the genre. Loyal to a fault and painfully human, she lingers with you long after the credits roll.

19. Aladdin

Anime: Magi

Aladdin is the small, cheerful magician at the center of Magi, his blue braid swinging as he stumbles into world-shaping adventures. Behind the childlike grin sits one of the most powerful and wise figures in the entire series. Warm, curious, and far deeper than he first lets on.

20. Mukuro Rokudo

Anime: Katekyo Hitman Reborn!

Mukuro is a mist-shrouded schemer with a grudge against the world and a habit of never quite dying. Calm, cryptic, and always three plans ahead, he is the kind of antagonist who slowly becomes impossible not to like. His dark blue hair suits a character built almost entirely out of mystery. Endlessly cool.

Honorable mentions

A few blue-adjacent favorites that just missed the strict cut: Inosuke Hashibira, whose black hair only turns blue at the tips, and Bulma, whose shade has drifted between blue, teal, and green across the years. Tenya Iida keeps Class 1-A in line with his dark blue mane, and Konan folds paper into deadly art over in Naruto.

Blue hair, unforgettable every time

Loyal maids, feral Espadas, disaster goddesses: blue haired anime characters cover an enormous range, and that is exactly the fun of it. The color might be the reason they first caught your eye, but it is the personalities underneath, Rem's devotion, Aqua's chaos, Grimmjow's pride, that make them stick. Pick a favorite and you are really picking a character, not a shade.

FAQs

Who are the most famous blue hair anime characters?

Rem from Re:Zero is probably the single most famous, right up there with Rei Ayanami and Rimuru Tempest. Hinata Hyuga and Grimmjow round out the names most fans picture first when the topic comes up.

Who are the best female anime characters with blue hair?

Rem, Hinata Hyuga, Rei Ayanami, Esdeath, and Aqua lead the pack, covering everything from loyal maids to terrifying generals to chaotic goddesses. There is a lot more range here than the color alone suggests.

Who are the best blue-haired anime guys?

For the guys, Grimmjow, Jellal, Rin Okumura, and Franky stand out, ranging from feral villains to loyal shipwrights. Kuroko and Nagisa are the top male light blue haired anime characters, both landing on the quieter, calmer side.

Which characters with light blue hair stand out?

Aqua, Kuroko, Nagisa Shiota, and Rimuru Tempest are the go-to characters with light blue hair, mostly sitting on the calmer or more cheerful end of the personality scale.

Who has short or long blue hair in anime?

Among anime characters with short blue hair, Rem and Kuroko lead the way. For long blue hair characters, Nejire Hado and Nefertari Vivi are the standouts, both wearing it well past the shoulders.
