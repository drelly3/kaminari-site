The anime most like Dandadan, ranked

Here are the anime like Dandadan that genuinely share its build, ranked from the closest match to the loosest. The Similarity Rating is our read on how near each one gets to Dandadan specifically, not how good the show is overall, and we kept the stars to whole numbers. The build we are measuring against is simple: a frenetic, genre-blending supernatural action-comedy with a central pair, wild animation, and real heart under the chaos.

1. Mob Psycho 100

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Shigeo “Mob” Kageyama is a painfully ordinary middle schooler who happens to be one of the most powerful psychics alive, working part-time for a con-artist exorcist while trying to live a normal life. He battles evil spirits and rival espers, but the real story is his struggle to grow as a person and keep his overwhelming power in check. The animation explodes into jaw-dropping surreal set-pieces whenever he lets loose. Under the spectacle is one of the most heartfelt coming-of-age stories in anime.

Of everything here, Mob Psycho 100 is the undisputed twin in visual execution and spirit-world absurdity. Created by ONE and elevated by master animators, it shares Science SARU's wildly expressive, morphing sakuga, its dynamic hand-drawn camera work, and its modern Japanese urban spirit exorcisms, all centered on a socially awkward, earnest boy finding his emotional footing amidst chaos. Mob's spirit battles and his quiet struggle to grow mirror the way Dandadan buries real heart inside its madness. If you loved how it swings from hilarious to breathtaking to moving in a single episode, start here.

2. Chainsaw Man

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Denji is a broke teenager who hunts devils to pay off a dead man's debt, until a betrayal fuses him with his chainsaw-devil companion and turns him into something new. Recruited by a government devil-hunting agency, he throws himself into a brutal, chaotic world while chasing the simplest teenage dreams. The series swings between crude comedy, shocking violence, and raw emotion without warning. The animation is gorgeous and unhinged.

Chainsaw Man is the direct creative family tree. Dandadan author Yukinobu Tatsu served as Tatsuki Fujimoto's lead assistant on Chainsaw Man, and the shared DNA is everywhere: identical rapid-fire pacing, juvenile sex jokes blended with raw heart, sudden transitions into grotesque body horror, and an eccentric cast operating purely on raw instinct. Denji's horny, earnest cluelessness and the show's habit of lurching from a gross-out gag to genuine devastation feel deeply Dandadan. It runs darker and more nihilistic than Dandadan's brighter chaos, so the tone differs, but the lineage is written all over it.

3. FLCL

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Naota is a bored kid in a nothing town whose life detonates when a wild woman on a Vespa runs him over, smacks him with a guitar, and moves into his house, right as robots start erupting from his head. What follows is a frenzied, surreal six-episode blast of sci-fi weirdness, coming-of-age confusion, and rock-and-roll energy. Nothing is explained and everything moves at a thousand miles an hour. It is a cult classic for a reason.

FLCL is the pure tonal engine. Dandadan director Fuga Yamashiro explicitly channels the manic, hormonal, coming-of-age hysteria of Gainax's FLCL, and once you watch the two together the link is obvious. A chaotic older woman and alien figure detonates an insecure boy's ordinary life, strange entities erupt from bodily cavities, and emotional repression manifests as reality-warping combat, which is Dandadan's whole engine compressed into a six-episode blast. The sheer visual inventiveness and the aliens-crashing-into-a-teen's-life premise feel like a clear ancestor. It is shorter, stranger, and less plot-driven, but the hyperactive, heartfelt, sci-fi-soaked vibe is pure Dandadan.

4. Undead Unluck

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Prime Video

Synopsis: Fuuko is a girl whose touch brings devastating bad luck to anyone near her, and Andy is an immortal man who wants nothing more than to finally die. The two form an unlikely pair and get swept into a secret organization that hunts people with bizarre, reality-bending powers called Negators. What starts as a chaotic action-comedy keeps peeling back layers of genuine tragedy and world-shaking stakes. It is loud, strange, and surprisingly emotional.

Undead Unluck is the closest match for high-concept, boundary-pushing rules and chaotic male-female duo dynamics. Andy and Fuuko mirror Okarun and Momo's escalating combat synergy, bouncing between bizarre alien and UMA fights, graphic bodily transformations, and a genuine, protective romance. The series shares Dandadan's knack for balancing crude humor, wild powers, and sudden gut-punches, with strange abilities governed by strict, clever rules. The breakneck energy and the central-duo heart make it feel like a close cousin, even without the ghosts.

5. Kill la Kill

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Ryuko Matoi storms into a tyrannical academy wielding half a giant scissor-blade, hunting the person who killed her father, and discovers a living sailor uniform that grants her terrifying power. What follows is an absurd, breakneck war against the student council and the forces behind it. The animation is loud, fast, and gleefully over-the-top. It is as ridiculous as it is secretly heartfelt.

Kill la Kill captures the blistering, unapologetic pace, the loud visual gags, and the transforming bodies. It turns eccentric clothing and alien fibers into high-octane spectacle, and it shares Dandadan's complete lack of creative self-censorship and its frenetic, never-sits-still momentum. The brash, lovable female lead and the lurch from gag to genuine emotion rhyme directly with Momo. There are no ghosts or aliens of Dandadan's kind, so the supernatural element is missing, but the maximalist, funny, heartfelt chaos is a real match.

6. Jujutsu Kaisen

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Yuji Itadori swallows a cursed object to protect his friends and is pulled into a hidden war between sorcerers and curses, the monsters born from human fear. He enrolls at a secret school for sorcerers and learns to fight alongside a ranked organization of specialists. The threats escalate fast, and the cost is brutal. The animation delivers some of the most explosive fights around.

Jujutsu Kaisen is the modern benchmark for urban Japanese curses, occult high school clubs, and monstrous physical manifestations born from negative human grudges. Most tellingly, Okarun's Turbo Granny curse shares the exact trope of harboring a dangerous, foul-mouthed ancient entity inside your own body, the same setup as Yuji harboring Sukuna. The occult-threats-hiding-in-the-everyday premise and the spectacular fights land for Dandadan fans who came for the supernatural action. It is darker, more combat-focused, and lighter on the romance and the chaos, which places it mid-list, but the curse-fueled core connects hard. Our Jujutsu Kaisen filler list sorts the handful of skippable detours.

7. Noragami

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Yato is a minor, broke god who takes odd jobs for five yen apiece, dreaming of his own shrine and a legion of worshippers. When a schoolgirl named Hiyori is dragged into his world, she gets tangled up with him and his troubled weapon-spirit partner, Yukine. Together they handle the dangerous phantoms that haunt the boundary between the living and the dead. It blends supernatural action, comedy, and real emotional heft.

Noragami blends modern Japanese cityscapes with ancient Shinto folklore and the Ayakashi, the spirits that haunt the edges of the living world. It captures Dandadan's balance between quirky, low-stakes comedy and emotional battles against corrupt phantom entities that threaten regular high schoolers. The comedic banter, the romance simmering between Yato and Hiyori, and the surprisingly heavy character arcs all echo Momo and Okarun's dynamic. It is a little more grounded in folklore and a little less frenetic, but the heart-plus-humor-plus-spirits formula is a close fit.

8. Yu Yu Hakusho

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Delinquent teenager Yusuke Urameshi dies saving a child, then gets a second chance at life as a Spirit Detective tasked with policing threats from the demon world. He gathers a crew of rivals-turned-allies and fights his way through tournaments and demon invasions. The series blends supernatural action, comedy, and a surprising amount of heart. It is a foundational classic of the genre.

Yu Yu Hakusho is the 90s ancestor of the urban supernatural brawler. High school delinquents rub shoulders with the spirit world, eccentric yokai, and psychic martial arts tournaments, all grounded by a fierce, ride-or-die romantic bond between Yusuke and Keiko that rhymes with Momo and Okarun's. Yusuke's rough charm, the comedic banter, and the mix of the occult with everyday life carry a clear Dandadan flavor, decades early. It is more tournament-focused and less surreal, which keeps it mid-list, but the ghosts-meet-attitude energy is a genuine fit.

9. Bleach

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Hulu

Synopsis: Ichigo Kurosaki is a scowling teenager who has always been able to see ghosts, until a Soul Reaper named Rukia transfers her powers to him during a Hollow attack and upends his life. Now tasked with guarding his suburban town from the monstrous Hollows that prey on wandering souls, he balances high school with a secret war in the spirit world. A deep cast and a steady escalation turn his neighborhood duty into something far larger. It is one of the definitive shonen classics.

Bleach is the blueprint for a high schooler discovering spiritual sight, combating monstrous Hollows that manifest in suburban neighborhoods, and dealing with eccentric guardian figures while juggling class life, which is Okarun and Momo's starting situation drawn a generation earlier. The ordinary-kid-pulled-into-a-spirit-war premise and the way the everyday and the occult keep colliding feel distinctly Dandadan. It is far more combat-driven and epic in scope, with little of the romance or the comedy chaos, so it sits lower, but the spiritual-sight-meets-suburbia core is a clear ancestor. 10. Soul Eater

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: At a school for weapon-wielding meisters and the human weapons they partner with, teams of students hunt evil souls to turn their blades into legendary death-scythes. Maka and her scythe partner Soul, along with a cast of oddballs, face witches, madness, and monsters. The series pairs stylish, gothic-cartoon visuals with fast, funny action. It is weird, bold, and endlessly energetic.

Soul Eater features stylized supernatural combat, madcap cartoon expression, and witch-and-monster hunting, centered on dedicated boy-girl pairs who synchronize their soul wavelengths to battle creeping internal madness. That partner-pair synergy maps straight onto Momo and Okarun fighting in sync, and the gothic-cartoon art style gives it the same irreverent, high-energy feel. It is more of an ensemble and less focused on a single romance, which lands it lower, but the weird, stylish action-comedy build is a solid match.

11. Dorohedoro

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Prime Video

Synopsis: In a grimy, rain-soaked dystopia called the Hole, a lizard-headed amnesiac named Caiman hunts the Sorcerers who warped his body and erased his memories, with his fierce friend Nikaido at his side. The Sorcerers, led by the oddly domestic crime boss En, are just as strange and violent as the world they move through. Blood, body horror, and deadpan comedy coexist in every scene. It is unlike almost anything else out there.

Dorohedoro is a dark-horse match for the sheer unhinged weirdness of curses, transmutations, and bizarre creature designs. It balances extreme violence with endearing domestic comedy and oddball friendships, the same tonal tightrope Dandadan walks between gross-out mayhem and genuine warmth. Caiman and Nikaido's banter-filled partnership even echoes Momo and Okarun's ride-or-die energy. It is grimier and more surreal than Dandadan, and the romance is swapped for friendship, so it sits at the bottom, but the gleeful, violent strangeness is a strong pull. One note: it streams on Crunchyroll in some regions and Netflix in others, so check what you have.

A couple that get recommended but watch differently

Search around for anime similar to Dandadan and a few hits come up that share a piece of the vibe but not the chaotic occult-meets-sci-fi blend. They are worth your time. We just want the match to feel honest.

Spy x Family nails the action-comedy with a lovable central unit and real heart, but it is a grounded spy sitcom with no ghosts, aliens, or paranormal chaos, so the overlap is the tone rather than the premise. Kaiju No. 8 delivers monster-fighting spectacle and a charming underdog, yet it plays its premise earnest and straightforward rather than weird and unhinged. Both are excellent. Just go in knowing they scratch the comedy or the monster action, not Dandadan's specific blend of the occult and the absurd.

FAQs

Is there an anime exactly like Dandadan?

Not a perfect twin, because its occult-meets-aliens blend is genuinely rare. The closest are Mob Psycho 100, which matches the stylish supernatural action-comedy and the big emotional heart, and Chainsaw Man, which comes from the same creative family and shares the crude, chaotic, secretly tender energy. Each nails a core piece of Dandadan.

What is the best anime like Dandadan?

For the closest mix of chaos, comedy, and heart, Mob Psycho 100 and Chainsaw Man lead the pack. If you want the surreal alien coming-of-age side specifically, FLCL is the one to beat, with Undead Unluck close behind for the high-concept duo dynamic.

What anime has ghosts and aliens like Dandadan?

Dandadan's exact mix of yokai and aliens is hard to find in one show, so the closest route is to cover both sides: FLCL for the alien weirdness, and Noragami and Bleach for the spirits and Hollows invading everyday life.

Is there an anime with a couple like Momo and Okarun?

Yes. Undead Unluck pairs the unlucky Fuuko with the immortal Andy, and Noragami builds a slow-burn bond between Yato and Hiyori, both central duos whose chemistry grows amid constant supernatural chaos, much like Momo and Okarun.

Where can you watch anime like Dandadan?

Most of these stream on Crunchyroll, including Dandadan itself, while Undead Unluck sits on Prime Video, and Dorohedoro is on Crunchyroll in some regions and Prime Video in others. Streaming rights shift by region and over time, so double-check what is available where you are before you settle in.
