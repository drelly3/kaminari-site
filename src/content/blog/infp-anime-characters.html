INFPs, the Mediators of the MBTI world, are the quiet idealists: introverted, imaginative, and guided by a private set of values they will not compromise on. Anime is full of them, because the gentle dreamer with a steel core underneath makes for exactly the kind of character you fall for. We gathered twenty of the most INFP anime characters and, just as importantly, the reason each one fits.

The type runs on Introverted Feeling, which is a quiet inner compass that measures everything against personal values rather than outside rules. Add a vivid imagination and a deep well of empathy, and you get characters who feel things intensely, dream big, and refuse to betray who they are even when it would be so much easier to.

You will find gentle healers, tortured artists, and soft-spoken idealists here, spanning long-running shonen and Ghibli classics alike. We stuck to characters the community broadly agrees are Mediators, and parked the hotly debated ones in the honorable mentions where they belong. Let's get into it.

The 20 most INFP anime characters

Each pick below comes with the trait that seals it, whether that is a stubborn moral core, a runaway imagination, or a heart worn permanently on the sleeve. These are the anime characters who are INFP through and through.

1. Alphonse Elric

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist

Al is the Mediator heart of Fullmetal Alchemist. Trapped in a suit of armor, he never loses his gentleness, his empathy, or his unshakable belief that people are worth saving. His whole journey runs on a private moral code he will not bend, even when the world hands him every reason to. Pure Introverted Feeling.

2. Shigeo “Mob” Kageyama

Anime: Mob Psycho 100

Mob is what an INFP looks like with god-tier powers and zero ego about it. He keeps his enormous feelings locked inside until they overflow, values kindness over winning, and just wants to be a good person on his own quiet terms. The most gentle overpowered protagonist in anime, and it is not close.

3. Legoshi

Anime: Beastars

Legoshi is a textbook Mediator in the body of a large, frightening wolf. He is soft-spoken, endlessly conflicted about his own instincts, and forever weighing what is right against what is easy. His entire story is an INFP wrestling with identity and morality. Beastars practically runs on his inner monologue.

4. Shizuku Tsukishima

Anime: Whisper of the Heart

Shizuku is the INFP artist in her purest form. A bookish daydreamer who lives inside her stories, she sets out to write a whole novel simply because she has to know whether she can. Her arc is about chasing a private creative dream and measuring herself against her own ideals rather than anyone else's expectations. Textbook Mediator.

5. Orihime Inoue

Anime: Bleach

Orihime feels everything at full volume and tucks most of it behind a bright smile. Imaginative to the point of daydreaming mid-conversation, fiercely loyal, and motivated purely by protecting the people she loves, she is one of anime's warmest INFPs. Her strength was always emotional first.

6. Atsushi Nakajima

Anime: Bungo Stray Dogs

Atsushi is an INFP still learning to like himself. Gentle, self-doubting, and haunted by his past, he keeps choosing compassion anyway, extending kindness to people who have not earned it because his values quietly demand it. Watching him slowly find his own worth is the emotional spine of the show.

7. Natsume Takashi

Anime: Natsume's Book of Friends

Natsume is a Mediator to his core, a gentle boy who keeps choosing kindness even after a lonely childhood spent being feared for the spirits only he could see. Rather than wield the book of yokai names he inherits, he spends the series quietly giving each one back, guided entirely by his own soft sense of what is right. Sensitive, introspective, and slow to trust, he reaches for connection anyway, one careful bond at a time. His whole story is empathy quietly outlasting old wounds.

8. Muichiro Tokito

Anime: Demon Slayer

Muichiro drifts through half his scenes lost in his own head, which is about the most INFP thing a character can do. Behind the daydreaming is a gentle soul who reconnects with his buried feelings and fights for the people he quietly loves. Dreamy on the surface, deeply devoted underneath.

9. Crona

Anime: Soul Eater

Crona is anxiety, sensitivity, and a desperate need for genuine connection wrapped into one fragile character. Timid and easily overwhelmed, they still hold onto a tender inner world that the story slowly coaxes into the light. An INFP shaped by pain, but an INFP right to the core.

10. Tamaki Amajiki

Anime: My Hero Academia

Tamaki would rather melt into a wall than talk to anyone, and every INFP knows the feeling. Wildly anxious in public, he is also a loyal, big-hearted hero who believes in his friends far more than he believes in himself. The gap between his crippling nerves and his real strength is peak Mediator.

11. Izumi Miyamura

Anime: Horimiya

Miyamura hides a soft, artistic soul under a shy, closed-off exterior, which is a very INFP way to survive high school. Once he lets people in, he turns out to be gentle, thoughtful, and quietly devoted. His entire arc is about a rich inner world finally being seen and accepted.

12. Yuri Katsuki

Anime: Yuri on Ice

Yuri feels his sport with an intensity that borders on painful. Anxious, self-critical, and driven by love for the craft rather than trophies, he skates from a place of pure personal meaning. That values-first passion, wrapped in a bundle of nerves, is textbook Mediator energy.

13. Rikka Takanashi

Anime: Love, Chunibyo & Other Delusions

Rikka lives half inside a fantasy world of her own making, which is the INFP imagination taken to its most literal extreme. Her delusions turn out to be a shield for real grief, and underneath the theatrics is a sensitive kid processing loss the only way she knows how. Dreamer and mourner in one.

14. Yamaguchi Tadashi

Anime: Haikyuu!!

Yamaguchi spends most of Haikyuu quietly doubting himself in Tsukishima's shadow, which any INFP will recognize instantly. Sensitive, loyal, and desperate to contribute without making a fuss, he finds his courage through sheer personal conviction rather than swagger. His trembling, hard-won growth is some of the most relatable material in the show.

15. Meiko “Menma” Honma

Anime: Anohana

Menma is gentleness distilled. Even lingering as a wistful presence, she is defined by pure, uncomplicated love for her friends and a quiet wish to see them happy. Her whole arc runs on emotional idealism, the belief that the people she loves deserve peace. Heartbreak in Mediator form.

16. Nunnally vi Britannia

Anime: Code Geass

Surrounded by a family of schemers and warlords, Nunnally stays impossibly soft. She dreams of a gentle world and holds onto kindness as an actual political ideal, which in Code Geass makes her practically a unicorn. Her sweetness is not naivety, it is a value she chooses on purpose. Deeply INFP.

17. Chihiro Ogino

Anime: Spirited Away

Chihiro starts as a scared, sulky kid and grows into someone brave through pure heart. She wins over an entire spirit world not with power but with kindness, sincerity, and stubborn loyalty to the people she cares about. Her quiet moral compass carries the whole film. A young INFP finding her strength.

18. Rakka

Anime: Haibane Renmei

Rakka is a gentle, searching soul trying to make sense of her own existence, which is about as INFP as a premise gets. Deeply empathetic and quietly troubled, she keeps reaching out to others while wrestling with her own need for meaning. Haibane Renmei is essentially a meditation on a Mediator finding peace.

19. Chiyuki

Anime: Death Parade

Chiyuki carries an enormous amount of buried feeling behind a composed surface. Introspective and emotionally raw once her walls finally come down, she pushes the whole show to reckon with empathy and the weight of a single human life. Her quiet, values-driven arc is Mediator storytelling at its most affecting.

20. Toboe

Anime: Wolf's Rain

Toboe is the softest heart in a harsh, wandering world. The youngest of the pack, he leads with affection and loyalty, forming bonds the others are too guarded to risk. Gentle to a fault and guided almost entirely by feeling, he is an INFP trying to hold onto tenderness in a story that keeps testing it.

Honorable mentions

A few characters who turn up on plenty of INFP lists, but whose typing the community genuinely argues over: Shinji Ikari and Gaara (both pulled toward ISFP by some), Kaneki Ken (a very popular INFJ pick), and Nausicaä (frequently typed ENFJ for how decisively she leads). All Mediator-adjacent, all worth the debate.

Why we love a good Mediator

For all their shyness, INFP anime characters tend to be the ones who stay with you longest, because their strength is the kind you have to look closely to notice. Al's compassion, Mob's restraint, Legoshi's quiet conscience, it all comes from the same place: a heart that simply refuses to compromise. That quiet integrity is exactly why the Mediators so often end up being everyone's favorite.

FAQs

What is an INFP personality type?

INFP stands for Introverted, Intuitive, Feeling, and Prospecting, nicknamed the Mediator. These are quiet idealists who lead with a strong inner set of values, a rich imagination, and deep empathy, caring more about authenticity and meaning than rules or status. Plenty of INFP personality type anime characters put that on full display.

What anime characters are INFP?

Some of the clearest, least-debated examples are Alphonse Elric, Mob from Mob Psycho 100, Legoshi, Atsushi Nakajima, and Shizuku Tsukishima. Each one leads with empathy, imagination, and a personal moral code, which are the core traits of the Mediator type.

Who is the most INFP anime character?

Alphonse Elric and Mob are the two most cited, and it is easy to see why. Both are gentle, deeply principled, and quietly powerful, choosing kindness at every turn even when it costs them. Either one lands as a near-perfect Mediator.

Are there famous female INFP anime characters?

Absolutely. Orihime Inoue, Mitsuri Kanroji, Shizuku Tsukishima, Nunnally, and Rikka Takanashi are all strong female INFPs, each guided by empathy and a private set of ideals rather than outside expectations.

How can you tell if a character is INFP?

Look for a dominant sense of personal values, an idealistic streak, a vivid imagination, and a tendency to feel deeply but privately. The anime characters who are INFP usually hate conflict, dream big, and stay loyal to what they believe no matter the pressure.
