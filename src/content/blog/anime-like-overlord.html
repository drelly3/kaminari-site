Can't get enough of Ainz Ooal Gown ruling from his throne in Nazarick? Plenty of anime like Overlord deliver the same thrill: an overpowered, often villainous protagonist who dominates a fantasy world from a position of total strength, backed by a cast of devoted followers who would do anything for him. Few shows nail every piece of that formula, but the closest ones get surprisingly near.

The timing is good, too. Overlord: The Sacred Kingdom hit theaters in 2024 and is now streaming, continuing Ainz's conquest, and while Season 5 is widely believed to be in the works, it still has no confirmed release. So a solid watchlist is the best way to scratch the overlord itch in the meantime.

We ranked these from closest match to loosest, judged on one thing: how much each shares Overlord's actual build, an overpowered, often morally grey protagonist who dominates a dark fantasy world, commands devoted followers, and schemes or conquers, usually with game-world logic underneath. There is a quick table up top, plus honest notes at the end.

The anime most like Overlord, ranked

Here are the anime like Overlord that genuinely share its build, ranked from the closest match to the loosest. The Similarity Rating is our read on how near each one gets to Overlord specifically, not how good the show is overall, and we kept the stars to whole numbers. The build we are measuring against is simple: an overpowered, often villainous protagonist who dominates a fantasy world and commands devoted followers.

1. The Eminence in Shadow

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Hulu, HIDIVE

Synopsis: Cid Kagenou reincarnates into a fantasy world chasing one fantasy of his own: to be the mastermind who secretly controls everything from the shadows. He invents a fake evil organization to roleplay against and builds a secret order called Shadow Garden, only for the world-ending conspiracy he made up to turn out completely real. He is also, without quite realizing it, the strongest being alive. His devoted followers treat his improvised nonsense as the words of a genius.

This is the undisputed number-one match for the “Sasuga, Ainz-sama!” engine. An overpowered protagonist acts like an ominous dark mastermind who stumbles into actual conspiracies, surrounded by fanatically loyal, god-tier subordinates in Shadow Garden who interpret his every casual offhand remark as a ten-thousand-year divine master plan. Cid and Ainz are two sides of the same coin, the secretly all-powerful leader bluffing through a performance his underlings take as gospel. Swap Nazarick for Shadow Garden and the dynamic is almost identical.

2. The Saga of Tanya the Evil

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A ruthless salaryman is killed and reincarnated, by a god he refuses to acknowledge, as a small girl named Tanya in a war-torn, early-twentieth-century-style world. Coldly determined to secure a safe, comfortable life, she climbs the military ranks and wages brutal magical warfare without a shred of sentiment. She is brilliant, merciless, and utterly pragmatic. The result is a sharp, dark military fantasy.

Tanya is the closest match for Overlord's ruthless, cynical salaryman psyche. An amoral corporate bureaucrat reincarnates into a world at war and uses cold optimization, modern military doctrine, and overpowering magic to butcher her enemies, while military command continuously misinterprets her ruthless efficiency and promotes her into greater geopolitical chaos. That gap between her self-serving intent and how her superiors read it is the same loyal-misreading engine that drives Ainz's story. It swaps the fantasy kingdom for a military campaign, so there is no Nazarick to command, but the merciless-overpowered-lead energy is a direct hit.

3. That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A middle-aged man is stabbed and reincarnated as a slime in a fantasy world, with a skill set that lets him absorb abilities and a calm voice guiding his growth. As Rimuru Tempest, he evolves from the weakest monster imaginable into one of the most powerful beings alive, gathering devoted followers at every step. Rather than conquer for its own sake, he sets out to build a nation where monsters can live safely. It is cozy, funny, and quietly epic.

Slime is the direct sibling for monster nation-building and subordinate hierarchy. A former salaryman reincarnates as a non-human creature, unites wild monster tribes, names them to evolve them into loyal floor-boss-tier lieutenants, and founds a sovereign state, which is Ainz's Sorcerer Kingdom with the lights on. It only ranks behind Tanya because Rimuru is ultimately benevolent and diplomatic rather than coldly self-interested, so the dark edge is softer. The overpowered-ruler-and-his-devoted-empire structure, though, is the same from the throne room down.

4. Tsukimichi: Moonlit Fantasy

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Makoto Misumi is summoned to another world as a hero, then promptly dumped at the edge of it by a goddess who finds him too plain to bother with. Cast out and shunned by humans, he falls in with the demi-humans and monsters nobody else wants, and discovers his otherworld origin makes him absurdly overpowered. He builds a hidden home and a trading company while two devoted, monstrous companions fight over him. It is a warm, funny, slightly melancholy power fantasy.

Tsukimichi is a direct counterpart to Nazarick's monstrous isolation. Shunned by the goddess and rejected by humans, Makoto is forced to align with demi-humans and monsters, and he builds a hidden pocket dimension called the Demiplane populated by loyal orcs, lizardmen, and ancient dragons. His two closest retainers, Tomoe and Mio, rival Albedo and Shalltear in sheer possessive adoration, and he runs a covert merchant alter ego on the side, much like Ainz operating under an assumed name. The tone is gentler and the hero kinder than Ainz, but the overpowered-outcast-and-his-devoted-monster-household build is a close fit.

5. Log Horizon

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Thousands of players wake up trapped inside the MMO Elder Tale, unable to log out, and must build a functioning society out of the chaos. The hero is Shiroe, a brilliant strategist who wins through planning, politics, and a deep command of the game's systems rather than brute force. He works to establish order and institutions among the stranded players. It is the thinking person's trapped-in-a-game story.

Log Horizon is the masterclass in MMO mechanics turned reality and guild logistics. It lacks the dark villainy, but Shiroe, nicknamed the Villain in Glasses, uses complex MMO raid mechanics, cooldown timers, spell combinations, and economic subversion to establish order and govern an entire player society, which captures Ainz's deep technical knowledge of the game underpinning his world. Watching Shiroe scheme three moves ahead and found a functioning city-state rhymes directly with Overlord's strategic side. It is heroic and talky rather than villainous, so it ranks mid-list, but the game-world statecraft is a strong match.

6. I'm the Evil Lord of an Intergalactic Empire!

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Liam Sera Banfield is reincarnated into a far-future interstellar empire and decides, after a hard first life, to become a gloriously evil overlord who takes whatever he wants. The catch is that his every ruthless, self-serving act somehow lands as noble, and his subjects adore him as a benevolent hero. The more he tries to be cruel, the more beloved he becomes. It is a sharp, funny space-opera spin on the villain fantasy.

This one directly shares Overlord's comedy of administrative misunderstandings. A reincarnated noble resolves to become a tyrannical, corrupt despot who exploits his subjects, but his self-serving schemes accidentally result in beloved economic reforms, military triumphs, and subordinates worshiping him as an infallible, saintly ruler. The gap between how Liam sees himself and how his adoring followers interpret him is the exact comedic engine that powers Ainz's story, where fearsome intent and loyal misreading keep colliding. It is one of the closest picks here on the aspiring-overlord concept, just routed through a sci-fi empire.

7. Re:Monster

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Tomokui Kanata dies and is reincarnated as Goburou, the lowliest goblin imaginable, keeping his human memories and a ruthless will to survive. By devouring the beasts and enemies he kills, he absorbs their powers and evolves, climbing from the bottom of the food chain through sheer predation. He pulls his goblin kin up with him, forging them into a disciplined, terrifying fighting force. It is a brutal, amoral survival climb.

Re:Monster is a brutal, amoral monster-evolution story that shares Overlord's non-human, no-conventional-morality streak. The protagonist reincarnates as a lowly goblin with a ruthless survivalist mindset, devouring beasts and enemies to acquire their abilities and evolving himself and his goblin clan into an organized, terrifying monstrous army that butchers foes without hesitation. The monster-side perspective and the cold, pragmatic empire-building echo Ainz marshaling Nazarick's horrors toward his own ends. It is rougher and far smaller in scope, with none of Overlord's polish, but the amoral monster-army climb is a solid match.

8. The Misfit of Demon King Academy

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: The tyrannical Demon King Anos Voldigoad reincarnates two thousand years after his death, only to find his descendants have grown weak and his legend twisted beyond recognition. Enrolling at a magic academy that ranks students by bloodline, he effortlessly outclasses everyone while setting the record straight about who he really is. No threat comes close to matching him. The series leans into the sheer spectacle of an unbeatable protagonist.

The Misfit of Demon King Academy is an unapologetic power fantasy where Anos Voldigoad flexes effortless, god-tier dominance over arrogant magic hierarchies and preening nobles. It captures Ainz's absolute battlefield invincibility and his calm, unbothered intimidation, the satisfaction of a protagonist no one in the room can touch. The difference is temperament: Anos has genuine confidence and a traditional heroic moral core, where Ainz hides panic behind the performance and leans villainous. It is flashier and more heroic in tone, which keeps it mid-list, but the all-powerful-sovereign energy is pure Overlord.

9. Death Note

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Light Yagami, a brilliant and bored high schooler, finds a notebook that kills anyone whose name is written in it while picturing their face. Styling himself as a god of the new world, he sets out to erase criminals and rule a remade society from the shadows, drawing the attention of an enigmatic detective known only as L. What follows is a razor-sharp battle of wits with the whole world as the board. It is one of anime's definitive psychological thrillers.

If your favorite part of Overlord is the cold, masked anti-hero playing four-dimensional chess against entire nations with zero moral hesitation, Death Note delivers that purely. Light Yagami operates with the same calculated, amoral superiority as Ainz Ooal Gown outmaneuvering political rivals, always several moves ahead and utterly convinced of his right to rule. There is no fantasy world, no undead legion, and no loyal cabinet, so it sits lower on the build, but the scheming-genius-against-the-world core is a direct line. Our Death Note filler list lays out why there is nothing to skip across its run.

10. How Not to Summon a Demon Lord

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Takuma is a reclusive gamer renowned in his favorite MMO as the fearsome Demon Lord Diablo, when he is suddenly summoned into that game world by two girls hoping to enslave him, only for his own magic to bounce the spell back onto them. Cripplingly shy in real life, he copes by acting out his in-game Demon Lord persona, barking commands he barely means. Underneath the bravado he is overpowered beyond measure. It is an ecchi comedy with real game-mechanic flavor.

How Not to Summon a Demon Lord is a direct match for the social anxiety behind the terrifying persona. An introverted gamer is summoned into his MMORPG world with his max-level avatar and endgame gear, and he is forced to constantly roleplay as an arrogant, menacing demon lord to hide his intense panic and total lack of real-world confidence. That is Ainz's exact routine, the private scrambling under the fearsome mask, down to the game-system logic running the world and settling every fight. It leans harder into fanservice than Overlord, so go in expecting the ecchi, but the premise is a close cousin.

11. Demon Lord, Retry!

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Game developer Akira Oono is pulled into his own RPG as its final boss, the Demon Lord Kunai Hakuto, on the night he shuts the game down. Towering and intimidating, he travels the world with a young girl he takes under his wing, unintentionally building a fearsome reputation as he goes. His game-boss power makes most fights a formality. It is a rough-around-the-edges but earnest power fantasy.

Demon Lord, Retry! is squarely Overlord-shaped. An adult game developer is transported into his game as his endgame character, the Demon Lord Kunai Hakuto, and he explores the fantasy world, manages loyal subordinates, and relies on administrator-style game mechanics to effortlessly overpower native fantasy mages. Kunai's final-boss strength and the way the world builds him up into a looming legend echo Ainz's accidental infamy almost beat for beat. It is lower-budget and less sharp than the top picks, but the reluctant-demon-lord-in-a-game-world build is a clear fit.

A few overpowered isekai that skip the overlord part

Search around for anime similar to Overlord and plenty of overpowered-protagonist isekai come up that share the raw strength but not the villain-ruler-with-a-devoted-empire core. They are worth your time. We just want the match to feel honest.

Cautious Hero and Wise Man's Grandchild both give you a wildly overpowered lead who steamrolls every threat, but they are heroic and good-natured, with no dark ambition or kingdom to command. The Rising of the Shield Hero hands its hero a rough, bitter start and real power, yet it runs on vindication and revenge rather than domination. Each is a great watch. Just go in knowing they deliver the overpowered part without the scheming overlord and his loyal legion.

FAQs

Is there an anime exactly like Overlord?

Not a perfect twin, but a couple come very close. The Eminence in Shadow matches the overpowered lead surrounded by fanatically loyal followers who misread his every move, and The Saga of Tanya the Evil matches the cold, calculating conqueror who treats domination as a problem to solve. Each nails a core piece of Ainz's story.

What anime similar to Overlord has an overpowered villain?

For the darker, more ruthless lead, The Saga of Tanya the Evil, Re:Monster, and I'm the Evil Lord of an Intergalactic Empire! are the picks. Each centers a cold or amoral protagonist who dominates on their own terms, closer to Ainz than the usual heroic isekai.

Is Overlord getting a Season 5?

As of now, no release date has been confirmed. The Sacred Kingdom film continued the story in 2024 and is streaming, and production on a fifth season is widely believed to be greenlit, with the earliest realistic window falling around 2027.

What is the best anime like Overlord?

For the full overpowered-ruler-and-his-devoted-empire build, The Eminence in Shadow and The Saga of Tanya the Evil lead the pack. If you want the devoted monster nation specifically, That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime is the one to beat.

Where can you watch anime like Overlord?

Most of these stream on Crunchyroll, including Overlord itself and the Sacred Kingdom film, with a couple also on HIDIVE. Streaming rights shift by region and over time, so double-check what is available where you are before you settle in.
