Where Memory Snow and The Frozen Bond actually fit

The two OVA movies are the piece that throws people, so here's the honest placement for each.

Memory Snow (watch after Season 1). A light, mostly self-contained side story set shortly after the events of the first season, full of small character moments and comedy that only land once you know everyone. Some viewers like to slot it between Season 1 episodes 11 and 12 for a breather in the middle of the darkest stretch, and that works too, but dropping it in right after the Season 1 finale is the cleaner call. Either way, it means more once you've spent real time with the cast.

The Frozen Bond (watch after Memory Snow, before Season 2). This one focuses on Emilia's past with Puck, and here's the catch: chronologically it takes place before Season 1. Watching it first sounds logical, but it quietly spoils reveals about Emilia that the main series wants you to discover slowly. Save it until after Season 1 (right alongside Memory Snow works well), and it pays off as backstory instead of stepping on the mystery.

How to watch Re:Zero in chronological order (and why it's a trap for newcomers)

If you want the internal-timeline version, here's how to watch Re:Zero in chronological order: The Frozen Bond first (it's a prequel), then Season 1 episodes 1 to 11, then Memory Snow, then Season 1 episodes 12 to 25, then Season 2, Season 3, and Season 4.

Now the honest part. For a first-time viewer, that order works against you. The Frozen Bond gives away who Emilia is and what her deal is long before the main story wants you to know, and a big part of Season 1's pull is uncovering that slowly alongside Subaru. Chronological order is a genuinely fun rewatch once you already know every twist, and it's worth doing then. On a first pass, release order wins, and it isn't close.

Original Season 1 or the Director's Cut?

Before Season 2 aired, White Fox released a re-edit of Season 1 (officially the New Edition, widely called the Director's Cut) that condenses the 25 original episodes into a smaller run of longer ones, with some scenes tweaked or added. It's an alternate version of the same season, not a new chapter you need to watch on top of the original.

For a first watch, stick with the original 25-episode Season 1. The extra room lets the pacing breathe, and the weekly-episode structure suits how the story doles out its gut-punches. The Director's Cut is best treated as a faster re-entry point for returning fans who want to refresh before Season 2 without redoing the full run. New here? Original season. Coming back after a long gap? The re-edit is a reasonable shortcut.

How many seasons of Re:Zero are there?

As of 2026, Re:Zero has three completed TV seasons and a fourth in the middle of its broadcast, plus the two OVA films. Here's the arc breakdown at a glance:

  • Season 1 (2016, 25 episodes): Arcs 1 to 3. Subaru arrives in a new world and discovers Return by Death, his ability to reset to a checkpoint each time he dies.
  • Season 2 (2020-2021, 25 episodes): Arc 4. Aired split-cour across two halves and digs deep into the Sanctuary and Subaru's hardest emotional trials yet.
  • Season 3 (2024-2025, 16 episodes): Arc 5. The Watergate City stretch, split into an Attack Arc and a Counterattack Arc, and a big jump in scale.
  • Season 4 (2026, airing now): Arc 6. Broadcasting in two cours, with the back half having just started, so this is the current frontier of the story.

The main story is very much still going, which is a big part of why so many people are searching the Re:Zero watch order right now. Anyone catching up has a clear runway from Season 1 straight into what's airing today.

Is there any filler in Re:Zero? (and is Isekai Quartet part of it?)

No, Re:Zero has no traditional filler in the TV seasons. It's a faithful adaptation of the light novels, so every main-series episode moves the story. The extras are the only optional pieces, and even those are canon side stories rather than time-wasters.

A couple of common mix-ups worth clearing up. Isekai Quartet is a separate chibi crossover comedy that puts the Re:Zero cast alongside characters from other isekai shows, and it's a fun spin-off, not part of the main canon or the watch order. The little Break Time chibi shorts fall in the same bucket: pure comedy bonuses you can enjoy whenever, with nothing you'll miss by skipping.

FAQ

What is the correct order to watch Re:Zero?

For a first watch, go in release order: Season 1, then the Memory Snow and The Frozen Bond OVA films, then Season 2, Season 3, and Season 4. It keeps the reveals intact and slots the side stories where they read best.

Should I watch The Frozen Bond first since it's a prequel?

No. Even though it takes place before Season 1, watching it first spoils key reveals about Emilia. Watch it after Season 1 (alongside Memory Snow works well) so it lands as backstory rather than getting ahead of the mystery.

Do I need to watch the Director's Cut of Season 1?

Not for a first watch. The Director's Cut is a condensed re-edit of Season 1, best used as a quick refresher for returning fans before Season 2. First-timers should watch the original 25-episode season.

How many seasons of Re:Zero are there?

Three seasons are complete (Season 1, Season 2, and Season 3) and a fourth is currently airing, alongside the two OVA films. Season 1 has 25 episodes, Season 2 has 25, and Season 3 has 16.
