Monogatari has one of the most notorious watch orders in anime, and for good reason: the series tells its story out of chronological sequence on purpose. The good news is that the Monogatari watch order most fans recommend is simple. For a first watch, go in release order, starting with Bakemonogatari and following each part as it aired. The non-linear structure is intentional, so watching in broadcast order is how the reveals were built to land.

The one thing that trips people up is Kizumonogatari, the vampire prequel. Chronologically it comes first, but it was released years into the run, and keeping it in that later slot preserves a mystery the whole series is built around. That is why release order beats jumping into the timeline cold. Chronological order exists too, but it is a reward for rewatchers, not a first-time route.

Release order or chronological order?

This is the debate that defines the Monogatari watch order. Release order, the one above, is what we recommend for a first watch. The series constantly references events you have not seen yet and hides reveals inside its timeline jumps, and broadcast order is how those payoffs were designed to work. It also keeps the wordplay and running jokes landing in sequence.

Chronological order rearranges everything into in-universe time, and its headline change is moving Kizumonogatari to the very front, since it is the origin story. Nekomonogatari: Black slides ahead of Bakemonogatari, and Hanamonogatari drops to near the end, where it actually takes place. The rough chronological run is Kizumonogatari, then Nekomonogatari: Black, Bakemonogatari, Nisemonogatari, the Second Season arcs, Tsukimonogatari, Koyomimonogatari, both parts of Owarimonogatari, Hanamonogatari, Zoku Owarimonogatari, and finally Off & Monster Season. Save that route for a second pass, when watching the foreshadowing click into place is the whole reward.

Where to watch Monogatari

Streaming is simpler than the watch order, mostly. Crunchyroll carries the bulk of the series worldwide, from Bakemonogatari through Off & Monster Season, in subtitled form. One quirk to know: the final three episodes of Bakemonogatari originally streamed on the web and live on Amazon Prime in some regions, so you may need a second service for those. Availability shifts by region, so check what is live where you are before you dive in.
