The anime most like One Punch Man, ranked

Here are the anime like One Punch Man that genuinely share its build, ranked from the closest match to the loosest. The Similarity Rating is our read on how near each one gets to One Punch Man specifically, not how good the show is overall, and we kept the stars to whole numbers. The build we are measuring against is simple: an action-comedy that satirizes the hero or power-fantasy genre, led by an overpowered or unbothered protagonist, with deadpan humor and spectacle.

1. Mob Psycho 100

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Shigeo “Mob” Kageyama is a quiet middle schooler who happens to be one of the most powerful psychics alive, working for a con-artist exorcist while trying to live an ordinary life and win over his crush. He would rather suppress his overwhelming powers than use them, until his emotions boil over into breathtaking displays of force. The animation turns those outbursts into some of the most inventive action in anime. Under the spectacle is a sincere story about growing up.

From the same creator as One Punch Man, this is the closest match there is. It shares the overpowered-but-unbothered lead, the sharp comedy, the deconstruction of what power actually means, and the jaw-dropping action that erupts out of a deadpan everyday world. Mob and Saitama are two sides of the same idea, protagonists so strong that the real story is their search for meaning beyond the fighting. If you loved One Punch Man's heart hiding under the jokes, start here.

2. The Disastrous Life of Saiki K.

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Prime Video

Synopsis: Kusuo Saiki is born with every psychic power imaginable, which sounds amazing until you realize it has made his life unbearably dull and complicated. All he wants is a quiet, normal existence, but his absurd classmates and his own godlike abilities constantly get in the way. Told in rapid-fire deadpan narration, each short sketch piles on the comedy. It is one of anime's funniest shows.

The premise is One Punch Man's comedic core distilled: an overpowered protagonist so far beyond everyone else that his limitless power has become a source of boredom and exasperation rather than triumph. Saiki's weary deadpan is Saitama's bored shrug turned into a whole series, and the humor skewers the same power-fantasy tropes. It is pure comedy with none of the big action set-pieces, which is the one real difference, but the overpowered-and-over-it DNA is a perfect match.

3. Gintama

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: In an alternate Edo where aliens have conquered Japan, a lazy, sweet-toothed samurai named Gintoki takes odd jobs with his ragtag crew to make rent. The series bounces between absurd parody, fourth-wall-breaking gags, and sudden, genuinely thrilling action arcs. It will spend ten episodes on a toilet joke and then break your heart. It is a comedy institution.

The connection is the blend of relentless parody comedy and spectacular action, delivered with a total willingness to mock the genre it lives in. Like One Punch Man, Gintama skewers shonen tropes with a straight face, then turns around and delivers a serious, beautifully animated fight when you least expect it. Its overpowered-slacker hero even rhymes with Saitama's unbothered vibe. It is far longer and more sprawling, but the comedy-plus-action-plus-satire build is a strong match.

4. The Eminence in Shadow

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Hulu, HIDIVE

Synopsis: Cid Kagenou reincarnates into a fantasy world obsessed with one goal: to become the mastermind who secretly controls everything from the shadows. He invents a fake evil organization to roleplay against, only for the conspiracy he made up to turn out completely real, and he happens to be the strongest being alive. His devoted followers take his improvised nonsense as genius. It is a comedy that keeps paying off its own absurd premise.

The build link is the overpowered protagonist played for laughs plus a sharp parody of the genre around him, wrapped in genuinely great action. Cid effortlessly crushing enemies who have no idea how outmatched they are is a chuunibyou riff on Saitama's one-punch gag, and the show skewers edgy power fantasies the way One Punch Man skewers hero fantasies. The spectacle is a real highlight too. It leans isekai rather than superhero, but the overpowered-comedy-plus-satire build fits well.

5. Cautious Hero

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: When a goddess summons a hero to save a doomed world, she gets Seiya, a warrior so absurdly overpowered and so neurotically over-prepared that he trains for hours before fighting slimes and triple-checks every threat. His paranoia drives the goddess up the wall even as he steamrolls every enemy. The series mines its one joke for surprising depth. It is a sharp isekai comedy.

The connection is the overpowered hero whose very strength is the comedy engine: Seiya is so far above his opponents that the show mines laughs from how little anything challenges him, which is Saitama's exact predicament. It parodies the summoned-hero isekai the way One Punch Man parodies the superhero genre, and the fights are a fun bonus. It swaps capes for a fantasy quest, but the overpowered-lead-as-a-joke build is a close fit.

6. Konosuba

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: After a pathetic death, Kazuma is reborn into a fantasy world and, as a joke, chooses a useless goddess named Aqua as his starting gift. He ends up leading a party of gloriously incompetent misfits, failing upward through quests that go sideways in the funniest ways possible. The series gleefully trashes every isekai cliche. It is a comedy classic.

The build match is the loving parody of a genre and the refusal to take any of it seriously, which is One Punch Man's comedic spirit in isekai form. Konosuba skewers the power-fantasy dream the way One Punch Man skewers the hero fantasy, finding its jokes in deflating expectations at every turn. There is no overpowered lead or big spectacle here, so it sits lower, but the genre-parody comedy is a genuine match.

7. Assassination Classroom

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A bizarre, tentacled creature destroys most of the moon and threatens Earth, then announces he will become a middle-school teacher, and the only way to stop him is for his students to assassinate him before graduation. The catch is that he is the best teacher they have ever had. The series mixes absurd comedy, action, and real heart. It is funnier and more moving than it has any right to be.

The connection is the overpowered character at the center of an action-comedy, with an unbeatable being whose power is the whole premise, much like Saitama. Koro-sensei casually dodging every attempt on his life rhymes with Saitama shrugging off every threat, and the show blends gags, fights, and genuine emotion the way One Punch Man does. It is more heartfelt and classroom-bound than superhero satire, which keeps it mid-list, but the overpowered-lead-plus-comedy build fits.

8. Kill la Kill

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Ryuko Matoi storms a tyrannical academy wielding half a giant scissor-blade, hunting her father's killer, and bonds with a living sailor uniform that grants her terrifying power. What follows is an absurd, breakneck war against the student council and the forces behind it. The animation is loud, fast, and gleefully over-the-top. It is as ridiculous as it is secretly heartfelt.

The build link is the maximalist, over-the-top action-comedy energy and the willingness to be completely absurd, which taps into One Punch Man's gonzo spectacle. The elastic, show-off animation and the gleeful excess rhyme with One Punch Man's biggest fights, and the comedy runs just as hot. There is no overpowered-and-bored lead or genre satire here, so it sits lower, but the ludicrous, funny, spectacular build is a solid match.

9. The Devil is a Part-Timer!

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: On the verge of conquering his fantasy world, the Devil King Satan is forced to flee to modern Tokyo, where he loses his powers and takes a job at a fast-food restaurant to pay rent. He throws himself into climbing the management ladder with the same ambition he once brought to world domination. His enemies, also stranded, end up just as caught in the mundane grind. It is a charming fish-out-of-water comedy.

The connection is the comedy of a hugely powerful being dropped into an ordinary life, finding more drama in paying rent than in any battle, which echoes Saitama's deadpan everyday existence between fights. The gap between epic stakes and mundane reality is the same joke One Punch Man loves. It is lighter on action and leans fully into the workplace comedy, so it sits lower, but the powerful-being-living-an-ordinary-life build is a fun match.

10. Hinamatsuri

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A yakuza enforcer's orderly life is upended when a capsule containing a deadpan, immensely powerful psychic girl named Hina crashes into his apartment, and he reluctantly takes her in. What follows is a string of absurd, warm-hearted misadventures involving her powers, a put-upon classmate, and an accidental career in bartending. The comedy is bone-dry and the heart sneaks up on you. It is an underrated gem.

The build match is the deadpan comedy built around a wildly overpowered character whose abilities mostly create chaos rather than heroics, which rhymes with One Punch Man's understated humor. Hina's flat, unbothered attitude toward her own god-tier powers is pure Saitama energy, and the show gets its laughs from how little she cares about any of it. There are no big battles or genre satire, so it sits lower, but the overpowered-and-deadpan build is a strong comedic fit.

11. Tiger & Bunny

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Prime Video

Synopsis: In a city where superheroes are corporate-sponsored celebrities who earn points for televised rescues, a veteran hero past his prime is forced to partner with a brash rookie. The mismatched duo bickers their way through crime-fighting while a deeper conspiracy unfolds. The series mixes buddy-cop comedy, superhero action, and sly commentary on fame. It is a fresh spin on the genre.

The connection is the superhero-genre satire: a show that pokes at hero culture, fame, and recognition the way One Punch Man does, where being a great hero and being a famous one are very different things. The ranked, sponsored hero system even echoes One Punch Man's Hero Association and Saitama's struggle for credit. It is more earnest action-drama than straight comedy, which keeps it lower, but the hero-satire build is a genuine match.

A couple that get recommended but watch differently

Search around for anime related to One Punch Man and a few giants come up that share the superpowers or the big fights but not the comedy-and-satire build. They are worth your time. We just want the match to feel honest.

My Hero Academia gives you a world of ranked heroes and spectacular battles, but it is an earnest underdog story played completely straight, with none of One Punch Man's deadpan parody of the genre. Dragon Ball Z delivers legendary overpowered fights and plenty of charm, yet it treats its power escalation seriously rather than mocking it. Both are excellent. Just go in knowing they bring the heroics or the spectacle without the satire that makes One Punch Man what it is.

FAQs

Is there an anime exactly like One Punch Man?

Not a perfect twin, but two come very close. Mob Psycho 100 comes from the same creator and shares the overpowered-yet-unbothered lead, the comedy, and the incredible action, while The Disastrous Life of Saiki K. nails the exact joke of a protagonist bored by his own limitless power. Each captures a core piece of what makes One Punch Man work.

What is the best anime like One Punch Man?

The best One Punch Man similar anime overall is Mob Psycho 100, from the same creator, which blends the comedy, the heart, and the spectacular action better than anything else. For the pure overpowered-and-over-it comedy, The Disastrous Life of Saiki K. is right beside it, with Gintama close behind for parody and action together.

Does any anime have an overpowered hero like Saitama?

Yes. The Disastrous Life of Saiki K., Cautious Hero, and The Eminence in Shadow all center a lead so powerful that their strength itself becomes the comedy, and Assassination Classroom builds its whole premise around an unbeatable being, much like Saitama.

Is One Punch Man getting a Season 3?

Yes. Season 3 premiered on October 12, 2025, animated by J.C.Staff, adapting the Monster Association and Garou arc. A second cour has been confirmed to continue the story in 2027.

Where can you watch anime like One Punch Man?

Every pick on this list streams on Crunchyroll, including One Punch Man itself. Streaming rights shift by region and over time, so double-check what is available where you are before you settle in.
