The two versions of Sailor Moon (the real question)

Here is the knot at the center of the whole Sailor Moon series: there are two complete anime adaptations of Naoko Takeuchi's manga, and they are separate continuities, not sequels.

The original 90s anime (1992 to 1997) is the classic that made Sailor Moon a global icon. It runs about 200 episodes across five seasons, tells the entire story with a warm, character-rich style, and carries plenty of monster-of-the-week filler along the way. It is a big, cozy commitment.

Sailor Moon Crystal (2014 onward) is the modern reboot. It sticks close to the manga, strips out the filler, and tells the same arcs in a fraction of the runtime, continuing into two film duologies that finish the story. It is faster and more faithful, though some fans feel it trades away a little of the original's charm, especially in its early episodes.

Neither is the wrong choice. They are two takes on one legend, and which suits you depends on how much time and nostalgia you are bringing.

Which Sailor Moon should you watch first?

For most newcomers, my pick is Sailor Moon Crystal. It gives you the complete, manga-accurate story (all five arcs, ending included) in around 21 hours, with no filler to wade through and modern animation throughout. If you want to know the whole tale without a marathon, start there.

That said, the 90s anime earns its devotion. Its slower pace gives the characters room to breathe, its humor and heart are hard to match, and for many fans it simply is Sailor Moon. If you love retro animation and do not mind filler and a long runtime, the original is a wonderful place to start instead. In hard numbers, Crystal runs about 21 hours all the way through the films, while the 90s anime is a far bigger commitment at roughly four times that. So the honest answer to what order to watch Sailor Moon is: choose your version first, then follow that path straight through, and the Sailor Moon watch order stays simple once you have picked a lane.

Watching both versions

Plenty of fans eventually want both, and there is no strict rule for the order. A common approach is to watch Crystal first for the streamlined, complete story, then go back to the 90s anime to enjoy its slower, richer telling. Others prefer to experience the original the way it aired and treat Crystal as a faithful refresher afterward. Either way works, since the two are separate continuities covering the same events. Just avoid bouncing between them mid-story, so you keep each version's reveals and pacing intact. Handled like that, even the full completionist Sailor Moon watch order stays manageable rather than overwhelming.

Dubs and where to watch Sailor Moon

If you watch dubbed, go with the modern Viz dub rather than the older, heavily edited 90s dubs. The Viz version is uncut, faithful to the original, and covers the whole series, including seasons and movies the earlier dubs never touched.

Streaming is a little scattered. Sailor Moon Crystal's three seasons tend to live on Crunchyroll and Hulu, while the Eternal and Cosmos films stream on Netflix worldwide, which is an easy detail to miss. The original 90s anime turns up on services like Tubi and Pluto in some regions, and on Viz's own platform in the US. Availability shifts by region and over time, so confirm what is live where you are before you plan a viewing.

FAQ

Which Sailor Moon should I watch first?

For a quick, complete, filler-free version, start with Sailor Moon Crystal, then its Eternal and Cosmos films. If you prefer the beloved classic and do not mind filler or a long runtime, start with the original 90s anime instead. Both tell the full story.

Do I need to watch the original 90s anime before Crystal?

No. Crystal is a standalone reboot that retells the same manga from the beginning, so you can start there with zero prior knowledge. The 90s anime is a separate continuity you can watch before, after, or not at all.

What order do I watch Eternal and Cosmos in?

Watch Eternal (both parts) first, then Cosmos (both parts). They continue directly from Sailor Moon Crystal Season 3 and act as its fourth and fifth chapters, with Cosmos delivering the finale.

Are the original Sailor Moon movies canon, and do I need them?

The three 90s films (Promise of the Rose, Hearts in Ice, and Black Dream Hole) are non-canon side stories. They are enjoyable but skippable, and none are required to follow the main plot.

Where can I watch Sailor Moon?

Sailor Moon Crystal is generally on Crunchyroll and Hulu, the Eternal and Cosmos films are on Netflix, and the 90s anime appears on services like Tubi, Pluto, and Viz depending on region. Availability varies by region, so check what is streaming near you before you start.
