ENFPs, the Campaigners, are anime's beating optimistic heart: the boundlessly enthusiastic dreamers who befriend everyone, believe in people harder than those people believe in themselves, and somehow talk their worst enemies into becoming friends. Warm, spontaneous, and endlessly curious, they run on connection and possibility, lighting up every room and rallying whole casts behind an impossible dream. Anime loves the type more than almost any other. We gathered twenty of the most convincing ENFP anime characters and the trait that seals each one.

The Campaigner is powered by open-ended imagination and deep personal values, always chasing the next what-if and the next person worth caring about. That is what separates a true ENFP from the very similar ESFP, the Entertainer: where the Entertainer lives fully in the sensory present, the Campaigner is forever dreaming about who everyone could become. Idealistic, a little scattered, and impossible to keep down for long.

One note before we start: these are community typings, not creator-confirmed labels, and the ENFP-versus-ESFP line gets argued endlessly, so we stuck to broadly agreed Campaigners. That means a few beloved names sit this one out, which we explain at the end. Here are the anime characters with ENFP personality wiring who prove optimism is a superpower.

The 20 most ENFP anime characters, ranked

Each pick comes with the Campaigner trait that seals it: the relentless optimism, the gift for connection, or the dream too big for one person to carry. These are the ENFP anime characters fans name first, and yes, we kept the Entertainers off the guest list.

1. Naruto Uzumaki

Anime: Naruto

If the Campaigner needs a patron saint, it is Naruto. He turns relentless optimism into an actual fighting style, wearing down enemy after enemy until they cannot help but believe in his dream of a kinder world. Loud, warm, and powered entirely by the bonds he builds, he wins less through strength than through refusing to give up on a single person. Our Naruto Quotes collection is basically the Campaigner handbook.

2. Monkey D. Luffy

Anime: One Piece

Luffy chases a single enormous dream with zero regard for the odds, and gathers one of anime's most beloved crews just by being impossible not to follow. He lives entirely by his own values, befriends former enemies on instinct, and cares nothing for rules that get between him and the people he loves. Free-spirited, fearless, and endlessly warm, he is the Campaigner as a future King of the Pirates. Our One Piece Quotes collection sails right alongside him.

3. Gon Freecss

Anime: Hunter x Hunter

Gon meets the world with wide-open curiosity and an almost unsettling capacity for empathy, even toward the monsters trying to kill him. He chases possibilities the way other kids chase bugs, forms instant deep bonds, and follows his gut straight into danger. That mix of boundless wonder and fierce feeling is the Campaigner in its purest form. Our Gon Quotes collection captures that relentless spark.

4. Anya Forger

Anime: Spy x Family

Anya turns pure enthusiasm into a personality, throwing herself into every scheme and school day with total, chaotic delight. She reads minds and mostly uses the power to help the people she loves and stir up a little mischief, driven by big feelings and even bigger imagination. Impulsive, warm, and forever narrating her own adventure, she is a pint-sized Campaigner. Few characters radiate this much joy per scene.

5. Vash the Stampede

Anime: Trigun

Vash hides a lifetime of pain behind a goofy grin and an unshakable refusal to take a single life. The gunman travels a brutal wasteland preaching love and peace, defusing violence with humor and a stubborn idealism that costs him dearly. That commitment to human potential, even when the world gives him every reason to quit, is deeply Campaigner. Silly on the surface, profound underneath, and impossible not to root for.

6. Kamina

Anime: Gurren Lagann

Kamina is hype incarnate, a swaggering dreamer who inspires an entire underground people to look up and fight for the surface. He believes in his crew so hard that they start believing in themselves, powering the whole story on charisma and sheer force of will. His faith in the impossible outlives even him. The Campaigner as a revolution in a cape, and one of anime's great inspirational forces.

7. Usagi Tsukino

Anime: Sailor Moon

Usagi is a crybaby klutz who becomes a legendary hero purely on the strength of her enormous heart. She would rather cry with an enemy than fight one, wins battles through compassion, and pulls a whole team of guardians together through pure warmth. That belief in love as an actual power source is the Campaigner distilled. Silly, emotional, and quietly one of the most influential heroines anime ever produced.

8. Tamaki Suoh

Anime: Ouran High School Host Club

Tamaki runs the Host Club on pure theatrical enthusiasm, treating every guest like the most important person alive and every day like a grand romantic production. Dramatic, generous, and secretly desperate for connection, he builds a found family out of misfits through sheer relentless warmth. He feels everything at maximum volume. A textbook Campaigner in a cravat, and the beating heart of his ridiculous little kingdom.

9. Marin Kitagawa

Anime: My Dress-Up Darling

Marin is enthusiasm with no off switch, throwing her whole self into cosplay and the people she cares about without a shred of self-consciousness. She loves what she loves loudly, draws quieter people out of their shells just by being so genuinely warm, and treats every new interest as the best thing that ever happened. Open-hearted and impossible to dislike, she is the Campaigner as pure, joyful passion.

10. Josuke Higashikata

Anime: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

Josuke pairs an easygoing warmth with a fierce protective streak, championing his sleepy hometown and everyone in it. He makes friends effortlessly, follows his own moral compass, and meets the bizarre with cheerful adaptability, right up until someone insults his hair. That blend of kindness, loyalty, and spontaneity marks him as a Campaigner among the JoJos. Our JoJo Filler List sorts the whole saga out.

11. Chika Fujiwara

Anime: Kaguya-sama: Love Is War

Chika is the chaos engine that keeps Kaguya-sama from ever taking itself too seriously, following her whims wherever they lead with total commitment. Unpredictable, playful, and running entirely on vibes, she derails everyone's careful schemes just by being delightfully herself. Her scattered spontaneity and warmth are peak Campaigner energy. Nobody on this list is more fun to watch and less possible to plan around.

12. Jiraiya

Anime: Naruto

Beneath the goofball routine, Jiraiya is a sentimental dreamer who wants nothing less than a world free of hatred. The Toad Sage inspires everyone he mentors, chases a grand vision of peace, and hides real wisdom behind a wandering, free-and-uninhibited grin. His faith in the next generation shapes the entire series. Our Jiraiya Quotes collection holds his best hard-won wisdom.

13. Kaori Miyazono

Anime: Your Lie in April

Kaori bursts into a grieving boy's grey world and drags color back into it through sheer, defiant vitality. A violinist who plays by feeling over rules, she chases beauty and connection with an urgency that pushes everyone around her to live more fully. Her free spirit and emotional honesty are the Campaigner turned bittersweet. Few characters leave a mark this deep in so little time.

14. Reki Kyan

Anime: SK8 the Infinity

Reki loves skateboarding with his whole chest, and his real gift is making everyone around him love it too. Endlessly enthusiastic, a little insecure under all the sunshine, he builds boards and friendships with the same hands-on passion. His warmth pulls a shy transfer student straight out of his shell and into the best summer of both their lives. The Campaigner as pure, board-shaping joy.

15. Nejire Hado

Anime: My Hero Academia

Nejire is a swirl of questions and curiosity, bouncing from one fascination to the next and cheerfully derailing serious conversations with whatever just caught her eye. Behind the airy chatter is a top-tier hero with real power and a genuine care for people. That restless wonder and easy warmth make her a clear Campaigner. Our My Hero Academia Quotes collection has more from the school that produced her.

16. Nagisa Hazuki

Anime: Free!

Nagisa is the glue that pulls an old swim team back together, running on infectious enthusiasm and a total refusal to let anyone drift apart. Playful, affectionate, and relentlessly upbeat, he charms his way past every obstacle and sulks adorably when charm fails. His whole arc is about keeping people connected. A pure Campaigner in swim jams, and the emotional spark plug of his group.

17. Chitanda Eru

Anime: Hyouka

Chitanda runs on an all-consuming curiosity captured in one iconic phrase: I'm curious. Once a mystery grabs her, she pursues it with wide-eyed, unstoppable enthusiasm that drags her reluctant classmate along for the ride. Gentle, earnest, and endlessly fascinated by the world, she powers a whole series on sheer inquisitive warmth. The Campaigner's love of possibility, wrapped in perfect politeness.

18. Himiko Toga

Anime: My Hero Academia

Toga is what happens when a Campaigner's craving for connection curdles into something frightening. She loves intensely, follows her feelings without any brakes, and just wants to live openly as herself in a world that called her a monster for it. That warped, tragic warmth makes her one of the series' most unsettling and sympathetic villains at once. A Campaigner the world failed, twisted into a cautionary tale.

19. Twice

Anime: My Hero Academia

Twice hides one of anime's biggest hearts behind a cracked mask and a chaotic mind. Fiercely loyal to the outcasts who took him in, he would do anything for his friends and feels every loss like a physical blow. His warmth and devotion to his found family are pure Campaigner, which is exactly what makes his story so devastating. Proof that the villains you cry for are often the ones who loved too hard.

20. Mako Mankanshoku

Anime: Kill la Kill

Mako is a one-girl hype machine, delivering unhinged motivational speeches from atop random spotlights and loving her chaotic best friend with zero conditions. Impulsive, ridiculous, and radiating pure joy, she keeps a brutal show grounded in genuine warmth and comic timing. Her loyalty runs bottomless beneath all the nonsense. The Campaigner as comic relief with a heart the size of the moon.

Honorable mentions

A few names get typed here constantly but land just over the line into ESFP, the Campaigner's present-focused cousin, the Entertainer. Natsu Dragneel, Asta, and Mina Ashido all live in the sensory now rather than chasing what-ifs, which makes them Entertainers rather than Campaigners. Son Goku sits on that same ESFP side for most typologists. On the genuine Campaigner bench, Obito Uchiha shows the type's idealism curdled by grief, and Euphemia of Code Geass rallies a whole nation on optimism alone before tragedy strikes. All of them keep the debate lively.

Why the Campaigners win us over

Line them up and the pattern is impossible to miss: ENFP anime characters do not overpower their stories, they inspire them. Naruto's belief, Luffy's freedom, Kamina's hype, it all runs on the same engine, a heart that sees the best in people and refuses to stop chasing a brighter version of the world. That is why the Campaigner so often turns out to be the character who changes everyone around them, usually without noticing they did it.

FAQs

What is an ENFP personality type?

ENFP stands for Extroverted, Intuitive, Feeling, and Perceiving, nicknamed the Campaigner. ENFPs are warm, imaginative, people-focused optimists who chase possibilities, form deep connections easily, and inspire everyone around them. In fiction the type lands on hopeful heroes, relentless dreamers, and the friends who refuse to give up on anyone.

Which anime characters are ENFP?

The clearest ENFP anime characters include Naruto Uzumaki, Monkey D. Luffy, Gon Freecss, Anya Forger, and Vash the Stampede. Each one runs on optimism, empathy, and imagination, winning people over through warmth rather than force, which is the Campaigner in a nutshell.

Who is the most ENFP anime character?

Naruto Uzumaki is the near-unanimous answer. His boundless optimism, gift for turning enemies into friends, and refusal to give up on a single person make him the character the whole type gets measured against.

Are these ENFP-T or ENFP-A anime characters?

Both show up. The more secure, self-assured ones like Luffy and Kamina lean toward the assertive ENFP-A end, while the more anxious, approval-seeking ENFP-T anime characters, Tamaki Suoh and Reki Kyan among them, carry the self-doubt that defines the turbulent Campaigner.

What is the difference between ENFP and ESFP anime characters?

Both are warm, spontaneous extroverts, but their focus differs. An ENFP is driven by imagination and possibility, dreaming about who people could become, while an ESFP lives in the vivid sensory present, chasing the thrill of right now. That is why fan-favorites like Natsu Dragneel and Goku usually land ESFP rather than ENFP.
