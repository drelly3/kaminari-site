The short anime characters worth knowing are the ones who read as clearly below average and still own every scene they stand in. We are talking roughly the sub-165 cm band, from the 5'4" crowd all the way down past 145 cm, where a character is visibly the smallest person in the fight and somehow the last one you would bet against. A lot of the most beloved short anime guys live right here, and their height is almost never a throwaway detail. It is fuel, a chip on the shoulder that the writing keeps cashing in.

There is a useful line to draw first. Small anime characters, in the way people usually search for them, tend to mean the pint-sized mascots and chibi types, a reindeer doctor barely knee-high or a gaming prodigy who has not hit a growth spurt yet. Short, by contrast, means a fully grown or teenage character who simply landed below the average, then turned that into a personality. We give the truly tiny their own tier near the bottom so they do not crowd out the short-but-deadly crowd up top.

Below is our ranked pick of the best short anime characters, ordered from smallest to tallest, each with a verified official height. After the list we round up the genuinely tiny end of the roster and answer the questions fans ask most, starting with who the shortest anime character actually is.

Top 12 short anime characters

We ranked these smallest to tallest, sticking to characters with documented official heights. Where two shared a number, the one whose height does more storytelling work edged ahead.

1. Rukia Kuchiki

Anime: Bleach

At 144 cm, the shinigami who kicks off the entire story is also one of the smallest people in it, which the series never lets you forget. Rukia carries herself like the noble Kuchiki heir she was adopted into, all sharp remarks and colder pride, right up until her ice-white Zanpakuto turns a battlefield into a frozen sculpture garden. She spends her early arcs being underestimated by everyone taller than her, then keeps proving that the smallest blade in the room can be the coldest.

2. Historia Reiss

Anime: Attack on Titan

Historia stands 145 cm and spent seasons hiding behind a sweet, harmless mask because being small and pleasant kept her invisible. Then the mask comes off. The girl everyone dismissed as a background sweetheart turns out to have the steeliest spine in the cast, refusing to be a symbol, a martyr, or anyone's convenient pawn. Her whole arc runs on the gap between how little space she takes up and how immovable she becomes once she decides what she wants.

3. Edward Elric

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

The patron saint of short kings measures a furious 149 cm, and calling him small is the fastest way to start a fight. Ed became the youngest State Alchemist in history through raw genius, yet the thing that reliably shatters his composure is a milk carton and a height comparison. His temper over those missing inches is played for laughs and also cuts deep, since his body paid a real price years ago. He grows a little by the finale, and earns every centimeter.

4. Shinobu Kocho

Anime: Demon Slayer

The Insect Hashira smiles through everything at 151 cm, which lands scarier than any glare. Being too small to take a demon's head the way her fellow Hashira do, Shinobu rebuilt her entire fighting style around lethal wisteria poison, turning a physical limit into a signature. That permanent grin hides a mountain of grief and a plan colder than anyone expects. Petite, patient, and quietly one of the most dangerous people in the corps when the smiling stops.

5. Ciel Phantomhive

Anime: Black Butler

Roughly 152 cm of aristocratic menace, Ciel is a thirteen-year-old running a noble house, a toy empire, and the Queen's dirtiest errands with a demon butler bound to his side. His height is a weapon he wields on purpose. Grown men keep dismissing the small boy in the eyepatch, which is precisely when the trap he laid three moves ago snaps shut. Cold, calculating, and carrying eyes far older than the frame they sit in.

6. Krillin

Anime: Dragon Ball

At 153 cm, Krillin is the most human man in a cast full of gods, and the bravest short guy in the medium because of it. He will never be the strongest fighter on the field, a fact the series drives home again and again, and he suits up anyway, hurling a Destructo Disc at monsters that could erase him by accident. Watching someone that small and that outmatched keep choosing the front line is the whole reason he endures.

7. Feitan Portor

Anime: Hunter x Hunter

Feitan proves that the smallest figure in the room can be the one to fear most. Barely clearing 155 cm, the Phantom Troupe's soft-spoken torture specialist talks in clipped fragments and moves like a rumor, until the moment he converts his own accumulated pain into a technique that burns like a miniature sun. Blink at the little man in the corner and you might miss the last thing several unlucky people ever saw.

8. Killua Zoldyck

Anime: Hunter x Hunter

Killua is 158 cm of assassin bloodline still figuring out how to be somebody's friend. Raised as a living weapon, he turns his size into pure advantage, a small target crackling with electricity and moving faster than the eye can lock onto. Strip away the claws and the killer training and you find the most fiercely loyal companion in the show, a kid who would rather protect one person than rule anything. Small hands, enormous heart.

9. Levi Ackerman

Anime: Attack on Titan

The internet's beloved short king clocks in at 160 cm and happens to be the deadliest soldier alive. Levi weaponizes his low center of gravity, spinning through the air on maneuver gear like a blade someone threw with a grudge, cutting down Titans in a frame most people would call slight. Blunt, borderline obsessive about cleanliness, and utterly done being asked about his height, he settles the question the only way that matters, by outfighting everyone taller.

10. Manjiro "Mikey" Sano

Anime: Tokyo Revengers

Around 162 cm, the founder of Tokyo's most feared gang looks like he should be getting carded at the convenience store, and that mismatch is the entire threat. Mikey is baby-faced, sweets-obsessed, and capable of folding a grown man in half with a single kick. The childlike exterior makes the darkness he carries underneath land twice as hard, a small figure everyone adores and nobody in their right mind crosses. Charisma and menace stacked into one tiny package.

11. Hinata Shoyo

Anime: Haikyu!!

At 162.8 cm, Hinata is the shortest guy on the court who decided the net was merely a suggestion. His entire journey is a rebuttal to the belief that volleyball belongs to the tall, powered by a vertical leap that defies the tape and a decoy role that yanks whole defenses out of position. Fearless to the point of recklessness, he became one of the sport's most disruptive players by refusing the ceiling that everyone else sketched over his head.

12. Vegeta

Anime: Dragon Ball

The Prince of all Saiyans stands 164 cm, a solid head shorter than half the people he is busy threatening, and his ego has never once conceded the point. Vegeta's pride is the tallest thing in any room he enters. Rival, husband, father, and eternal runner-up, he keeps hurling himself at a summit that keeps sliding higher, all from a frame shorter than the man he has spent a lifetime chasing. Nobody has ever converted a height complex into raw motivation this efficiently.

The genuinely tiny: small anime characters worth a mention

A few names sit below the short-but-mighty crowd, in true mascot-and-prodigy territory. These are the ones people picture when they mean tiny rather than merely below average.

Tony Tony Chopper from One Piece measures about 90 cm in his default Brain Point, a cotton-candy-loving reindeer doctor who is roughly the size of a large toddler and made of pure heart. Shiro from No Game No Life barely reaches 4'6", an eleven-year-old gaming savant who dismantles impossible problems from behind a permanent deadpan. Tatsumaki from One Punch Man floats almost everywhere she goes, which conveniently keeps anyone from clocking just how tiny she is before she flattens a city block with a flick of psychic force. Small frames, outsized presence, every one of them.

Small frames, giant footprints

Height is the cheapest kind of underdog shorthand a designer has, and these characters spend it well. It turns Edward Elric's temper into a running joke that also aches, gives Levi's dominance a satisfying David-versus-Titan angle, and lets Shinobu rebuild an entire combat style around the one thing her size denies her. The best short anime characters treat those missing inches as a starting condition to overcome, not a limit to accept, which is exactly why so many of them end up being the fiercest, most stubborn hearts in their casts.

FAQs

Who is the shortest anime character?

It depends on where you draw the line. Count every mascot, fairy, and pint-sized creature and the shortest anime character drops into single-digit centimeters, with beings like One Piece's Chopper (about 90 cm in base form) already dwarfing that tier. Among the short human characters most people actually mean, the sub-150 crowd leads, with names like Rukia Kuchiki at 144 cm and Historia Reiss at 145 cm sitting near the floor.

Who are the most popular short anime guys?

The names that top nearly every fan ranking are Levi Ackerman, Edward Elric, and Killua Zoldyck. All three make the same point in different ways, that a small frame and elite skill are a terrifying combination, and that height has nothing to do with how much of a series a character can carry.

What is the difference between short and small anime characters?

It is mostly about category. The small label usually covers the mascot and chibi tier, tiny creatures or child prodigies like Chopper or Shiro who read as genuinely little. Short refers to teens and adults who simply came in under the average, like Levi or Vegeta, and made a personality out of it.

Do short anime characters grow taller over time?

Some do. Edward Elric grows from 4'11" to around 5'6" across his series, and teenage characters often gain height after a timeskip. Just as often the height is locked in as a defining trait, since a character like Levi being small is half of what makes him iconic.

Why are so many powerful anime characters short?

Contrast is the entire trick. A small fighter demolishing something enormous is a cleaner underdog image than any speech could sell, so writers hand real power to small frames on purpose. Levi, Edward, and Tatsumaki all exist to hammer home that raw strength and vertical inches have nothing to do with each other.
