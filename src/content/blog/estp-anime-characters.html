ESTPs, the Daredevils, are anime's adrenaline junkies: the reckless brawlers, cocky delinquents, and quick-thinking thrill-seekers who leap before they look and figure out the rest on the way down. Bold, blunt, and endlessly competitive, they live for the rush of the present moment, read a fight in real time, and would always rather act than plan. When a story needs someone to punch first and strategize mid-swing, this is who shows up. We gathered fifteen of the most convincing ESTP anime characters and the trait that seals each one.

The Daredevil runs on sharp, in-the-moment sensory awareness paired with cool, on-the-fly logic, which is why ESTPs make brilliant improvisers and terrible planners. That is what separates them from the very similar ESFP: both live for the thrill of right now, but where the Entertainer is powered by warm feeling and a love of the crowd, the Daredevil is powered by tactical logic and the challenge itself. Charming, restless, and allergic to sitting still.

One note before we start: these are community typings, not creator-confirmed labels, and the ESTP-versus-ESFP line gets argued endlessly, so we stuck to broadly agreed Daredevils. If a character solved a problem by charging straight at it, they had a real shot at being one of the best ESTP characters anime has produced. Here are the ESTP anime characters who prove that sometimes the best move is to stop thinking and start swinging.

The 15 most ESTP anime characters, ranked

Each pick comes with the Daredevil trait that seals it: the love of a good fight, the split-second improvisation, or the total refusal to overthink. These are the ESTP anime characters fans name first, the ones already mid-swing before anyone finished the plan.

1. Yusuke Urameshi

Anime: Yu Yu Hakusho

Yusuke is the ESTP the whole type gets measured against. The delinquent-turned-Spirit-Detective punches first, asks questions never, and actively hunts down the strongest opponents just to test himself against them. He reads a fight in real time, adapts on pure instinct, and treats rules as gentle suggestions. Loyal to his friends and allergic to anything boring, he is the Daredevil in its rawest, most reckless form.

2. Katsuki Bakugo

Anime: My Hero Academia

Bakugo launches into action without a second thought and strategizes on the fly, cracking an opponent's weakness by attacking over and over until it gives. Explosive, blunt, and fiercely competitive, he views fighting as the answer to almost everything and has zero patience for anyone slowing him down. That reactive, Se-first intensity is why the community keeps landing him on ESTP. Our Bakugo Quotes collection has more of that fire.

3. Nobara Kugisaki

Anime: Jujutsu Kaisen

Nobara lives completely in the moment, meeting every fight with aggressive, tactical physicality and zero interest in anyone's rulebook. Confident, blunt, and unapologetically herself, she improvises in combat, trusts her own read of a situation, and refuses to shrink for a soul. That mix of present-focused aggression and cool self-assurance is pure Daredevil. Our Jujutsu Kaisen Filler List keeps your binge clean.

4. Inosuke Hashibira

Anime: Demon Slayer

Inosuke is instinct with a boar's head strapped on. Raised in the mountains, he charges headfirst into every situation, fights on pure sensory reflex, and learns entirely by doing rather than thinking. Competitive to a hilarious fault and completely allergic to sitting still, he adapts mid-battle faster than most people can form a plan. The Daredevil as a feral, lovable force of nature. Our Demon Slayer Filler List keeps the run clean.

5. Greed

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist

Greed wants everything the world has to offer, and he goes after it with charm, nerve, and a total lack of hesitation. The most human of the homunculi lives for pleasure, power, and the thrill of the moment, improvising his way through fights and alliances alike. Bold, hedonistic, and secretly more loyal than he lets on, he is the Daredevil's appetite for life turned all the way up. Ambition you cannot help but root for.

6. Denji

Anime: Chainsaw Man

Denji is grounded entirely in immediate physical needs, a good meal, a warm bed, and whatever thrill is right in front of him. He throws himself into brutal, chaotic fights on pure instinct, adapting on the fly with reckless creativity and zero long-term thought. Crude, impulsive, and disarmingly honest about what he wants, he is the Daredevil stripped down to raw sensory drive. Nobody lives more completely in the now.

7. Faye Valentine

Anime: Cowboy Bebop

Faye is a gambler, a con artist, and a bounty hunter who trusts her instincts over any plan and her own survival over anyone's rules. Quick-witted, bold, and always chasing the next score, she reacts to trouble with sharp improvisation and a cool refusal to get sentimental about it. Beneath the bravado is someone still figuring out who she is. The Daredevil as a woman who lives entirely by her own risky wits.

8. Isaac Netero

Anime: Hunter x Hunter

Netero spent a lifetime chasing one thing: the pure, electric joy of a real fight. The old chairman trained his body to superhuman limits not out of duty but out of love for the thrill, and he meets the strongest enemy in the series with a grin and total present-moment focus. Bold, playful, and terrifyingly skilled, he is proof the Daredevil never really grows out of craving the next great battle. 9. Grimmjow

Anime: Bleach

Grimmjow is a predator who lives for the fight and nothing else. The Espada throws himself at stronger opponents purely for the rush, ignores orders that get between him and a good brawl, and adapts mid-battle with feral, instinctive skill. Prideful, blunt, and gloriously reckless, he wants to win on his own terms or not at all. The Daredevil as an apex hunter with a permanent chip on his shoulder.

10. Jewelry Bonney

Anime: One Piece

Bonney does whatever she wants, whenever she wants, and dares the world to stop her. A rookie pirate captain with a huge appetite and an even bigger nerve, she improvises through danger with bold, in-the-moment confidence and a gambler's instinct for risk. Reckless, quick-tempered, and fiercely independent, she thrives on the unpredictability of the Grand Line. The Daredevil as a pirate who plays every hand by feel. 11. Chuuya Nakahara

Anime: Bungo Stray Dogs

Chuuya is a cocky, hot-blooded martial artist who solves most problems by hurling himself, and sometimes his enemies, directly at them. He excels in close-quarters combat, reacts with lightning reflexes, and executes with blunt, pragmatic force rather than elaborate schemes. Proud and quick to anger, he backs every ounce of attitude with real skill. The Daredevil as Port Mafia's most stylish brawler.

12. Kiba Inuzuka

Anime: Naruto

Kiba is all energy, instinct, and forward momentum, charging into fights alongside his dog with a grin and a battle cry. Brash, competitive, and endlessly confident, he relies on sharp senses and split-second reactions rather than careful strategy, and he never once considers backing down. Loyal and loud in equal measure, he is the Daredevil as a feral, fun-loving ninja. Our Naruto Quotes collection has more from his village.

13. Cana Alberona

Anime: Fairy Tail

Cana lives loud and reckless, a hard-drinking card mage who trusts her gut in a fight and her nerve everywhere else. Bold, blunt, and completely comfortable taking risks, she reads a situation fast and acts faster, backing her carefree attitude with real combat skill when it counts. Underneath the party-girl energy is a fierce loyalty to her guild. The Daredevil who would rather live big than live careful.

14. Mr. Satan

Anime: Dragon Ball

Mr. Satan built a whole career on bravado, showmanship, and an uncanny knack for landing on his feet. The self-proclaimed World Champion talks a huge game, improvises his way out of every disaster, and somehow charms the entire planet into loving him. Beneath the cowardice and the con is a surprisingly bold opportunist who thrives on the spotlight. The Daredevil as a lovable fraud who keeps rolling the dice.

15. Noi

Anime: Dorohedoro

Noi is a towering, cheerful powerhouse who throws herself into every fight with pure physical joy. Impossible to miss and impossible to keep down, she meets danger with a grin, reacts on instinct, and heals through nearly anything the world throws at her. Loyal, blunt, and gloriously in-the-moment, she treats combat like the best kind of fun. The Daredevil as the most upbeat bruiser in a very grimy world.

Honorable mentions

A few more Daredevils just missed the cut. Eustass Kid matches Luffy's generation blow for reckless blow in One Piece, Hidan turns immortality into pure chaotic aggression in Naruto, and Diego Brando races and fights on ruthless instinct in JoJo's Steel Ball Run. Why the Daredevils never look before they leap

Line them up and the pattern is obvious: ESTP anime characters do not wait for the perfect plan, they trust their instincts and dive straight in. Yusuke's recklessness, Bakugo's explosions, Inosuke's headfirst charge, it all runs on the same engine, a mind wired for the thrill of right now and a body already moving before the risk registers. That is why the Daredevil so often turns out to be the character who wins the fight nobody thought was winnable, purely by refusing to overthink it.

FAQs

What is an ESTP personality type?

ESTP stands for Extroverted, Sensing, Thinking, and Perceiving, nicknamed the Daredevil or Entrepreneur. ESTPs are bold, action-oriented thrill-seekers who live in the present, solve problems on the fly, and trust their instincts over any long-term plan. In fiction the type lands on reckless brawlers, cocky delinquents, and charming risk-takers.

Which anime characters are ESTP?

The clearest ESTP anime characters include Yusuke Urameshi, Katsuki Bakugo, Nobara Kugisaki, Inosuke Hashibira, and Denji. Each one charges headfirst into the action, reads a fight in real time, and would rather act than plan, which is the Daredevil in a nutshell.

Who is the most ESTP anime character?

Yusuke Urameshi from Yu Yu Hakusho is the near-unanimous answer and one of the most stereotypical ESTPs in all of anime. His reckless bravado, love of a tough fight, sharp in-the-moment instincts, and total disregard for rules make him the character the whole type gets measured against.

What is the difference between ESTP and ESFP anime characters?

Both live for the thrill of the present, but their inner compass differs. An ESTP leads with cool tactical logic, treating a fight as a problem to crack, while an ESFP leads with warm feeling and a love of the crowd, treating life as one big performance. Daredevils compete, Entertainers entertain, and if the Entertainers are more your speed, our full ESFP anime characters list has them covered.

What is the difference between ESTP and ISTP anime characters?

Both are cool, tactical, and brilliant in a fight, but energy is the giveaway. An ESTP is loud, social, and thrives on the thrill and the crowd, while an ISTP is quiet, private, and fights with detached, self-contained precision.
