The anime most like Code Geass, ranked

Here are the anime like Code Geass that genuinely share its build, ranked from the closest match to the loosest. The Similarity Rating is our read on how near each one gets to Code Geass specifically, not how good the show is overall, and we kept the stars to whole numbers. The build we are measuring against is simple: a mecha-and-military political drama with a brilliant, morally-grey mastermind who wields a special power to lead a rebellion against a tyrannical empire, with tactical battles of wits, operatic twists, and a tragic core.

1. Death Note

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Hulu

Synopsis: A brilliant, bored high schooler named Light Yagami finds a notebook that kills anyone whose name is written in it, and sets out to remake the world as a god by purging it of criminals. A genius detective known only as L takes up the chase, and the two wage a razor-sharp battle of deduction and deception. It is one of the most gripping thrillers in anime. It is a certified classic.

Of everything here, this is the undisputed intellectual counterpart. Light Yagami and Lelouch vi Britannia are the twin icons of 2000s anime anti-heroes: elite student prodigies who stumble onto a supernatural power, the Death Note and the Geass, create masked revolutionary alter egos in Kira and Zero to reshape society, live double lives right under the noses of the authorities, and wage psychological battles of wits against brilliant detectives. Swap the notebook for the Geass and the chessboard for the battlefield, and you have the same story. If you came for Lelouch's scheming, start here. Our Death Note filler list shows it is almost pure story.

2. Guilty Crown

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Prime Video

Synopsis: In a near-future Japan under the heel of an occupying force after a catastrophe, a timid boy meets an enigmatic girl and gains the power to draw weapons from people's hearts, then is swept into an underground resistance against the regime. The production is gorgeous and the drama is operatic. It swings for huge emotional highs. It is a flawed but striking spectacle.

Guilty Crown is the direct spiritual sibling, and it is no coincidence: it was written by Code Geass co-creator Ichiro Okouchi, and it follows the exact same geopolitical template. An oppressive foreign superpower occupies and strip-mines Japan after a cataclysm, an ordinary student meets an enigmatic girl who grants him a sovereign, soul-manifesting power, and he is swept into an underground resistance using humanoid mechs against high-tech military overlords. The occupied-Japan setting, the special power, and the operatic melodrama all rhyme directly. Its lead is a passive everyman rather than a mastermind, but the shared blueprint makes it the closest thing to a second Code Geass.

3. Legend of the Galactic Heroes

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Prime Video

Synopsis: Across a vast interstellar war, two brilliant commanders rise on opposite sides: an ambitious young aristocrat determined to overthrow a corrupt order and build a new empire, and a reluctant tactical genius defending a failing democracy. The scope is enormous and the politics are rich. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest space operas ever made. It is a towering achievement.

Legend of the Galactic Heroes is the pinnacle of tactical military grand strategy. If your favorite part of Code Geass was Lelouch maneuvering military divisions across a map like chess pieces, Reinhard von Lohengramm is his closest ideological peer: a charismatic, blond tactical prodigy who ruthlessly works through aristocratic corruption to overthrow an empire, clashing against an equally brilliant democratic strategist in Yang Wen-li. The operatic scale, the political intrigue, and the moral complexity on every side all match Code Geass's grey heart. There is no mecha or supernatural power, and it is far denser, but the masterminds-at-war build is a superb match.

4. Attack on Titan

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Humanity huddles behind walls against man-eating Titans, until the walls are breached and a young soldier is pulled into a war that uncovers a vast conspiracy. What begins as survival horror becomes a sprawling political tragedy about cycles of hatred. The deaths are constant and the twists are seismic. It builds to one of anime's most ambitious endings.

Attack on Titan shares Code Geass's military escalation, royal-bloodline conspiracies, and moral tragedy. Eren Yeager's evolution into an uncompromising, isolated revolutionary, alienating his closest friends, manipulating enemies and allies alike, and executing an apocalyptic gambit to unite the world's hatred upon himself, is modern anime's closest narrative heir to the Zero Requiem. The nation-against-nation intrigue, the betrayals, and the willingness to do monstrous things for a cause echo Lelouch directly. There is no mecha or power-driven rebellion in the same mold, which keeps it mid-list, but the grey-political-war-and-tragic-gambit build is a strong match. Our Attack on Titan filler list keeps your watch clean.

5. Moriarty the Patriot

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Prime Video

Synopsis: William James Moriarty, a brilliant aristocrat, leads a double life as the anonymous “Lord of Crime,” orchestrating elaborate schemes to tear down a rotten, rigidly classist British nobility and force a fairer society. His crusade eventually draws the attention of a young Sherlock Holmes. It is a stylish, cerebral reimagining of the Holmes mythos. It is a slick period thriller.

Moriarty the Patriot is a direct thematic match for Lelouch's Lord-of-the-Rebellion persona. William is an aristocratic genius who leads a double life as a masked, anonymous mastermind, orchestrating theatrical crimes and class revolts against a corrupt nobility to force social equality, all while engaging in a high-IQ chess match with a brilliant rival. The masked-genius-reshaping-society-through-elaborate-schemes core is Lelouch almost exactly, right down to the morally-grey means and the operatic flair. There is no mecha or war here, which keeps it mid-list, but the masked-mastermind-revolution build is a strong match.

6. Mobile Suit Gundam 00

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: In a near-future split between three superpower blocs, a secretive paramilitary group called Celestial Being deploys advanced mobile suits to forcibly end war wherever it erupts, making themselves the enemy of the entire world. Their interventions reshape global politics in ways they never intended. The mecha action is sleek and the geopolitics are dense. It is one of the strongest modern Gundam entries.

Produced by Sunrise alongside Code Geass, Gundam 00 shares its mecha-combat DNA and its geopolitical conflict. Celestial Being is an armed private organization using advanced mechs to execute tactical interventions against world superpowers, forcing a fractured globe to unite by making themselves the common enemy, which mirrors Zero's Black Knights and the logic of the Zero Requiem almost exactly. The mecha warfare, the political maneuvering, and the morally-grey means-to-an-end all connect. It is more of an ensemble than a single-mastermind story, which keeps it mid-list, but the mecha-and-geopolitics build is a strong match.

7. 86 (Eighty-Six)

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: In a nation that claims its war against autonomous drones is bloodless, the truth is that an oppressed underclass pilots those machines, treated as expendable and erased from the record. A sheltered commander from the ruling class begins to see the lie. It is a brutal, beautiful military tragedy about systemic injustice. It is one of the best anime of its era.

86 features the oppressive-empire dynamic and mechanized battlefield combat at Code Geass's core. The Republic of San Magnolia forces an entire subjugated ethnic minority, the “86,” into dehumanizing front-line mech warfare while the ruling elite live in comfortable denial behind city walls, which mirrors Britannia's ghetto system and the Elevens of Area 11 directly. The mecha combat is tactical and deadly, and the emotional and political weight hits as hard as Code Geass's darkest arcs. There is no single mastermind with a power leading the charge, which keeps it mid-list, but the oppression-and-mecha-war build is a superb match.

8. The Saga of Tanya the Evil

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A ruthless salaryman is reincarnated as a small girl in a war-torn, magic-powered analog of early-twentieth-century Europe, and applies cold corporate logic to becoming a terrifyingly effective military officer. Her goal is a safe, comfortable life; the cost is a trail of devastation. It is sharp, dark, and morbidly funny. It is a standout military fantasy.

The build link is the cold, Machiavellian military strategist who treats warfare as an optimization puzzle, which channels Lelouch's tactical mind and dramatic flair. Tanya's theatrical villain monologues, her battlefield deception, and her relentless exploitation of military law will instantly appeal to fans of Lelouch's outmaneuvering, and her magic-fueled combat supplies the special-power element. She fights for an empire rather than against one, and the scope is narrower, which keeps it lower, but the theatrical-genius-strategist build is a fair match.

9. Aldnoah.Zero

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: When a long-dormant war between Earth and an aristocratic Martian empire reignites, a cold, hyper-analytical high schooler named Inaho finds himself out-thinking vastly superior enemy war machines with nothing but tactics and nerve. A Martian soldier caught between worlds complicates everything. The mecha battles are tense chess matches. It is a slick, calculated war drama.

Aldnoah.Zero features an interplanetary war between Earth and an aristocratic Martian empire, the Vers Empire, with a protagonist who is a hyper-analytical high schooler using geometry, physics, and tactical decoys in obsolete training mechs to systematically dismantle overwhelmingly superior imperial super-weapons. That underdog-mind-beats-superior-force thrill is pure Lelouch on the battlefield, and the political mecha war with its grey rival echoes Code Geass's structure. It is colder and less operatic, and the lead has little charisma to speak of, which keeps it lower, but the tactical-mecha-war build is a genuine match.

10. Psycho-Pass

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: In a future where a system scans people's minds to measure their criminal potential, a rookie inspector hunts latent criminals, only to question the dystopia she serves and the brilliant philosopher-criminal exposing its cracks. It is a dense, chilling sci-fi thriller. The antagonist is unforgettable. It is a cornerstone of modern sci-fi anime.

Psycho-Pass is a cerebral battle of wits set in a high-tech surveillance dystopia. Its antagonist, Makishima Shogo, acts as an ideological terrorist who uses orchestrated provocations and untraceable crimes to expose the rot of the Sibyl System, trading Code Geass's mechs for philosophical cat-and-mouse detective work. That charismatic genius who weaponizes chaos to tear down a corrupt order is a clear cousin to Lelouch, and the debate over justice, control, and freedom is pure Code Geass material. There is no mecha, army, or supernatural power, which keeps it lower, but the brilliant-mind-versus-the-system build is a solid fit.

11. Akame ga Kill!

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A naive young fighter joins a band of assassins working to topple a corrupt, decadent empire from the shadows, using unique weapons called Imperial Arms. The cast is colorful and nobody is safe: the body count is notoriously high. It balances dark themes with pulpy fun. It is a divisive but memorable ride.

Akame ga Kill focuses on a clandestine underground rebel group, Night Raid, using specialized weapons to systematically assassinate corrupt imperial officials and spark a nationwide revolution against a tyrannical regime, which shares Code Geass's core of an underdog resistance toppling an empire. The moral greyness of doing ugly things for a just cause echoes the Black Knights closely. It leans more on gory direct brawls than high-level political chess, and it is pulpier overall, which keeps it lowest, but the rebellion-against-an-empire build is a fair match.

A couple that get recommended but watch differently

Search around for anime similar to Code Geass and a few names come up worth flagging, each with a catch.

The obvious one is the Code Geass franchise itself: the Lelouch of the Re;surrection film and the newer Rozé of the Recapture series continue the saga, so they are less a show like Code Geass and more literally more Code Geass, best watched after the original two seasons. Valvrave the Liberator is another Sunrise mecha-rebellion with a dystopian empire, political intrigue, and a dark twist, often called a spiritual cousin, but it is campier and messier than Code Geass, a fun ride that never quite reaches the same heights. Both are worth your time, just with those notes in mind.

FAQs

Is there an anime exactly like Code Geass?

Not a perfect twin, but two come very close. Death Note is the mastermind match, a brilliant, morally-grey genius who gains a supernatural power and creates a masked alter ego to remake the world, while Guilty Crown is the direct spiritual sibling, written by a Code Geass co-creator and built on the same occupied-Japan, soul-power, resistance template. Each captures the core of what makes Code Geass tick.

What is the best anime like Code Geass?

For the closest overall match, Death Note and Guilty Crown lead the pack, one for the scheming anti-hero, the other for the shared blueprint. If you want the mecha-and-military-war side specifically, Mobile Suit Gundam 00 and 86 are the best anime like Code Geass to jump into next, both pairing tactical mecha combat with real political weight.

Why are anime like Code Geass and Death Note always paired together?

Because Lelouch and Light are the two icons of the same archetype: the brilliant, charismatic anti-hero who gains a supernatural power, builds a masked revolutionary identity, and wages a secret, morally-grey war to remake a broken world through a battle of wits. If you searched for anime like Code Geass and Death Note, you are chasing that exact genius-anti-hero thrill, and each is the first show fans point to for the other.

Is there a new Code Geass anime?

Yes. Beyond the original two seasons, the franchise released the Lelouch of the Re;surrection film in 2019 and the all-new series Code Geass: Rozé of the Recapture in 2024, which follows a fresh cast years later. More projects have been announced, so the saga is actively expanding.

Where can you watch anime like Code Geass?

Most of these stream on Crunchyroll, including the original Code Geass, with Legend of the Galactic Heroes also on Prime Video. Streaming rights shift by region and over time, so double-check what is available where you are before you settle in.
