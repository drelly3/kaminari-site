In most shonen, the catchphrase is just hype. In My Hero Academia, it's a promise, and the whole series is about how much it costs to keep one. All Might says "I am here" through mouthfuls of blood. Deku shatters his own arms nearly every time he reaches someone in time. "Plus Ultra" gets harder to shout every single season. That's why these My Hero Academia quotes land the way they do. They aren't slogans, they're vows.

One note before we start: plenty of the MHA quotes floating around online are fan-made, so everything here is verified from the actual series. And a light heads-up that the emotional section gets into how the story ends.

My Hero Academia quotes about going beyond

The engine of the whole show is a two-word command, and All Might delivers it like a battle cry.

"Go beyond. Plus Ultra!"

It sounds like bravado until you realize it's the only setting these characters have. All Might turns it into real advice when Deku is running on empty.

"If you feel yourself hitting up against your limit, remember for what cause you clench your fists. Remember why you started down this path, and let that memory carry you beyond your limit."

Even Aizawa, the least sentimental teacher alive, builds the same idea into his classroom rule. These My Hero Academia inspirational quotes work because the school itself runs on them.

"If you don't go beyond your limits, you won't become first-class."

My Hero Academia quotes about what makes a hero

The series has a real thesis about heroism, and Deku states it plainly: winning was never the point.

"A hero isn't someone who wins. A hero is someone who runs toward the person who needs help."

That's the whole character in one line, and it's why he keeps throwing himself at fights he can't win. He frames the version he's chasing even more simply.

"Saving people with a smile, that's the hero I want to be."

All Might, the man Deku is trying to become, draws the line at the exact moment it's hardest to cross.

"When there's nothing to gain, that's when a real hero steps in!"

MHA quotes that hit hard

Spoilers for how the series ends ahead. These are the ones that hit hardest, because you watch them cost someone everything. It starts before Deku even has a power, when a quirkless kid sprints at a villain to save his bully.

"My body just moved on its own."

That single reckless act is what convinces the world's greatest hero to bet everything on him.

"You can become a hero."

Four words that rebuild a boy who'd been told the opposite his whole life. And seasons later, as All Might burns through the last of his strength and hands the future to his student, he closes the circle with the line that breaks every fan.

"Next, it's your turn."

My Hero Academia quotes about weakness and growth

Not every lesson here is gentle. Bakugo, the walking ego, learns the hard way that arrogance has a blind spot.

"If all you ever do is look down on people, you won't recognize your own weaknesses."

And in one of the best moments in the series, Deku refuses to let Todoroki throw away half of who he is, screaming at him mid-fight until it lands.

"It's your power, not his!"

That's the whole MHA idea of growth in a sentence: what you were handed, good or bad, becomes yours the moment you decide what to do with it. All Might frames the same lesson around failure, and makes it sound almost easy.

"Whether you win or lose, you can always come out ahead by learning from the experience."

The most famous lines every fan knows

Some lines are pure reassurance, and none is bigger than All Might's entrance. If you want the best MHA quotes for a bad day, start here.

"It's fine now. Why? Because I am here!"

That "I am here" became the sound of safety for an entire fictional world, and eventually Deku inherits both the phrase and the weight behind it. All Might's other signature is his definition of the job itself, and it's among the best My Hero Academia quotes for a reason.

"A true hero always finds a way for justice to be served!"

Said with that big grin, through whatever injury he happened to be hiding that day.

Next, it's your turn

Put all of it together and My Hero Academia is really one long relay race. All Might inherited his power from someone, poured it into a quirkless kid who ran toward danger before he had any reason to, and spent his last embers making sure that kid could carry it further than he ever could. Now that the story is complete, with the manga finished in 2024 and the anime wrapping its final season, you can see the whole handoff at once, and the best My Hero Academia quotes stop being catchphrases and become what they always were: promises, passed from one set of shaking hands to the next. Frequently asked My Hero Academia questions

What is the most famous My Hero Academia quote?

All Might's "It's fine now. Why? Because I am here!" is the most iconic, closely followed by the U.A. motto "Plus Ultra." Deku eventually inherits the "I am here" line, which is part of why it carries so much weight.

What does "Plus Ultra" mean?

It's U.A. High's motto, borrowed from a Spanish phrase meaning "go beyond." In the series it becomes a whole philosophy: push past your limit, then push past that too. All Might's full version is "Go beyond, Plus Ultra."

What did All Might say when he passed on his power?

As he burned through the last of One For All, All Might told Deku, "Next, it's your turn," passing both his strength and the role of Symbol of Peace to his student. It's widely considered the most emotional moment in the series.

Which MHA quote hits the hardest?

For most fans it's "Next, it's your turn," or "You can become a hero," the line that changed Deku's life when the world had written him off. Both hit because they mark a torch being passed at real cost.

What's Deku's best quote about being a hero?

"A hero isn't someone who wins. A hero is someone who runs toward the person who needs help." It captures his entire character: for Deku, heroism was never about strength, it was about who you run toward.
