Anime characters with black hair are so common that the color barely registers as a choice, which is exactly why it hits so hard when a character truly owns it. Black is the medium's honest default, the shade that matches most of the real-world casts these stories are drawn from, so a designer who leans into it is usually saying something about restraint, intensity, or menace rather than reaching for a loud palette. A dark hair anime guy tends to read as grounded and serious before he says a word, which is one reason the anime male black hair look dominates so many fan searches.

There is a little room in the definition too. Plenty of dark hair anime characters technically shade into deep blue or near-black brown under studio lighting, and most fans lump those in without a second thought. Within the black-haired ranks, two silhouettes do the heaviest lifting: long black hair, which reads noble, mysterious, or dangerous, and short black hair, which reads clean, sharp, and intense. The most famous black-haired faces of all, the straw-hatted Luffy and the spiky Goku, are so defined by other traits that we are setting them aside to spotlight characters whose dark hair is closer to the center of who they are.

Below is our ranked pick of the most popular anime characters with black hair, leaning into the male-heavy way people search for them while making room for the women who wear it just as well. After the list we group the standout long and short black hair looks and answer the questions fans ask most.

Top 12 anime characters with black hair

We ranked these by how completely the character owns the look, not by raw popularity alone, though the two tend to travel together.

1. Sasuke Uchiha

Anime: Naruto

The blue-black hair that launched a thousand rivalries belongs to the Uchiha clan's most famous survivor. Dark hair is practically the Uchiha brand, and Sasuke wears it as both inheritance and burden across an arc that drags him from prodigy to avenger to reluctant ally. Every brooding stare and every Sharingan flash lands harder framed by that ink-dark fringe. He is the character most people picture the instant the topic of black-haired anime leads comes up.

2. L Lawliet

Anime: Death Note

The messiest black hair in anime sits on top of its sharpest mind. In Death Note, L slouches, goes barefoot, eats his weight in sugar, and cracks impossible cases while looking like he rolled out of bed mid-thought, and the permanent bedhead is a huge part of the appeal. That deliberate scruffiness sells the idea of a genius who has zero energy left over for anything as trivial as a comb. Fans have voted him to the top of black-haired character rankings for years, and the hair is inseparable from the legend.

3. Levi Ackerman

Anime: Attack on Titan

The clean black undercut that half the fandom has taken to their barber belongs to humanity's strongest soldier. Levi's hair is as disciplined as the rest of him, sharp lines and not a strand out of place, a visual echo of a man who values control and cleanliness above almost everything. He spins through the air cutting down Titans without disturbing the part in it. Precise, blunt, and lethal, he made a simple dark cut into one of the medium's most imitated looks.

4. Lelouch Lamperouge

Anime: Code Geass

Black hair and a schemer's smirk make an unforgettable pair on the exiled prince turned masked revolutionary. Lelouch's dark, slightly tousled hair frames every villainous monologue and every chess-board gambit as he plays entire empires against each other. The Geass smoldering in one eye pops against it. His whole tragic-mastermind aesthetic depends on that grounded dark palette, the everyday student hiding the most dangerous mind in the room.

5. Itachi Uchiha

Anime: Naruto

The low black ponytail signals tragedy before Itachi ever opens his mouth. Older brother, clan prodigy, and the man who carried out an atrocity under orders no one else could bear, he wears his dark hair long and severe, the tear-troughs beneath his eyes deepening the weary calm. It is one of the most quietly mournful looks in shonen. The style became so iconic that "long black ponytail plus terrible secret" is now practically its own character shorthand.

6. Megumi Fushiguro

Anime: Jujutsu Kaisen

Spiky black hair and a permanent scowl make Megumi one of the most-voted dark-haired characters of the current era. His hair juts out in sharp, restless points, matching a sorcerer who would rather brood in the background than chase the spotlight. Commanding an army of shadow shikigami, he treats his own talent like a chore he never asked for. The moody teenager look is doing a lot of work, and the black hair anchors all of it.

7. Kirito

Anime: Sword Art Online

Kirito built an entire in-game identity around the color, earning the title the Black Swordsman with matching dark hair and twin black coats. His look is the rare case where the character consciously chose black as a statement of intent, a lone player determined to clear a death game on his own terms. The dark palette became his brand. Off the battlefield he is gentler than the edgelord aesthetic suggests, but the hair keeps the myth intact.

8. Guts

Anime: Berserk

Long before Kirito, Guts was the original black-haired lone swordsman, all jet-dark hair and grim resolve behind a sword the size of a person. His hair falls in a rough, battle-worn mess that suits a man who has never once had the luxury of stopping to fix it. Every scar and every mile shows. The look defined the brooding dark-haired warrior template that countless later heroes borrowed from without ever matching his sheer endurance.

9. Shigeo "Mob" Kageyama

Anime: Mob Psycho 100

The plainest black bowl cut on this list belongs to one of its most powerful characters, and the mismatch is entirely the point. Mob looks like the quietest, most forgettable kid in class, hair sitting in a flat, unremarkable dome, right up until his emotions hit a hundred percent and he reshapes the sky. Understatement is the whole design philosophy. His ordinary dark hair is a deliberate mask over a world-bending esper.

10. Nico Robin

Anime: One Piece

Robin carries long black hair and the calmest presence on a very loud crew. The lone survivor of an erased civilization, she spent years as a wanted fugitive before finding a family, and that history sits behind an unshakably composed exterior. Her intellect anchors the crew, her wit runs dry and dark, and her ability to sprout limbs from any surface makes her deceptively deadly. Poised and quietly formidable, she is proof the look carries just as much weight on the women.

11. Yor Forger

Anime: Spy x Family

Yor's black hair frames a double life that runs on contradiction. By day she is a sweet, socially awkward office worker and improvised mother, endearingly hopeless at small talk. By night she is one of the deadliest assassins in the city. The gap between the gentle persona and the lethal one is the entire engine of her character, and her dark, elegant style bridges both halves without ever tipping her hand.

12. Homura Akemi

Anime: Puella Magi Madoka Magica

Homura's long black hair marks the moment a timid girl hardened into something colder and far more determined. What starts as a shy character becomes a relentless one, looping through time again and again out of a devotion that curdles into obsession. The flowing dark hair mirrors that shift from softness to steel. Beneath the composed, guarded surface sits one of the most quietly heartbroken characters in the medium.

Black hair, unmistakable presence

Black hair asks the least of a character designer and somehow gives the most back. It lets Sasuke carry the weight of a bloodline, lets L look like a genius who forgot to sleep, and lets Yor hide a killer behind a gentle smile, all without a single loud color doing the work for them. The best anime characters with black hair treat the shade as a blank canvas for personality, which is exactly why the look never gets old no matter how many hundreds of characters share it.

FAQs

Who is the most popular black hair anime guy?

Sasuke Uchiha, L Lawliet, and Levi Ackerman trade the top spots on nearly every ranking of the black hair anime guy archetype. Japanese popularity polls also push Monkey D. Luffy and Goku to the front, since both are black-haired, though most fans think of their other traits first. For a pure dark hair anime guy who is defined by the look itself, Sasuke and L lead the pack, and they anchor most lists of male characters with black hair.

Which anime characters have long black hair?

The anime characters with long black hair worth knowing include Itachi Uchiha, Byakuya Kuchiki, Madara Uchiha, and Illumi Zoldyck among the men, with Nico Robin, Yor Forger, and Kagome leading the women. When someone pictures an anime guy with long black hair, it is usually a villain or a swordsman, since the look is reserved for the most composed, mysterious, or dangerous characters.

Which anime characters have short black hair?

The anime characters with short black hair everyone pictures first are Levi Ackerman, Sasuke Uchiha, Mob, and L, joined by Eren Yeager and Haikyu's Tobio Kageyama. The style spans everything from rigid military cuts to messy, lived-in fringes.

Why do so many anime characters have black hair?

Anime characters that have black hair are everywhere because black is the realistic default for the largely Japanese casts these stories depict, so it is the natural baseline before a designer reaches for anything flashier. Studios also use it to signal that a character is grounded, serious, or intense, and it makes a striking contrast against brighter-haired cast members, which is partly why anime characters with black hair outnumber every other color in the medium.

Which female anime characters have black hair?

Nico Robin, Yor Forger, and Homura Akemi are among the most recognizable, with Nezuko Kamado, Momo Yaoyorozu, and Kagome close behind. Black hair reads just as elegant and commanding on the women as it does on the men.
