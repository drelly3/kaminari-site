Gundam looks like the most intimidating franchise in anime, and newcomers usually ask the wrong question first. There is no single Gundam watch order, because Gundam is not one story. It is dozens of separate series across a handful of unconnected timelines, most of them fully standalone. So you do not need to watch Gundam in order. The real question is not what order to watch Gundam in, but which series to start with, and we would point most newcomers to The Witch from Mercury.

The scale is real. Gundam has run since 1979 across more than forty TV series, films, and OVAs, which is why it feels like homework. Those productions are grouped into separate universes, each with its own war and its own cast. Cosmic Era does not connect to After Colony, and you never need one to enjoy another.

There is no one Gundam series order to memorize, only timelines to choose from. Only the Universal Century runs in sequence, its series building on each other across decades. Below we sort out where to start, map every timeline, and lay out the Universal Century in the order to watch it.

Where to start: the best Gundam beginner picks

The best starting point depends on how much you care about modern animation. For most newcomers, we would begin with a recent standalone series, since they need no prior knowledge and look sharp. The Witch from Mercury (2022) is the easiest on-ramp of all, a complete two-cour story with a fresh lead and a school-meets-mecha hook that pulls people in fast. Right behind it sit Iron-Blooded Orphans (2015) for something grittier, Gundam 00 (2007) for a modern-feeling war drama, and Gundam SEED (2002) for the classic that converted a generation.

If you would rather start where the franchise began, the Universal Century is the backbone, and we cover its order below. Just know that the 1979 original shows its age, so plenty of newcomers bounce off it. Beginning with a modern standalone, then circling back to the Universal Century once you are hooked, tends to work far better than forcing yourself through the oldest one cold.

The Universal Century watch order

The Universal Century is the one part of the Gundam watch order that truly runs in sequence, a single sprawling history from UC 0079 onward. For a first watch, production order works best, since it follows how the story was built and spares you jarring jumps in animation quality. You can begin with the original 1979 TV series, its condensed Movie Trilogy, or the modern prequel Gundam: The Origin, then continue from Zeta Gundam through the rest.

That sequence is the essential Universal Century, from the One Year War through Char and Amuro's final clash and on into the next generation. Two later series, Gundam F91 (UC 0123) and Victory Gundam (UC 0153), carry the timeline further into the future if you want it. Around the main line sit a stack of side-story OVAs like War in the Pocket, The 08th MS Team, Stardust Memory, Thunderbolt, and the 2024 CG series Requiem for Vengeance, all optional and best saved for after the core. On a rewatch, the in-universe chronological order becomes a rewarding second pass, but production order is the smoother first trip.

Where to watch Gundam

Gundam is more available than you might expect. Crunchyroll carries most of the modern series and a large slice of the classic Universal Century, so it is the natural home base. Netflix holds a few of the films, including Hathaway and the Unicorn RE:0096 recut, in many regions. Best of all, the official Gundam.info channel on YouTube streams a rotating library of full series for free. Availability shifts by region, so check what is live where you are before you commit to a timeline.

Pick a universe and pilot in

For all its size, the Gundam watch order is freeing once the secret clicks: there is no single path, because each timeline stands alone. Start with a modern standalone like The Witch from Mercury, branch into whatever universe grabs you next, and save the deep Universal Century run for when you want the franchise's full history. There is no wrong door into Gundam, only the one that gets you piloting soonest.

FAQ

What is the Gundam watch order?

There is no single Gundam watch order, because the franchise is split into separate timelines that do not connect. You start with one self-contained series rather than watching everything in sequence. Only the Universal Century timeline has an internal order, running from Mobile Suit Gundam through Zeta, ZZ, Char's Counterattack, Unicorn, and Hathaway.

Where should a beginner start with Gundam?

A modern standalone series is the best first pick. The Witch from Mercury is the easiest place to start, with Iron-Blooded Orphans, Gundam 00, and Gundam SEED close behind. Each is self-contained, so you can enjoy it with zero prior Gundam knowledge and then explore other timelines from there.

Do I need to watch Gundam in order?

Not across the franchise. Because the timelines are separate, you can watch them in any order you like, so there is no need to follow one master list. The only place order matters is within the Universal Century, where the series build on each other and play best in production order.

What is the Universal Century watch order?

For a first watch, use production order: Mobile Suit Gundam (or its Movie Trilogy), then Zeta Gundam, Gundam ZZ, Char's Counterattack, Gundam Unicorn, and Hathaway. Later series like F91 and Victory Gundam continue the timeline, and the side-story OVAs are optional extras best saved for after the core.

Where can I watch Gundam?

Crunchyroll hosts most modern series and much of the classic Universal Century, while Netflix carries a few films like Hathaway. The official Gundam.info YouTube channel also streams many full series for free. Availability varies by region, so check what is live near you before you start.
