A Certain Magical Index has a reputation for being a scheduling nightmare, and this time it is fair. The good news: the A Certain Magical Index watch order has one clean path once it is laid out. For a first watch, go Index, then A Certain Scientific Railgun, Index II, the Miracle of Endymion movie, Railgun S, Accelerator, Railgun T, and finally Index III. That is the entire core, in the order that makes the story hit hardest.

The tangle comes from two shows sharing one city. Index follows Touma, a boy whose right hand cancels any power, through the magic side of Academy City. Railgun hands the lead to Mikoto Misaka for the science side, the espers and the politics that run it. The two shows sit on overlapping timelines and keep crossing paths.

So here is how to watch A Certain Magical Index in order without losing the thread: the full lineup at a glance, the two questions every newcomer asks, and where it all streams. One quirk to flag up front, the grand finale actually aired years before two of the seasons that were built to set it up.

It aired back in 2018 and 2019, before both Accelerator and Railgun T, but it is the chronological climax of the whole franchise, so saving it for the end keeps the biggest payoff where it belongs. Index first or Railgun first?

Here is the first real fork, and it is the one people argue about most. A Certain Magical Index came first and builds the world from the ground up, so it is the natural front door. A Certain Scientific Railgun is technically the spin-off, but it looks sharper, moves faster, and Misaka is one of the most loved leads in the medium, which makes it a tempting place to begin.

My honest read: start with Index. Touma's story explains the rules of Academy City and the magic side that everything else leans on, and Railgun quietly assumes you have picked some of that up. Index does open dense, though, and if the heavy magic lore has you bouncing off, starting with Railgun is a completely fair gateway. You meet Misaka first, fall for the science side, then double back to Touma once you are hooked. Neither choice breaks the story, so pick the entry point that keeps you watching.

Release order or chronological order?

The question of what order to watch A Certain Magical Index in usually comes down to two camps. Release order (the blended path at the top of this guide) is the one I recommend for a first watch, because the reveals were written to land in that sequence and the pacing feels deliberate. Chronological order rearranges everything into strict in-universe time, cutting between Index and Railgun arc by arc. It makes for a fascinating rewatch once you already know the story, but for a newcomer it is fiddly and easy to lose your place in.

There is a third option worth knowing about. If hopping between two casts bugs you, watch by series instead: all of Index, then all of Railgun, or the reverse. You stay with one protagonist the whole way, at the cost of some cross-series timing. Plenty of fans swear by it. For most people, though, the blended release order up top is the smoothest first trip through.

Where to watch A Certain Magical Index

Streaming is simpler than the watch order, at least. Crunchyroll is the main home for the whole franchise now that the old Funimation library has folded into it, so Index, Railgun, Accelerator, and the movie mostly live under one roof. Regional availability still shifts around, so check what is live in your country before you settle in. None of that changes the sequence, only where each piece happens to stream.

FAQ

What is the A Certain Magical Index watch order?

The recommended A Certain Magical Index watch order is A Certain Magical Index, A Certain Scientific Railgun, Index II, the Miracle of Endymion movie, Railgun S, Accelerator, Railgun T, and A Certain Magical Index III last. It blends release order with a little timeline sense so Index III lands as the finale it was written to be.

Should I watch Index or Railgun first?

Start with A Certain Magical Index. It sets up Academy City and the magic side the rest of the franchise builds on, and Railgun assumes some of that knowledge. If Index feels too dense early on, Railgun is a fair alternative entry point, since you can meet Misaka first and circle back to Touma later.

Do I need to watch Railgun to understand Index?

Not strictly. Index works on its own for the main plot, and you can follow Touma's story without Railgun. You will miss a lot of Misaka's development and the fuller version of the shared Sisters arc, though, so Railgun is well worth folding in rather than skipping.

Should I watch A Certain Magical Index in release order or chronological order?

Release order for a first watch. The reveals and pacing were designed around it, so it plays smoother. Chronological order interleaves the series by in-universe time and makes a great rewatch, but it is fiddly for newcomers and easier to get lost in.

Where can I watch A Certain Magical Index?

Crunchyroll carries the franchise, including both Index and Railgun, the Accelerator spin-off, and the Endymion movie, following the merge of the old Funimation catalog. Availability shifts by region, so check what is streaming near you before you start.
