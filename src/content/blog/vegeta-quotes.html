Somewhere right now, someone is posting "Push through the pain, giving up hurts more" over a Vegeta edit, fully convinced the Prince of all Saiyans said it. He didn't. Not in the anime, not in the manga, not in any dub. It's one of dozens of fake Vegeta quotes the internet has quietly decided are real. So this list does something most don't: it sticks to lines Vegeta actually said, sorted by the mood that sends you looking, and it calls out the famous fakes at the end so you never get caught quoting fan edits. Real ones only. Let's give the prince his due.

Vegeta quotes for when you need to prove them wrong

Vegeta's whole identity is refusing to be looked down on, so it's fitting that some of the best Vegeta lines come from him with his back against the wall and everyone counting him out.

"You may have invaded my mind and my body, but there's one thing a Saiyan always keeps... his pride!"

Majin Vegeta, shoving Babidi out of his own head. Even mind control couldn't take the one thing he refuses to surrender.

"There's no such thing as fair or unfair in battle. There is only victory or, in your case, defeat."

Pure Vegeta economy. No lecture, no comfort, just the scoreboard.

"I didn't care about being better than Kakarot. I didn't care about being a Super Saiyan. I didn't care if I lived. And then, it happened. The sleeper has awakened. I am the Prince of all Saiyans once again!"

He describes finally going Super Saiyan after training at 450 times gravity, the moment he stopped straining for it and it arrived anyway. The prince, reclaiming his title out loud.

Vegeta quotes for when you're done coming in second

For most of the series Vegeta is chasing a low-class Saiyan who keeps ending up ahead of him, and that wound produces some of his rawest lines.

"What do you know of meaningless? Spend most of your life ruled by another. Watch your race dwindle to a handful. And then tell me what has more meaning than your own strength!"

Screamed at the Supreme Kai during the Buu saga, when he's told his pride doesn't matter. For a man whose whole people were wiped out and who spent his childhood as Frieza's weapon, strength isn't ego. It's the only thing that was ever his.

"Go, Kakarot. You're number one."

The one that costs him the most. After a lifetime of insisting he's the superior Saiyan, Vegeta finally admits, out loud, that his rival is the better man. Growth, from someone allergic to it.

You can feel the whole rivalry in the gap between those two lines. He trains alone in the gravity chamber while Goku naps his way to new heights, and the fact that a "low-class soldier" surpassed a prince ate at him for years before he could say those four words.

Vegeta quotes for when you're fighting for someone else

"Trunks... Bulma... I do this for you. And yes, even for you, Kakarot."

His goodbye before the Final Explosion, blowing himself apart trying to take Majin Buu with him. It's the turning point of his entire character. As Piccolo narrates the moment, it's the first time in his life Vegeta fights for someone other than himself, and he can't even say it without folding his respect for Goku into it. The villain who arrived on Earth to destroy it dies trying to save his family. That's the whole arc in one line, and it's the reason so many fans call this the definitive Vegeta moment.

Funny Vegeta lines for when you need a laugh

The proudest man in the universe is also, secretly, a bit of a drama king, and that's half his charm.

"Sure, let's go see Yamcha."

Sarcastically caving to Bulma at a party, and quietly revealing that her ex still lives rent-free in his head years later. The line was Christopher Sabat's idea for the dub, and it's peak petty Vegeta.

"I'll eat your whole race!"

Shouted at an octopus. During a cruise. Vegeta holds grudges against seafood, and honestly the commitment is admirable.

"This is between me and your circus clown of a father!"

Snapping at Gohan about Goku mid-standoff. Vegeta's insult game stays undefeated, and Goku's deadpan "circus clown, well that's a new one" only makes it better.

The famous Vegeta quotes he never actually said

Here's the part the motivation pages won't tell you, mostly because they're the ones spreading it. A big chunk of the quotes you see on edits and posters are fan-made. Good lines, sometimes genuinely inspiring, but not from Dragon Ball.

The biggest offender:

"Push through the pain. Giving up hurts more."

You will not find this in any episode or the manga. It's a fan creation that went viral on gym edits, and it's now quoted back as gospel. A few others in the same boat: "It's not over when you lose, it's over when you quit," and "Only a failure abandons his principles and his pride." All fan-written, none canon.

No shade if you've shared one. They're catchy, and they capture a version of Vegeta that feels right. But if you want the real thing with the same fire, reach for "one thing a Saiyan always keeps, his pride," or the meaningless speech above. Vegeta gave you plenty of actual lines about refusing to break. He doesn't need the ghostwritten ones.

The Prince Earns His Crown

Vegeta's best lines only land because of where he started. A genocidal prince who showed up to wipe out Earth becomes the guy who blows himself apart for his wife and son, and every quote sits somewhere on that road from pride to love. Right now Dragon Ball is in a quiet stretch, with Daima wrapped and no new series airing and Akira Toriyama gone since 2024, which makes going back through the real Vegeta quotes feel less like nostalgia and more like keeping the character honest. Whatever mood brought you here, there's a genuine line above that meets it. No fakes required.

Frequently asked Vegeta questions

What is Vegeta's most famous quote?

His sacrifice line, "Trunks... Bulma... I do this for you. And yes, even for you, Kakarot," from the Majin Buu saga. It's the emotional peak of his arc and the most quoted Vegeta moment. His "It's over 9000!" scouter line is the most memed.

Did Vegeta really say "Push through the pain, giving up hurts more"?

No. It's a fan-made line that went viral on motivational edits. It doesn't appear in any episode, movie, or the manga. For a real Vegeta quote with the same energy, go with "one thing a Saiyan always keeps, his pride."

Who actually said "It's over 9000"?

Vegeta, not Goku, reading his scouter over Goku's power level. It's constantly misattributed to Goku. The original Japanese line was actually "over 8000," bumped to 9000 in the English dub.

What's the best Vegeta quote about pride?

"You may have invaded my mind and my body, but there's one thing a Saiyan always keeps, his pride." He says it while throwing off Babidi's mind control, which makes it the purest distillation of his whole character.

What did Vegeta say before he sacrificed himself?

"Trunks... Bulma... I do this for you. And yes, even for you, Kakarot." Then he unleashes the Final Explosion to try to destroy Majin Buu. It's the first time he ever fights for someone other than himself.
