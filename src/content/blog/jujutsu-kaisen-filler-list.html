Fake Filler Alert: Arcs Fans Wrongly Skip

Scan any Jujutsu Kaisen filler list, and you'll see a wall of green, but fan advice tells a different story. These arcs and episodes get mislabeled as skippable constantly, and cutting any of them means missing manga canon.

Hidden Inventory / Premature Death (Season 2, Episodes 1-5)

The most commonly mislabeled arc in JJK. When Season 2 opens, Yuji, Megumi, and Nobara vanish completely. The story jumps back in time to follow teenage Gojo and Geto on a mission to protect the Star Plasma Vessel. Casual viewers see the main trio disappear and assume it's a side story.

Hidden Inventory explains why Gojo became who he is, why Geto fell, and what the entire Shibuya Incident is emotionally built on. Skip it, and Shibuya loses half its weight. Characters, motivations, and betrayals in later arcs trace directly back to these five episodes. Arguably the best arc in the series, and certainly the worst one to cut.

Episode 21: Jujutsu Kaisen Baseball (Season 1)

A full episode of Goodwill Event baseball. Feels like pure comic relief. It's manga canon, it develops the Kyoto school cast (particularly Todo and Mechamaru), and its character moments carry into later arcs. It's also one of the funniest episodes in the series. The Goodwill Event matters, and this episode is part of it.

Jujutsu Kaisen 0 (Movie)

Sometimes treated as an optional prequel you can circle back to whenever. JJK 0 adapts Akutami's original manga (Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical School) and introduces Yuta Okkotsu, who becomes a central player in the Culling Game. Watching Season 3 without JJK 0 means meeting Yuta with zero context for his power, his motivation, or his connection to Rika. Watch it after Season 1 or before Season 2.

The Hot Take: Filler Is a Solved Problem

JJK didn't avoid filler through discipline or restraint. It avoided filler because the production model changed.

Naruto and Bleach aired 50+ episodes per year without breaks. When the anime caught up to the manga, studios had two options: go on hiatus or invent filler arcs. They chose filler because weekly time slots don't wait. JJK runs 12-24 episodes per season, takes a multi-year break, and comes back when there's enough manga to adapt. MAPPA never needed to invent a single scene because the schedule never demanded one.

The manga finished in September 2024 with 271 chapters, and the anime has only adapted through chapter 181 as of Season 3. Filler was never a risk for JJK, and it won't be for most seasonal anime going forward.

FAQ

Does Jujutsu Kaisen have filler?

No. The complete Jujutsu Kaisen filler list contains zero filler episodes across all three seasons. Every episode is a direct manga adaptation. The only skippable content is recap specials and compilation films.

How many Jujutsu Kaisen episodes can I skip?

None of the numbered canon episodes. You can skip three recap specials (two from Season 2, one from Season 3) and two compilation films (Hidden Inventory comp movie and Jujutsu Kaisen: Execution). Everything else is essential.

Is the Hidden Inventory arc filler?

No. Hidden Inventory (Season 2, episodes 1-5) is fully manga canon and one of the most important arcs in the series. It drops the main trio to focus on young Gojo and Geto, which leads some fans to mistake it for a side story. Skipping it undermines the entire Shibuya Incident.

Is the Jujutsu Kaisen 0 movie required?

Strongly recommended. JJK 0 introduces Yuta Okkotsu, who becomes a central character starting in the Culling Game arc (Season 3+). Watch it after Season 1 or before Season 2.

What order should I watch Jujutsu Kaisen?

Season 1 (24 episodes), JJK 0 (movie), Season 2 (23 episodes), Season 3: The Culling Game Part 1 (12 episodes). Skip the recap specials and compilation films if watching straight through.
