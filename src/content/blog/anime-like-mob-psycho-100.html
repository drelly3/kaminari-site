The anime most like Mob Psycho 100, ranked

Here are the anime like Mob Psycho 100 that genuinely share its build, ranked from the closest match to the loosest. The Similarity Rating is our read on how near each one gets to Mob Psycho 100 specifically, not how good the show is overall, and we kept the stars to whole numbers. The build we are measuring against is simple: an action-comedy with a deep emotional coming-of-age core, a humble protagonist who values growth over his overwhelming powers, experimental animation, and a blend of deadpan comedy and sincere feeling.

1. One Punch Man

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Saitama has trained so hard that he can defeat any opponent with a single punch, which has left him deeply, hilariously bored. He drifts through a world of professional heroes and monstrous threats, mostly unrecognized, searching for a challenge and a little meaning. The animation is spectacular and the comedy is deadpan gold. It is a modern classic.

Of everything here, this is the undisputed creative twin, because it comes from the exact same author. One Punch Man is ONE's other masterpiece, and it shares his deadpan comedic timing, his distinctive design philosophy, and an overpowered protagonist whose immense power fails to solve his inner emotional fulfillment or personal growth. Both ask what actually makes a person worthwhile when raw strength is a given. It is more satire than coming-of-age, but as ONE's sibling work, it is the natural next watch. 2. The Disastrous Life of Saiki K.

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Prime Video

Synopsis: Kusuo Saiki is born with every psychic power imaginable, which has made his life an exhausting chore, and all he wants is to be left alone to eat sweets in peace. His absurd classmates and his own godlike abilities constantly get in the way. Told in rapid-fire deadpan narration, it is relentlessly funny. It is one of anime's great comedies.

Saiki K. is the comedy-side mirror of Mob's powers. Its lead is an introverted high schooler with omnipotent psychic abilities, telekinesis, telepathy, pyrokinesis, clairvoyance, who views those powers as an annoying curse and wants nothing more than to be an ordinary student, only to be constantly dragged into bizarre situations by eccentric peers. Saiki and Mob are kindred spirits, overpowered kids who find their gifts more burden than blessing. The emotional depth is lighter, but the overpowered-esper-wants-normalcy premise and the deadpan comedy make it a near-perfect match.

3. Hinamatsuri

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A capsule containing a deadpan, immensely powerful telekinetic girl named Hina crashes into the apartment of a young Yakuza, and he reluctantly takes her in. What follows is a string of absurd, warm-hearted misadventures as he ends up genuinely parenting her. The comedy is bone-dry and the heart sneaks up on you. It is an underrated gem.

Hinamatsuri is the closest match to Mob Psycho's Mob-and-Reigen found-family dynamic. A deadpan, emotionally detached girl with cataclysmic telekinetic powers falls into the life of an ordinary adult, a mid-level Yakuza who first exploits her abilities for utility but ends up genuinely parenting her, which rhymes directly with Reigen and Mob. It blends massive telekinetic destruction with genuinely touching emotional maturation and deadpan comedy, exactly Mob Psycho's mix. The esper-and-reluctant-guardian heart and the dry humor make it a near-perfect match.

4. Dandadan

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A girl who believes in ghosts and a boy who believes in aliens discover both are terrifyingly real, and are swept into a frenetic world of occult spirits and alien invaders. The animation is explosive and the tone swings from horror to comedy to tenderness. It is one of the most exciting modern anime around. It is a wild, heartfelt ride.

Directed by Science SARU, Dandadan captures the fluid, wildly expressive sakuga that Studio Bones made famous in Mob Psycho. It blends modern school life with high-speed occult spirit exorcisms, yokai curses, and urban psychics, and underneath the chaos is a sweet, earnest teenage dynamic, which is Mob Psycho's exact combination of spectacle and heart. Both come from the cool, unconventional corner of modern manga and treat animation as a playground. It is louder and more chaotic than Mob Psycho's gentle tone, which keeps it a notch lower, but the expressive-animation-and-heart build is a strong match.

5. Mashle: Magic and Muscles

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Mash Burnedead is born with no magic in an elitist magical society, so he bluffs his way into an elite magic academy and solves every problem with absurd physical strength. His deadpan calm and his devotion to his friends carry him through chaos. The tone is a deadpan gag comedy. It plays like a magic-school shonen crossed with a bodybuilding joke.

Mashle is a direct thematic companion to Mob and the Body Improvement Club. Mash is an innocent, deadpan, bowl-cut boy who, like Mob, is socially naive and answers complex supernatural attacks with pure physical gym discipline, deadpan bluntness, and extreme devotion to protecting his friends. The parallel to Mob choosing muscles and sincerity over flashy power is uncanny, right down to the haircut and the flat affect. It is more of a straight gag comedy than Mob Psycho's emotional depth, which keeps it mid-list, but the deadpan-muscle-boy build is a clear match.

6. Ranking of Kings

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Bojji is a deaf, physically weak prince mocked and pitied by nearly everyone, who dreams of becoming a great king, accompanied by a shadow-creature named Kage who becomes his first true friend. What looks like a simple storybook grows into a profound, emotional epic. The animation soars in its climaxes. It is a quietly devastating fairy tale.

Ranking of Kings captures Mob Psycho's innocent, vulnerable emotional core. Bojji is a weak, ridiculed underdog whose real strength is his boundless kindness, and his shadow-blob companion Kage mirrors Dimple's reluctant, growing loyalty almost exactly. It pairs storybook simplicity with jaw-dropping, fluid animation climaxes and a profound message about empathy, which is Mob Psycho's whole heart. There are no psychic powers here, which keeps it from the very top, but for Mob's tender, kindness-first soul, it is a beautiful match.

7. Noragami

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A minor god named Yato takes odd jobs for five yen each, dreaming of a shrine of his own, while a human girl and a troubled spirit-weapon get tangled in his world. The series blends supernatural action, deadpan comedy, and surprisingly deep emotional arcs. It is funny, stylish, and quietly moving. It is an underrated gem.

Produced by Studio Bones, Noragami shares the crisp action and urban supernatural exorcisms of Mob Psycho. Yato is a broke, eccentric god-for-hire who works odd jobs for five yen and heavily channels Reigen Arataka's opportunistic hustler energy, but slowly forms a protective, deeply caring bond with a troubled young spirit. That Reigen-like mentor who turns out to genuinely care is pure Mob Psycho, and the deadpan comedy and sneaky emotional arcs line up too. It is a bit more conventional in structure, but the Bones-exorcism-and-mentor build is a strong fit.

8. Gurren Lagann

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A timid boy named Simon and his hot-blooded brother-figure dig out of their underground village and into an epic battle for the surface, piloting a mecha powered by sheer willpower. It is a wildly over-the-top spectacle with a huge emotional core. The animation escalates to the cosmic. It is a beloved classic.

Gurren Lagann matches the soaring emotional journey of a timid, meek boy discovering his inner strength, guided by a loud, charismatic mentor. Simon's arc from shy underground digger to confident hero echoes Mob's own growth, and the brash mentor Kamina is a hot-blooded cousin to Reigen's bravado. It escalates from small-scale struggles into reality-shattering, hand-drawn animation climaxes driven entirely by emotional willpower, which shares Mob Psycho's belief that heart beats raw ability. It is far louder and more bombastic than Mob Psycho's understated tone, which keeps it lower, but the timid-kid-grows-with-a-mentor build is a genuine match.

9. Kill la Kill

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Kill la Kill captures the frenetic, morphing, no-holds-barred hand-drawn animation freedom and over-the-top visual comedy of Mob Psycho's best combat sequences. Every frame is pushed as far as it can go, and under the chaos is a real story about identity and found family. It dials up the punk-rock attitude and hyperactive chaos well past Mob Psycho's gentler register, which keeps it lower, but for the sheer expressive-animation energy that makes Mob Psycho's fights sing, it is a fair match.

My Hero Academia shares Studio Bones' high-tier action production and the core philosophy that true strength comes from empathy, self-improvement, and heart rather than raw destructive talent. Like Mob, Deku starts as an awkward, gentle boy who has to learn how to channel an overwhelming internal power without letting it break him, and the found-family class matters as much as the fights. A couple that get recommended but watch differently

Search around for anime similar to Mob Psycho 100 and two names come up that capture its soul beautifully, with one catch each. They are worth the effort.

Ping Pong the Animation is arguably the closest in spirit, a Masaaki Yuasa masterpiece with the same bold, experimental animation and the same profound coming-of-age heart, where a withdrawn teen slowly learns who he is. The catch is that it can be genuinely hard to find on legal streaming depending on your region. A Silent Voice nails Mob Psycho's tenderness, its kindness, and its focus on a quiet kid growing and connecting, but it is a standalone film drama with no psychic action at all, so it scratches the emotional itch rather than the spectacle one. Both are essential for Mob Psycho fans who love its heart most.

FAQs

Is there an anime exactly like Mob Psycho 100?

Not a perfect twin, but two come very close. One Punch Man is from Mob Psycho's own creator, ONE, and shares its deadpan comedy and its subversion of the power fantasy, while The Disastrous Life of Saiki K. nails the exact premise of an overpowered esper who just wants a normal, quiet life. Each captures the core of what makes Mob Psycho 100 tick.

What is the best anime like Mob Psycho?

For the closest match, One Punch Man and The Disastrous Life of Saiki K. lead the pack with their overpowered-esper, deadpan-comedy energy. If what you loved most was Mob's heart, the best anime like Mob Psycho 100 are Hinamatsuri and Ranking of Kings, one for the found-family warmth, the other for its innocent emotional core.

What anime has a quiet, overpowered protagonist like Mob?

The closest is Saiki K., whose lead is an immensely powerful esper who downplays his gifts and wants a peaceful life, just like Mob. Mash in Mashle shares the same deadpan, humble, bowl-cut energy, and Saitama in One Punch Man carries Mob's character-first spirit even as his story goes louder.

Is Mob Psycho 100 getting a Season 4?

No. Mob Psycho 100 is a complete trilogy, with its three seasons airing between 2016 and 2022 and the manga long finished. The story reaches a definitive, satisfying ending, so a fourth season is not planned.

Where can you watch anime like Mob Psycho 100?

Most of these stream on Crunchyroll, including Mob Psycho 100 itself, with The Disastrous Life of Saiki K. also available on Prime Video. Streaming rights shift by region and over time, so double-check what is available where you are before you settle in.
