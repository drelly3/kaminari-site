Chainsaw Man has 0 filler episodes out of 12. Every single episode of the TV anime is adapted from Tatsuki Fujimoto's manga, covering chapters 1 through 38 without a frame of padding. For a show this short, the filler question answers itself before you finish asking it.

But the Chainsaw Man filler list people actually need goes beyond the original 12 episodes. The Reze Arc movie (released September 2025) isn't a standalone side story. It's the direct continuation, adapting chapters 38 through 52. If you watch Season 1 and skip the movie, you'll be lost when Season 2 arrives. And Season 2 is coming: MAPPA officially announced the Assassins Arc at Jump Festa 2026 and revealed a new trailer at their 15th Anniversary event in June 2026, alongside a tease for a mystery new Chainsaw Man project.

The franchise is accelerating, and the Reze Arc movie's $174 million worldwide gross with near-universal critical praise confirms this isn't a series cooling down. Here's the complete guide to everything that exists, everything coming next, and the watch order that ties it all together.

Chainsaw Man filler doesn't exist for the same reason most modern seasonal anime stays clean: MAPPA had years of completed manga to draw from and no pressure to pad a weekly broadcast schedule. Secretly Peak: Chainsaw Man Moments Worth Defending

Every Chainsaw Man filler list can tell you there's nothing to skip in 12 episodes. These three picks are the moments that deserve more attention than they get.

Episode 8: "Gunfire"

The Eternity Devil arc climax. Denji pulls the cord and goes full chainsaw for the first time in a sustained, extended fight. The animation is relentless, the pacing refuses to let you catch your breath, and it's the moment the show proves it can deliver on its premise's absurd potential. The hotel fight is Chainsaw Man's thesis statement compressed into 23 minutes: crude, violent, and somehow exhilarating in a way that shouldn't work but absolutely does.

Episode 12: "Katana vs. Chainsaw"

The Season 1 finale. The rooftop fight is the animation peak of the first season, and the emotional weight of everything that happened to Himeno, Aki, and Denji lands in a way the early episodes only hinted was possible. The show doesn't end on a resolution. It ends on a launch pad, and the momentum carries straight into the Reze Arc. Fans who watched this episode in 2022 waited three years for the next chapter. It was worth it.

The Reze Arc Movie

The strongest piece of Chainsaw Man content released so far. What could have been a simple "villain of the arc" story becomes something genuinely moving. Reze is the first character in the series who makes Denji feel like a real person rather than a walking appetite, and the tragedy of their connection gives the franchise an emotional register it hadn't fully committed to before. The fight sequences benefit from the theatrical budget, and the ending hits harder than anything in Season 1. Fans who only watched the TV anime are missing the best the franchise has produced.

FAQ

Does Chainsaw Man have filler?

No. Chainsaw Man has a 0% filler rate across all 12 TV episodes. Every episode is adapted directly from Tatsuki Fujimoto's manga. The Reze Arc movie is also fully canon.

Is the Chainsaw Man Reze Arc movie canon?

Yes. The Reze Arc movie adapts chapters 38-52 of the manga and is the direct continuation of Season 1. It is essential viewing before Season 2 (Assassins Arc) begins.

How many episodes of Chainsaw Man are there?

12 TV episodes in Season 1, plus the Reze Arc theatrical film. Season 2 (Assassins Arc) has been announced but has no confirmed episode count or release date yet.

What order should I watch Chainsaw Man?

Season 1 (Episodes 1-12), then the Reze Arc movie. That's the complete viewing order for all currently released content. Season 2 (Assassins Arc) will follow when it airs.

When does Chainsaw Man Season 2 come out?

No official release date has been announced. Season 2 (titled "Chainsaw Man: Assassins Arc") was confirmed at Jump Festa 2026 in December 2025, and MAPPA revealed a new trailer at their 15th Anniversary event in June 2026. Industry estimates suggest a 2027 premiere.
