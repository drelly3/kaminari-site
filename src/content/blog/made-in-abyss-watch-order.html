The recap films and the one movie you cannot skip

Three Made in Abyss movies exist, and they are not equal. Journey's Dawn (2019) and Wandering Twilight (2019) are compilation films that re-cut Season 1 into two features, with a few minutes of new animation each. They tell the same story you already get from the series, just compressed, so most viewers skip them. They only make sense as a faster substitute for Season 1 on a rewatch, never as something to watch after it.

Dawn of the Deep Soul (2020) is the opposite. It is a full sequel film with entirely new story, and it is the true bridge between the two seasons. It adapts the Idofront arc, delivers some of the franchise's heaviest emotional turns, and sets up the exact situation Season 2 opens in. Treat it as canon episodes in movie form, not as a bonus. The one rule that matters: watch Dawn of the Deep Soul before Season 2, every time.

FAQ

What is the Made in Abyss watch order?

The Made in Abyss watch order is Season 1, then the film Dawn of the Deep Soul, then Season 2 (The Golden City of the Scorching Sun). Those three are the full core story. The two compilation films, Journey's Dawn and Wandering Twilight, are recaps of Season 1 and can be skipped.

Do I need to watch the Made in Abyss movies?

Only one of them. Dawn of the Deep Soul is essential and must be watched between the two seasons. The other two films, Journey's Dawn and Wandering Twilight, are just recaps of Season 1, so they are optional and most viewers skip them.

Is Dawn of the Deep Soul necessary before Season 2?

Yes. Dawn of the Deep Soul is a canon sequel film, not a side story, and Season 2 picks up directly from its events. Skipping it means missing a major arc and starting Season 2 without the context it depends on. Always watch it first.

Can I watch the compilation films instead of Season 1?

You can, but only as a shortcut. Journey's Dawn and Wandering Twilight compress Season 1 into two films with minor new footage, so they work if you are short on time or rewatching. The series tells the story with far more room to breathe, so it is the better first experience.
