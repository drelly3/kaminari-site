Blonde is one of anime's most common hair colors, and also one of its most iconic. It lands on loud shonen heroes, elegant knights, chaotic goddesses, and terrifying villains alike. We rounded up twenty of the best blonde anime characters, male and female, and ranked them on the personalities that made them matter, not just the golden hair.

Anime blonde hair shows up in every genre and shade. You will spot bright yellow haired anime characters like Zenitsu, golden-braided icons like Edward Elric, and popular blonde anime characters who basically defined their whole series. Roughly half this list is female, which fits, since some of the most beloved blondes in anime are women.

Long-running shonen legends, modern scene-stealers, and a villain or two round out the roster, every pick earning its place on charisma and character. Let's get into it.

The 20 best blonde anime characters

Gathered from every corner of anime and ranked loosely by how unforgettable they are, not by shade. Heroes, icons, and a couple of villains, all sharing one very golden trait.

1. Naruto Uzumaki

Anime: Naruto

Naruto's spiky blonde hair is the face of shonen itself. The loudmouth orphan who dreamed of being Hokage turned relentless optimism into an actual superpower, dragging an entire world toward peace through sheer refusal to give up on anyone. Few characters have inspired more fans to keep going.

2. Lucy Heartfilia

Anime: Fairy Tail

Lucy is the heart the whole Fairy Tail crew orbits. A celestial mage who fights alongside her summoned spirits like family, she is kind, determined, and braver than she gives herself credit for. Her warmth and loyalty are a big part of why the guild feels like home.

3. Edward Elric

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist

Ed is a genius, a hothead, and a kid carrying the weight of one terrible mistake. His golden braid and short temper are iconic, but it is his fierce devotion to his brother and his refusal to sacrifice a single life that make him unforgettable. One of anime's best-written protagonists, blonde or otherwise.

4. Usagi Tsukino

Anime: Sailor Moon

Usagi is the blueprint nearly every blonde heroine after her borrowed from. A crybaby who would rather nap than fight, she still carries the whole world on her shoulders and protects her friends with a heart of pure gold. Sailor Moon proved a gentle, silly girl could be the strongest one in the room.

5. Sanji

Anime: One Piece

Sanji is all sharp suits, sharper kicks, and a soft spot a mile wide. The Straw Hats' cook fights with his legs to keep his hands safe for cooking, never raises them against a woman, and would starve before letting a crewmate go hungry. Chivalry and firepower in one blonde package.

6. Saber

Anime: Fate/stay night

Saber carries herself like the king she once was. Noble, disciplined, and bound by an unbending code of honor, she commands every scene she is in without ever raising her voice. Behind the regal calm is a leader haunted by the weight of her choices. Pure presence.

7. Winry Rockbell

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist

Winry is the mechanic who keeps Ed standing, literally. Brilliant with automail and fierce about the people she loves, she anchors Fullmetal Alchemist with warmth and a wrench that doubles as a weapon. Her strength is the quiet, steady kind that holds everyone else together.

8. Zenitsu Agatsuma

Anime: Demon Slayer

Zenitsu spends most of Demon Slayer screaming, crying, or both, which makes it all the sweeter when his bright yellow hair flashes and he cuts down a demon in his sleep. Under the cowardice is a loyal, big-hearted kid who fights hardest for the people he loves. Yellow-haired chaos with a spine of steel.

9. Marin Kitagawa

Anime: My Dress-Up Darling

Marin is sunshine given a personality. An outgoing cosplayer who wears her passions with zero shame, she coaxes the withdrawn Gojo out of his shell through pure enthusiasm and genuine kindness. Her confidence and warmth made her an instant modern favorite.

10. Kurapika

Anime: Hunter x Hunter

Kurapika is calm on the surface and a storm underneath. The last of his clan, he channels his grief into an ice-cold quest for justice that costs him more and more of himself along the way. His blonde hair and gentle manner hide one of anime's most driven, tragic hearts.

11. Historia Reiss

Anime: Attack on Titan

Historia spends half of Attack on Titan pretending to be someone else, and the other half becoming who she really is. Underneath the fake smile is a girl with a spine of iron who decides to define herself on her own terms. Her quiet transformation into a real leader is one of the show's best.

12. Misa Amane

Anime: Death Note

Misa is often the first blonde many fans picture, and for good reason. Devoted to the point of danger and driven entirely by love, she throws herself into Light's scheme with a reckless loyalty that reshapes the whole story. Chaotic, tragic, and impossible to ignore.

13. Loid Forger

Anime: Spy x Family

Loid is the master spy pretending to be a normal dad, and slowly, hilariously, becoming one for real. Cool, competent, and quietly overwhelmed by the fake family he grows to actually love, he anchors Spy x Family with equal parts action and heart. The blonde super-dad we all want.

14. Meliodas

Anime: The Seven Deadly Sins

Meliodas looks like a harmless blonde kid and is anything but. The Dragon Sin of Wrath hides centuries of power and pain behind a lazy grin, fiercely protective of the people he loves and terrifying when they are threatened. Deceptively soft on the surface, ancient underneath.

15. Kento Nanami

Anime: Jujutsu Kaisen

Nanami brought exhausted-office-worker energy to Jujutsu Kaisen and fans adored him for it. His dirty blonde hair and no-nonsense professionalism hide a deeply principled man who treats being a decent adult as its own kind of heroism. The mentor everyone quietly wishes they had.

16. Darkness

Anime: KonoSuba

Darkness is a noble crusader who is also a complete disaster, which is exactly the KonoSuba way. Genuinely brave and devoted to her ideals, she throws herself into danger with a conviction the rest of the party can only envy. Chaotic, loyal, and far more capable than she ever lets on.

17. Tsunade

Anime: Naruto

Tsunade is the legendary Sannin who became Hokage, and one of the strongest women in anime. Beneath the gambling habit and the temper is a healer carrying enormous grief who still chooses to protect her village with everything she has. Power and heartbreak in equal measure.

18. Katsuki Bakugo

Anime: My Hero Academia

Bakugo starts as pure explosive rage and slowly, grudgingly, grows into a real hero. His ash-blonde spikes match a personality that runs permanently hot, but underneath the fury is a fierce competitor who genuinely wants to be the best for the right reasons. One of anime's best-earned redemptions.

19. Violet Evergarden

Anime: Violet Evergarden

Violet begins as a soldier who barely understands her own feelings and grows into someone who helps others put theirs into words. Her golden hair frames a quiet, careful soul learning what love actually means, one letter at a time. One of anime's most quietly devastating arcs.

20. Tanya von Degurechaff

Anime: The Saga of Tanya the Evil

Tanya is what happens when a ruthless salaryman gets reincarnated as a small blonde girl and handed command of a nation's military. Cold, calculating, and gleefully willing to do terrible things in the name of efficiency, she is one of anime's most memorable blonde villains. Terrifying in the most entertaining way possible.

Honorable mentions

A few more blondes worth a shout: Riza Hawkeye keeping Mustang alive in Fullmetal Alchemist, Armin outthinking everyone in Attack on Titan, Dio Brando chewing scenery as JoJo's most iconic villain, and Tamaki Suou charming all of Ouran High School Host Club. Any one of them could headline a list of their own.

The gold standard

Naruto's spiky yellow, Saber's regal gold, Tanya's villainous platinum: blonde anime characters have given us some of the medium's most iconic heroes, sweethearts, and villains. The color might catch your eye first, but it is the personalities, Lucy's warmth, Ed's fire, Tanya's menace, that keep these characters at the top of every list. Pick a favorite and you are really picking a whole story.

FAQs

Who are the best female blonde anime characters?

Some of the most beloved female blondes in anime are Lucy Heartfilia, Sailor Moon, Saber, Winry Rockbell, and Tsunade. They range from gentle sweethearts to legendary warriors, proving the color fits every kind of heroine.

Who are the most popular blonde anime characters?

Naruto Uzumaki and Edward Elric usually top the list of popular blondes, with Sanji, Kurapika, and Lucy close behind. These are the blondes most fans picture the moment the topic comes up.

What shades of blonde appear in anime?

Anime blonde hair covers a huge range. You get golden tones on characters like Edward Elric, dirty blonde anime characters like Kento Nanami, platinum blonde and ash shades on the cooler types, and the occasional strawberry blonde or bleached look. If it lands anywhere near gold, anime has drawn it.

Which anime characters have short or long blonde hair?

For anime characters with short blonde hair, Naruto, Bakugo, and Nanami lead the way. For anime characters with long blonde hair, Lucy Heartfilia, Saber, and Tsunade stand out, wearing it long and unmistakable.

Are there blonde anime villains?

Plenty. Tanya von Degurechaff is a standout among blonde anime villains, ruthless and unforgettable, and Dio Brando from JoJo might be the most iconic blonde villain of all. Blonde hair suits a menacing smile as well as a heroic one.
