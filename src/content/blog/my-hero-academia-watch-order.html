Release order or best order (where the movies fit)?

Here is the only real fork, and it comes down to the four movies. Release order is the simplest path: watch the seasons as they aired and catch each film around the time it came out. It works fine, because none of the movies are required to follow the plot. The best order just nudges those films into the spots where they make the most sense in the story.

Our recommended placements are simple. Two Heroes fits neatly after Season 2, Heroes Rising lands best after Season 4, World Heroes' Mission slots in after Season 5, and You're Next works well after Season 7. Every one of these is a standalone side story with the main cast, canon-adjacent but self-contained, so the exact placement is forgiving. Watch them where we suggest, save them all for the end, or skip them entirely, and the core My Hero Academia watch order still holds together.

As for the OVAs and a strict chronological order, we would not sweat either on a first watch. The short OVAs are fun bonuses that sit between seasons, easy to save for a rewatch. A chronological cut barely differs from release order here, since MHA runs with almost no filler and adapts the manga closely, which we broke down episode by episode in the My Hero Academia filler list. For most people, seasons in order with the movies tucked in is the smoothest way through.

FAQ

What is the My Hero Academia watch order?

The My Hero Academia watch order is Seasons 1 through 8 in number order, with the four movies optional along the way: Two Heroes after Season 2, Heroes Rising after Season 4, World Heroes' Mission after Season 5, and You're Next after Season 7. The seasons carry the full story, so the films and OVAs are bonuses rather than requirements.

Do I need to watch the My Hero Academia movies?

No. Two Heroes, Heroes Rising, World Heroes' Mission, and You're Next are standalone side stories that do not change the main plot. They are well animated and fun, so they are worth folding in if you love the series, but you can skip them without missing anything essential.

Should I watch My Hero Academia in release order or chronological order?

Release order is the easy choice, and it barely differs from chronological here, since MHA adapts the manga closely with almost no filler. The only real adjustment a chronological watch makes is slotting the movies into their story spots, which is exactly what our recommended order above already does.

Should I watch My Hero Academia: Vigilantes?

Only if you want more. Vigilantes is a prequel spin-off about street-level heroes years before Deku reaches UA, and it is optional. It plays as a companion piece rather than a required chapter, so watch it whenever you are in the mood for a grittier side of hero society.

Where can I watch My Hero Academia?

Crunchyroll streams every season of My Hero Academia in both sub and dub, and the four movies are available there and on major digital storefronts in most regions. Availability changes by region, so check what is streaming near you before you start.
