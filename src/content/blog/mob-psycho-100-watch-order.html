FAQ

Is there any filler in Mob Psycho 100?

No. All three seasons adapt the source material straight through, so there are no filler episodes to skip. The only optional entries are the recap special and the OVA, and both sit outside the main story by design.

Do I need to watch the Reigen special before Season 2?

Not at all. It's a Season 1 recap narrated by Reigen, so a first-time viewer can jump from Season 1 straight into Season 2 without missing a thing. It's most fun as a refresher on a rewatch, and its ending nods to Season 2's arrival.

Are there any Mob Psycho 100 movies?

No theatrical movies exist. The Reigen recap special sometimes gets called a "TV movie" because of its hour-long runtime, but it's a recap, not a separate film, and nothing in the Mob Psycho 100 watch order requires it.

How many episodes is Mob Psycho 100 in total?

The three main seasons run 37 episodes (12, then 13, then 12). Add the recap special, the Company Outing OVA, and the six Mini shorts if you want the complete set of extras on top of that.

Where can I watch Mob Psycho 100 in order?

Crunchyroll carries all three seasons subbed and dubbed, which covers the full Mob Psycho watch order in one place. Availability can vary by region, so confirm what's streaming where you are before you start.
