😤 Welcome the fear

With anything meaningful in life, you will be faced with uncertainty

Will you welcome the fear and continue to move forward?

Every shonen anime character you ever respected has… so why not you?

⚔️ Win the small battles

Now, it doesn’t have to be all at once

It’s not like Luffy just woke up and started beating up Big Mom

Take your big dreams, and break them down into small habits

So small to the point that even on your worst days, you can still do them

From there, just focus on winning those little battles each day

For example, these big goals:

  • Run 3 marathons this year
  • Bench 225 lbs
  • Finish 10 books a month
  • Hit 100,000 subs on youtube
  • Build a $1 million business
  • Catch up to One Piece

Turn into these small habits:

  • Walk for 10 min
  • Open the gym door today
  • Read 1 page of my new book
  • Write the hook of my next youtube video
  • Spend 10 min on that business idea
  • Watch 1 One Piece episode

Of course, you can always level up these habits as you build consistency

Though at the end of the day

Stacking these little wins will ultimately lead you to the outcomes you want

👂 Listen to your gut

Whatever you choose to do, follow your gut

When met with fear, really just sit with it inside your body and listen

Listen for what feels alive behind the fear

Listen for what excites you

Even in the midst of uncertainty, that excitement will lead you to the life you so desire
