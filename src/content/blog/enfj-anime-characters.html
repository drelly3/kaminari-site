ENFJs, the Protagonists, are anime's inspiring hearts: the charismatic leaders, beloved mentors, and warm-hearted heroes who bring out the best in everyone around them and fight for the greater good without a second thought. Empathetic, driven, and gifted at reading exactly what people need, they rally whole casts behind a shared dream and lift the people beside them higher than they could climb alone. Anime is built on characters like these. We gathered nineteen of the most convincing ENFJ anime characters and the trait that seals each one.

The Protagonist runs on warm, outward-facing empathy paired with a clear vision of where everyone should end up, which is why ENFJs make such natural teachers and leaders. That focus on guiding the whole group is what separates them from the very similar ENFP, who chases personal what-ifs more than collective harmony. Charismatic, giving, and happiest when the people around them are thriving.

One note before we start: these are community typings, not creator-confirmed labels, and ENFJ sits right beside a couple of close cousins, so we kept this to the broadly agreed Protagonists at the top of the ENFJ anime canon. The quieter, behind-the-scenes Advocates get their own INFJ anime characters list if you want to compare. Here are the ENFJ anime characters who prove that the best leaders are the ones who make everyone else better.

The 19 most ENFJ anime characters, ranked

Each pick comes with the Protagonist trait that seals it: the gift for inspiring people, the mentor's instinct, or the vision that pulls a whole cast forward. These are the ENFJ anime characters fans name first, the ones who make everyone around them want to be better.

1. All Might

Anime: My Hero Academia

All Might is the Protagonist in its purest, most inspiring form. He tailors his entire life around one vision, to be the Symbol of Peace who unites a whole society in hope, and he lifts every person he meets toward their own greatness. Warm, optimistic, and endlessly giving even as it costs him everything, he mentors a quirkless boy into a hero simply by believing in him first. Our All Might Quotes collection carries that whole heart.

2. Tanjiro Kamado

Anime: Demon Slayer

Tanjiro leads with empathy so deep he mourns the demons he has to slay. Hyper-aware of everyone's feelings and driven by a bottomless need to protect, he connects with allies and enemies alike, seeing the human buried inside even his worst foes. That warmth married to unshakable resolve is the Protagonist at its most moving. Our Tanjiro Quotes collection has more of that gentle strength.

3. Jonathan Joestar

Anime: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

Jonathan is ENFJ hero material distilled: honorable, warm, and burning with the desire to make a difference. He tries to befriend even Dio before the vampire proves irredeemable, then dedicates his whole life, and ultimately gives it, to protecting the world from that evil. Noble to a fault and beloved by everyone around him, he set the template for what a JoJo protagonist should be. The Giver who gave everything.

4. Kyojuro Rengoku

Anime: Demon Slayer

Rengoku burns twice as bright as anyone around him, and he uses that fire entirely to lift others up. The Flame Hashira leads with booming positivity, mentors younger slayers on the spot, and faces death itself while telling the people he protects to set their hearts ablaze. His whole ethos is passing his strength forward. Our Demon Slayer Filler List keeps the run clean while you relive every second of it.

5. Vinsmoke Sanji

Anime: One Piece

Sanji runs on a code of care so strong it defines his whole life: feed anyone who is hungry, never raise a hand against a woman, and protect his crew with everything he has. Driven by deep interpersonal loyalty and a chivalrous read of right and wrong, he channels his fire into looking after the people he loves. That devotion to harmony and others' wellbeing is pure Protagonist. Our One Piece Quotes collection sails right alongside him.

6. Minato Namikaze

Anime: Naruto

Minato is a true leader who guides others with wisdom, warmth, and an almost effortless ability to connect. The Fourth Hokage inspires devotion through empathy rather than fear, carries the weight of his whole village on his shoulders, and gives his life without hesitation to protect its future. That mix of vision and selfless responsibility is the Protagonist as a beloved leader. Our Minato Quotes collection holds his best wisdom.

7. Fuegoleon Vermillion

Anime: Black Clover

Fuegoleon leads the Crimson Lions with a burning sense of honor and a genuine investment in every knight under him. He inspires loyalty through example, pushes his squad to grow into their best selves, and carries an unshakable vision for a fairer kingdom. Noble, disciplined, and deeply invested in the people he leads, he is the Protagonist as a captain worth following into fire. Command built on care rather than fear.

8. Isabella

Anime: The Promised Neverland

Isabella is the Protagonist turned quietly terrifying. As Mama of Grace Field, she reads every child's heart with uncanny precision and uses that emotional insight to control them, all in service of a vision she has convinced herself is right. Warm on the surface and calculating underneath, she weaponizes the exact gifts that make ENFJs beloved. Proof the type's charisma and foresight can build a nurturer or a jailer.

9. Mirio Togata

Anime: My Hero Academia

Mirio does whatever it takes to succeed so that he can lift everyone else with him. Inspiring, relentlessly positive, and gifted at spotting the potential in people who cannot see it in themselves, he wants to be a symbol of hope just like the hero he admires. He puts others first as naturally as breathing. The Protagonist as the big brother the whole class quietly wishes they had.

10. Emma

Anime: The Promised Neverland

Emma is the beating heart of Grace Field's escape, a leader who refuses to leave a single child behind no matter the cost. She rallies terrified kids with empathy and unshakable optimism, reads exactly what each of them needs, and pours herself into a vision of freedom for everyone. Her warmth is a strategy and a superpower at once. The Protagonist as a heroine who leads with pure, stubborn love.

11. Makima

Anime: Chainsaw Man

Makima is the Protagonist's gifts bent toward domination. She reads human emotion like an open book, manipulates bonds with surgical precision, and works relentlessly toward a grand, chilling vision for humanity's future. Every warm word is a lever, every kindness a calculation. She is one of anime's most unsettling villains precisely because she wields the type's charisma and foresight without a shred of its warmth. A dark mirror of the Giver.

12. Ai Hoshino

Anime: Oshi no Ko

Ai is an idol who learned to give the world exactly the love it wanted, even while figuring out how to feel it herself. Supportive, emotionally perceptive, and radiant on stage, she shapes everyone around her while chasing a vision of the performer she wants to become. Her warmth is real even when her circumstances force her to perform it. The Protagonist as a star whose light outlasts her.

13. Tooru Oikawa

Anime: Haikyuu!!

Oikawa's real gift is not his serve, it is his uncanny ability to draw one hundred percent out of every teammate. The setter reads each player's emotional and mental state, then pushes exactly the right button to lift their game. Charismatic, driven, and obsessed with elevating the people around him, he leads through connection as much as skill. The Protagonist as a captain who makes ordinary players extraordinary.

14. Nana Shimura

Anime: My Hero Academia

Nana mentored All Might into the Symbol of Peace, and her philosophy still echoes through the whole series: keep a smile on your face, because saving hearts matters as much as saving lives. Gentle, strong, and endlessly optimistic, she fought for justice and poured her belief into the next generation. She is the Protagonist as the teacher whose warmth shapes heroes long after she is gone.

15. Falco Grice

Anime: Attack on Titan

Falco is a rare thing in a story about cycles of hatred: a boy who sees past the tribe to the person. Deeply empathetic and quietly brave, he understands the feelings on both sides of a war designed to make that impossible, and he acts on that understanding at real risk to himself. That intuitive compassion is the Protagonist's whole heart. Proof that empathy can be its own quiet form of heroism.

16. Makoto Naegi

Anime: Danganronpa

Naegi is the Ultimate Hope, and he earns the title by uniting fractured, despairing people through sheer steadfast optimism. He validates everyone's feelings, refuses to give up on even the most broken person in the room, and inspires collective belief when all seems lost. His superpower is making other people hope again. The Protagonist as an ordinary boy whose kindness turns out to be the strongest force in the building.

17. Teruhashi Kokomi

Anime: Saiki K

Teruhashi is adored by everyone she meets, and she genuinely cares about earning that adoration by being good to people. Perceptive, poised, and quietly determined, she reads a room instantly and shapes it toward warmth, even as the series pokes fun at how hard she works at it. Beneath the everyone-loves-her image is a real desire to connect. The Protagonist as the most beloved girl in school, doing her homework on kindness.

18. Nozomi Tojo

Anime: Love Live!

Nozomi is the spiritual glue of her idol group, the one quietly nudging everyone toward each other and toward their best selves. Perceptive and nurturing, she reads what her friends need before they say it and orchestrates the little moments that hold the team together. Her care runs deep beneath the teasing and the tarot cards. The Protagonist as the mother hen who builds a family out of a friend group.

19. Hitohito Tadano

Anime: Komi Can't Communicate

Tadano has an almost telepathic read on group dynamics and social atmospheres, and he uses it entirely to support the people around him. Perceptive, patient, and quietly selfless, he helps a girl with severe social anxiety chase her dream of making friends, reading every room so she does not have to. His whole arc is using empathy to lift someone else up. The Protagonist as the most quietly considerate boy in class.

Honorable mentions

A few names hover at the edges. Monkey D. Luffy and Tamaki Suoh both radiate leader energy, but their heads live in dreams and personal what-ifs more than group harmony, which is why we filed them as Campaigners over on the ENFP list. On the genuine Protagonist bench, Might Guy pours his whole heart into his students in Naruto, Hashirama Senju unites a warring world in the same series, and Victor Nikiforov coaches with pure warmth in Yuri on Ice. All of them lead by lifting others up.

Why the Protagonists make everyone around them better

Line them up and the pattern is unmistakable: ENFJ anime characters do not just win their own battles, they raise everyone standing beside them. All Might's belief, Tanjiro's empathy, Rengoku's fire, it all runs on the same engine, a heart that reads what people need and a will to give it to them no matter the cost. That is why the Protagonist so often turns out to be the character a whole cast is quietly built around, the one who makes the people near them braver, kinder, and stronger.

FAQs

What is an ENFJ personality type?

ENFJ stands for Extroverted, Intuitive, Feeling, and Judging, nicknamed the Protagonist, Teacher, or Giver. ENFJs are charismatic, empathetic leaders who read people effortlessly, inspire everyone around them, and pour themselves into helping others reach their potential. In fiction the type lands on beloved mentors, warm-hearted heroes, and the leaders a whole cast rallies behind.

Which anime characters are ENFJ?

The clearest ENFJ anime characters include All Might, Tanjiro Kamado, Jonathan Joestar, Kyojuro Rengoku, and Vinsmoke Sanji. Each one leads with empathy, inspires the people around them, and fights for the greater good, which is the Protagonist in a nutshell.

Who is the most ENFJ anime character?

All Might from My Hero Academia and Tanjiro Kamado from Demon Slayer top most fan rankings. All Might builds his whole life around being a symbol of hope, while Tanjiro connects with even his enemies through pure empathy, and between them they capture the Protagonist's whole heart.

Are these ENFJ-T or ENFJ-A anime characters?

Both show up. The confident, self-assured ones like All Might and Rengoku sit near the assertive ENFJ-A end, while the more anxious, self-doubting ENFJ-T anime characters, Tanjiro and Emma among them, carry the worry that comes with feeling responsible for everyone around them.

What is the difference between ENFJ and ENFP anime characters?

Both are warm, idealistic extroverts, but their focus differs. An ENFJ reads the room and guides the whole group toward a shared vision, leading with structured empathy, while an ENFP chases personal possibilities and inspires people one dreamer at a time.
