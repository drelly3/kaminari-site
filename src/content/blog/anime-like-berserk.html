Still carrying the weight of Guts's endless struggle after watching the Black Swordsman cut through demons and despair alike? Plenty of anime like Berserk deliver the same mix: a dark, brutally violent fantasy world, a brooding warrior hardened by trauma, grotesque monsters and body horror, and the iron-willed refusal to stop fighting no matter how bleak it gets. No show fully replaces it, but the closest ones hit that same grim, visceral nerve.

Berserk's anime has never quite captured the manga, from the cult-classic 1997 series to the Golden Age films to the divisive later adaptations, and after Kentaro Miura's passing in 2021 the story now continues in print under his longtime collaborators. For fans, that leaves a real hunger for more of its dark-fantasy intensity on screen.

The anime most like Berserk, ranked

Here are the anime like Berserk that genuinely share its build, ranked from the closest match to the loosest. The Similarity Rating is our read on how near each one gets to Berserk specifically, not how good the show is overall, and we kept the stars to whole numbers. The build we are measuring against is simple: a dark, brutally violent seinen fantasy epic with a brooding, tortured warrior fighting demons and fate in a grimdark world, carried by trauma, revenge, perseverance, body horror, and visceral combat.

1. Claymore

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Prime Video

Synopsis: In a medieval world terrorized by shapeshifting, man-eating demons called Yoma, an organization fields half-human, half-demon warrior women, the Claymores, who hunt them with enormous swords. Clare, driven by a personal vendetta, walks a razor's edge between her humanity and the monster inside her. The violence is graphic and the world is bleak. It is dark fantasy at its most uncompromising.

Of everything here, Claymore is the undisputed closest sibling to Berserk. It is a grim, European-inspired medieval dark fantasy where mutated half-human warriors wield massive greatswords to hunt grotesque, flesh-eating abominations called Yoma, and Clare's quiet, trauma-driven quest for vengeance mirrors Guts closely. The body horror, the bleak world, the ever-present threat of losing one's humanity, and the lone-warrior-with-a-huge-sword image all echo Berserk directly. For the single most Berserk-like anime, start here.

2. Vinland Saga

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Thorfinn grows up among Viking raiders, consumed by a need to avenge his father, until years of bloodshed force him to reckon with what revenge has cost him. The first season is a brutal historical war epic; the second turns inward into something quieter and profound. The violence is unflinching and the character work is extraordinary. It is one of the finest anime of its era.

Vinland Saga is the closest match to Berserk's Golden Age arc. There are no demons or magic here, but it captures the harsh reality of historical warfare, mercenary bands selling their blades to warring kings, and a protagonist in Thorfinn consumed by blinding vengeance, trauma, and the hollow nature of violence. Thorfinn and Guts are spiritual twins, both vengeance-driven warriors shaped by loss and surrounded by death, slowly wrestling with whether the struggle means anything. The grounded, no-fantasy setting is the one real difference, but for Berserk's most acclaimed arc and its emotional core, nothing comes closer.

3. Kingdom

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A war orphan dreams of becoming a great general and rising from nothing in the brutal, blood-soaked era of China's Warring States. Through sheer will and talent he climbs the military ranks amid massive, grounded battles and ruthless political scheming. The scope is enormous and the ambition is electric. It is one of the great historical war epics in anime.

Kingdom directly mirrors the large-scale military campaigns, the strategic field warfare, and the brother-in-arms brotherhood of the Band of the Hawk. It follows ordinary warriors rising through the ranks of bloody, historical battlefield conflicts in ancient China, driven by ambition and the dream of greatness, which is the exact engine behind Griffith's mercenary company and its climb. The grounded, blood-soaked battles and the lowborn-fighter-clawing-upward theme carry a strong Berserk flavor on its political and militaristic side. There is no fantasy or demons, which keeps it a notch down, but for the Golden Age's war-and-ambition half, it is a close fit.

4. Goblin Slayer

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: In a classic fantasy world of adventurers and quests, one grim man cares about only one thing: exterminating goblins, the small monsters everyone else dismisses as beneath notice. Driven by a childhood trauma, he approaches the task with cold, methodical brutality. The first episode is notoriously harsh. It is a dark deconstruction of the cozy fantasy-adventure genre.

Goblin Slayer drops you into a grim, low-fantasy world where adventurer parties face grotesque cruelty and slaughter from monstrous beasts, and its battered, armored protagonist shares Guts's stoic hyper-fixation on exterminating a specific threat with pragmatic, unglamorous brutality. The willingness to show how ugly and vicious that world really is, rather than romanticizing the adventure, is very Berserk, and the trauma driving his one-track crusade rhymes with the Black Swordsman. It is smaller and more repetitive in scope, which keeps it mid-list, but the battered-warrior-on-a-brutal-mission build fits well.

5. Fate/Zero

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Seven mages summon seven heroic spirits to fight a secret, deadly war for an omnipotent wish-granting relic. Alliances shift, ideals shatter, and the body count mounts as the war grows crueler than any of them imagined. It is a dark, gorgeously animated tragedy about ambition and the cost of winning. It is a high point of the Fate franchise.

Fate/Zero is a bleak, philosophical battle royale that shares Berserk's tragic exploration of ideology, betrayal, and sacrifice. Kiritsugu Emiya's cold utilitarianism and moral degradation strongly echo Griffith's willingness to sacrifice everything, even those closest to him, for his vision. The grim tone, the high-stakes bloodshed, and the way noble ideals collapse into horror carry a Berserk-like weight, especially in its darkest thematic undercurrents. There is no lone brooding swordsman or dark-fantasy demon world, which keeps it lower, but the tragic-ideology-and-betrayal build is a strong fit.

6. Attack on Titan

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Humanity huddles behind enormous walls, hiding from the Titans, giant humanoid monsters that devour people for no clear reason. When the walls are breached, young Eren and his friends join the brutal fight for survival, uncovering a conspiracy that reframes everything. The deaths are constant and the despair is real. It builds to one of the most ambitious, divisive endings in anime.

Attack on Titan shares Berserk's overwhelming sense of human helplessness against grotesque, cannibalistic titans, combined with military discipline, shocking betrayals, and an escalating dive into cosmic, apocalyptic stakes. Both pile trauma on their protagonists until rage and revenge consume them, both refuse to spare anyone, and both treat survival as a brutal, bloody struggle against an uncaring world. It is not medieval sword-and-sorcery, so the texture differs, but the relentless brutality and the tragic, fate-haunted weight are pure Berserk. 7. Hell's Paradise

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A legendary ninja assassin on death row is offered a pardon if he can retrieve an elixir of immortality from a mysterious, deadly island. He and a group of condemned criminals, each shadowed by an executioner, land on a paradise that turns out to be a nightmare of grotesque monsters and body horror. The deaths come fast and strange. It is a gorgeous, gruesome survival gauntlet.

Hell's Paradise sends feudal samurai and death-row convicts to a nightmarish, supernatural island teeming with grotesque, body-horror deities and unnatural monsters, capturing Berserk's visceral horror and bloody desperation. The island's otherworldly transformations and horrors echo Berserk's Apostles, and the grim, death-soaked quest carries the same bleak intensity. It follows a tighter, more conventional shonen-action structure than Berserk's sprawling despair, which keeps it mid-list, but the stoic-warrior-versus-grotesque-monsters build is a strong match.

8. Drifters

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Famous warriors from across human history are pulled into a strange fantasy world and thrown into its wars, among them a cunning samurai who fights alongside and against other legendary figures. From the creator of Hellsing, it blends brutal medieval-fantasy battle with dark humor. The action is bloody and stylish. It is a violent, swaggering dark-fantasy romp.

Created by Kouta Hirano of Hellsing, Drifters summons brutal historical warriors, samurai, warlords, and gunslingers, into a grim dark-fantasy world to wage tactical, ultra-violent warfare. It shares Berserk's bloody swordsmanship and its taste for gore and dark spectacle, and the warriors-versus-monsters carnage echoes its endless battles. The difference is attitude: Drifters leans into dark, chaotic comedy and edge where Berserk is bleakly serious, which keeps it lower, but the bloody-dark-fantasy-war build is a genuine match.

9. Hellsing Ultimate

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll, Prime Video

Synopsis: The Hellsing Organization hunts the monsters that prey on England, and its ultimate weapon is Alucard, a staggeringly powerful vampire who serves the very humans his kind devours. When a fascist remnant unleashes an army of the undead, the carnage is total. The gore is extreme and the style is relentless. It is ten episodes of gothic, blood-drenched excess.

Hellsing Ultimate is a masterpiece of gothic gore and slaughter, and it matches the unrelenting bloodbath and monstrous creature violence of Berserk better than almost anything. The one thing that sets it apart is its hero: Alucard is an invincible, swaggering vampire king, the exact opposite of Guts, who is a fragile mortal barely surviving every fight through agony and grit. That inversion is why it sits lower despite the shared brutality, but for raw violent spectacle and gothic dread, it is a strong fit.

10. Chainsaw Man

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Denji, a destitute teenager crushed by debt, merges with his chainsaw devil-dog to become a devil-hunting weapon for a shadowy agency. What follows is a blood-drenched, chaotic ride through a world where devils are born from human fears. The violence is visceral and the tone lurches from bleak to absurd. It is one of the most talked-about modern anime.

Chainsaw Man features nightmarish, abstract devil designs, extreme body horror, and crushing emotional loss, all of which share real DNA with Berserk's darkness. The grotesque monster designs and the brutal, out-of-nowhere deaths echo its cruelty. The difference is framing: it is an urban, modern dark comedy and character study rather than a sweeping medieval epic, which keeps it lower, but the body-horror-and-devils build is a solid fit. 11. Jujutsu Kaisen

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Yuji Itadori is pulled into a hidden war against curses, the malevolent monsters born from human negativity, after swallowing a cursed object to protect his friends. He trains among sorcerers as the threats escalate and the body count climbs. The fights are spectacular and the losses are brutal. It is one of the biggest modern dark-action series.

Jujutsu Kaisen ranks last here because it has the least in common with Berserk's build. The Shibuya arc brings real tragedy and body-horror curses, and the grotesque curse designs and merciless losses carry a flash of Berserk's cruelty. But it remains a fast-paced, modern urban sorcery shonen centered on high-IQ energy battles and domain expansions, a long way from a grimdark medieval warrior's slow, bloody struggle. The dark-monsters-and-brutal-loss elements are the match, which keeps it on the list, but only just. Our Jujutsu Kaisen filler list marks what matters.

A couple that get recommended but watch differently

Search around for anime similar to Berserk and two names come up constantly that Berserk fans swear by, but that are not on the main list for a reason. They are still worth seeking out.

Vagabond is the closest thing to Berserk in spirit, Takehiko Inoue's brutal, beautiful epic about the swordsman Miyamoto Musashi and the lonely, violent path of mastery. The catch is that it has no anime adaptation at all, so it lives only as a manga, which is why it cannot sit on a watchlist. Devilman Crybaby nails the demons, the apocalyptic bleakness, and the shocking brutality better than almost anything, a genuine dark-fantasy match, but it is a Netflix exclusive, so depending on your region it may not be available the way the picks above are. Both are essential for Berserk fans, just with those asterisks attached.

FAQs

Is there an anime exactly like Berserk?

Not a perfect twin, but two come very close. Claymore is the closest, with a lone warrior wielding a massive greatsword against grotesque demons in a grimdark medieval world, right down to the body horror and the monster-within struggle. Vinland Saga is right behind it as the best match for Berserk's acclaimed Golden Age arc, trading demons for the brutal reality of mercenary warfare and a vengeance-consumed hero. Each captures the core of what makes Berserk tick.

What is the best dark anime like Berserk?

For sheer darkness and brutality, Hellsing Ultimate, Hell's Paradise, and Claymore are the top picks, each drenched in gore and grotesque monsters. If you want a dark anime like Berserk built on despair and body horror rather than sword-and-sorcery, Attack on Titan is the one to beat.

What anime is like the 1997 Berserk anime?

The 1997 series is remembered for its Golden Age arc: grounded medieval politics, a mercenary band's rise, and a slow slide into horror. For that specific flavor, Vinland Saga and Kingdom capture the mercenary warfare and the ambitious, blood-soaked military rise that drives the Band of the Hawk, while Claymore and Goblin Slayer deliver the grimdark-medieval setting.

Is Berserk getting a new anime?

No new adaptation has been confirmed. Berserk's existing anime, the 1997 series, the 2012 to 2013 Golden Age film trilogy, and the 2016 to 2017 series, cover only part of the story, and the manga has continued in print under Miura's collaborators since his death in 2021. Any future adaptation remains speculation for now.

Where can you watch anime like Berserk?

Most of these stream on Crunchyroll, with Drifters and Hellsing Ultimate also available on Prime Video. Streaming rights shift by region and over time, so double-check what is available where you are before you settle in.
