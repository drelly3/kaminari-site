Izuku Midoriya is the kid who kept running long after everyone, his own best friend included, told him there was no point. Born without a quirk in a world where almost everybody has one, he spent his first fourteen years being labeled the useless one, the boy who could dream about heroes but never be one. Then he got picked anyway, and the whole of My Hero Academia becomes the story of him earning, inch by painful inch, a future nobody thought he was allowed to have. His best lines are the receipts for that climb.

I have gathered the Deku quotes that stay with me most, sorted by the kind of day you might need them on. Some are pure fuel for when you feel behind before you have even started. Some are the quieter Deku lines about empathy that show you why this particular hero hits different. If you have ever been the person counted out early and decided to keep going regardless, a lot of these are going to feel like they were meant for you.

Deku quotes for when everyone else got a head start

Deku begins the race a lap behind everyone. His origin is not a tragic backstory so much as a slow, grinding disadvantage, and these lines come from a kid learning to run anyway.

"All men are not created equal. This was the reality I learned about society at the tender age of four."

He opens the whole series with this, and it is bleaker than you expect from a shonen hero. Four years old is when Deku learns the world already sorted him into the losing pile, and rather than pretend otherwise, he names it out loud. The line lands because so many of us learned some version of it young too, that the playing field was never level. What he does with that knowledge, refusing to let it be the end of his story, is the entire point.

"If I match my pace with everyone else, then I'll never be number one."

Once he finally has a shot, Deku refuses to settle for keeping up. He knows that starting behind means he has to move differently, push harder, take the risks the naturally gifted can afford to skip. It is not arrogance, it is math. Anyone who has had to work twice as hard just to reach the same starting line understands why he cannot allow himself an average pace.

Deku quotes for when you want to quit but keep moving anyway

The heart of Deku is that he keeps his promises to himself even when his body is literally breaking. He shatters his own bones to throw a single punch and gets back up to do it again. These are the lines for the days the tank is empty and you move regardless.

"One For All wasn't something I was born with. That means I have to earn every percent."

Even the power handed to him becomes something he has to deserve. Deku never treats his gift as a shortcut, because he knows better than anyone that inheriting strength and actually controlling it are worlds apart. Every percent of that power costs him sweat and broken fingers. For anyone who has been handed an opportunity and felt the weight of proving they belonged in it, that sentence is a quiet gut-check.

"Don't worry about what other people think. Hold your head up high and plunge forward."

Coming from the boy who spent years crushed under exactly what other people thought of him, this is hard-won advice, not a platitude. Deku earned the right to say it by surviving the opposite. The school he attends takes its motto, going beyond your limit, more seriously than most, and Deku takes it more literally than anyone, because going beyond was the only way a quirkless kid ever got through the front gate. When your circumstances give you no margin, plunging forward stops being brave and starts being the only move you have.

Deku lines for when you can't walk past someone hurting

Here is what actually separates Deku from every other shonen lead. His real superpower was never One For All. It is that he cannot look at someone in pain, hero or villain, and keep walking.

"I want to be a hero who saves people with a smile."

He says it through tears, early on, describing the exact image that made him fall in love with heroism in the first place: someone who shows up in the worst moment and makes it feel survivable. It is a child's dream, and Deku spends the series proving it is also the hardest, bravest job there is. The people who have been saved by a stranger's steadiness in an awful moment know why that smile matters so much.

"Shigaraki is crying. I saw it."

Nothing sets Deku apart from the whole decade of protagonists around him like this line. In the middle of fighting the person trying to destroy everything he loves, Deku looks past the monster and sees the hurt child underneath, and he refuses to un-see it even when ignoring it would be safer and easier. Choosing empathy for the person who least deserves it is the most Deku thing imaginable, and it is why his heroism reads as real rather than performed.

"When a hero needs saving, someone else becomes the hero."

Here is the thesis of the entire series in one breath. Heroism, to Deku, is not a title you hold, it is a torch that passes to whoever is brave enough to pick it up next. He believes anyone can be the one who steps in, which is a genuinely radical thing for the strongest kid in the room to insist on. These are the Deku quotes that reframe the whole genre, because they hand the cape to the reader.

Deku quotes for when you're chasing someone better than you

Deku's rivalry with Bakugo is the friction that sharpens him for years. He does not want to tear his rival down, he wants to catch up to him, and that hunger is its own kind of fuel.

"You're the best. That's why I want to defeat you!"

Most rivals trash-talk. Deku does the opposite, telling Bakugo he wants to beat him precisely because he respects him that much. Growth, for Deku, means running straight at the strongest person in the room instead of hiding from them. If you have ever measured yourself against someone you admire and let it drive you rather than crush you, this is the mindset that makes it work.

By the end, even the name itself flips. "Deku" started as Bakugo's insult, a jab meaning useless, until a friend pointed out it could just as easily sound like "you can do it." Midoriya took the cruelest thing anyone ever called him and turned it into his hero name, a daily reminder that the label people give you does not get to decide what it means. Reclaiming that word is the quietest and most powerful thing he ever does.

Why "Deku" became a promise instead of an insult

Strip away One For All and the broken bones and the flashy Smash attacks, and what makes Izuku Midoriya unforgettable is that he was never supposed to make it, and he did, without ever losing the soft heart that made people underestimate him in the first place. He proves that being counted out early is not a verdict, that borrowed strength still has to be earned, and that empathy is a power worth more than any quirk. The best Deku quotes all circle the same stubborn hope: the starting line does not decide the finish, and the name they meant as an insult can become the thing you promise yourself every single morning. You can do it.

Frequently asked questions

What is Deku's most famous quote?

His tearful "I want to be a hero who saves people with a smile" is the one fans hold closest, since it captures why he wanted this life before he had any power to back it up. His opening narration, "all men are not created equal," runs a close second for people who love how honestly the series starts. Both say a lot about a kid who saw the world clearly and chose hope anyway.

What does "Deku" mean?

It starts as an insult. Bakugo coins it to mean useless or hopeless, a jab at Midoriya's lack of a quirk. Later his friend Uraraka points out that the same word can sound like "dekiru," meaning "you can do it," and Izuku decides to claim that version instead. He turns the cruelest nickname he was ever given into his hero name and a personal promise.

Did Deku say "I am here"?

Not originally. "I am here" is All Might's signature line, the reassurance the Symbol of Peace gives so people know they are safe. Deku only takes those words up much later, as he grows into becoming the next symbol himself. It is a torch being passed, so crediting it to Deku alone skips the whole meaning of the moment.

What is Deku's quirk?

For most of the series it is One For All, a power passed down through a line of heroes and gifted to him by All Might, which stockpiles raw strength across generations. Deku was born completely quirkless, which is what makes him such an outlier, and part of his arc is learning to control a power his body was never built to hold. He later gains access to several additional quirks stored within One For All.

What does PLUS ULTRA mean?

It is the motto of U.A. High and, loosely, of the whole hero society: go beyond, push past your limit. All Might popularizes the phrase, but Deku ends up embodying it more literally than anyone, because for a quirkless kid, going beyond what should be possible was the only way in. It has become the series' unofficial rallying cry for fans facing their own limits.
