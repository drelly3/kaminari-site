Hate is not a single feeling in anime, and the most hated anime characters do not all earn it the same way. Some are written to be despised, and despising them means the writers did their job. Some are not evil at all, just grating enough to make you reach for the skip button. And some got buried by the fandom for doing exactly what their story needed.

The genuinely reviled ones sit up top, the monsters whose actions put them among the worst anime characters of all time. Lower down the picture gets more interesting, where a character's biggest crime is being annoying, or being the scapegoat a whole fanbase decided it needed. We judged every one of them on writing and behavior, never on looks, which is more than a lot of the online pile-ons can say.

A few of these are hard to read about, so we kept the focus on why the reaction happens rather than the grim specifics. Here are fifteen of the most hated anime characters of all time, and the honest reason each one lands where it does.

The 15 most hated anime characters, ranked

Ranked from the genuinely monstrous down to the merely divisive. These are the most hated anime characters fans bring up first, sorted by why the venom is pointed their way. The top reads like a rogues' gallery, so stick around for the ones who arguably deserve a defense.

1. Shou Tucker

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist

No conversation about anime's most despised ends without Shou Tucker, and it usually starts with him too. The State Alchemist did something to his own daughter Nina so cold and unforgivable that it became the moment countless viewers point to as the day the series broke them. We will spare the specifics, since the show does not, and it needs no help from us. What makes Tucker linger is how ordinary his cowardice looks right up until it does not. The young Elric brothers never fully recover, and neither do most fans. If Fullmetal's gut-punches marked you too, our Edward Elric Quotes collection sits with the aftermath.

2. Malty S. Melromarc

Anime: The Rising of the Shield Hero

Malty, also known as Myne, kicks off Shield Hero by falsely accusing the protagonist of a crime he never committed, torching his reputation before the story has properly begun. Everything after doubles down: lying, scheming, and hanging her victims out to dry with a smile. She is written to be infuriating, and the show leans into it so hard that watching her finally meet consequences became a fandom event. Effective, absolutely. Pleasant, not for a second.

3. Griffith

Anime: Berserk

Some characters earn hatred through a single unforgivable choice, and Griffith made his during the Eclipse. For most of Berserk he is charismatic, driven, and almost sympathetic, which is exactly what makes the betrayal land like a blade between the ribs. Fans do not hate Griffith because he is badly written. They hate him because he is one of the finest villains the medium ever produced, and that hurts far more. Loathing him this much is a monument to how completely the story made you care first.

4. Danzo Shimura

Anime: Naruto

Danzo is the rot under Konoha's floorboards. Operating in the shadows with his Root operatives, he made the cold, calculating calls that turned a loyal teenager into a mass murderer to protect a village that never knew his name. The tragedy of Itachi runs straight through Danzo's decisions, which is why so many fans reserve a particular contempt for him. Our Itachi Quotes collection carries the full weight of what those choices cost.

5. The Celestial Dragons

Anime: One Piece

Oda built the Celestial Dragons to be hated on sight, and it works every single time. The self-styled nobility of the One Piece world treat other people as property, trade lives for sport, and expect the sky itself to apologize for existing near them. They are not complex, and that is the point. They are a mirror held up to real cruelty, and the story earns its biggest cheers whenever one of them gets humbled. Few groups make an audience root for their downfall this hard. Our One Piece Quotes collection has plenty of lines aimed squarely at people like them.

6. Nobuyuki Sugou

Anime: Sword Art Online

Sugou is a predator dressed up as a scientist, and Sword Art Online frames him that way on purpose. His treatment of Asuna is built to make your skin crawl and your fists clench, a villain whose whole function is to be someone you desperately want defeated. We will leave the details where they belong, off the page. The hatred he generates is the intended effect, which makes him one of the medium's most efficient hate magnets even if he is far from its deepest villain.

7. Akainu

Anime: One Piece

Akainu is what happens when absolute conviction loses every last trace of mercy. The marine's brand of Absolute Justice excuses any cruelty, and he is responsible for one of the most devastating losses in One Piece history, a moment fans still bring up with clenched teeth. What complicates the hate is that he genuinely believes he is right, which makes him more chilling than any cackling tyrant. You do not hate Akainu for being cartoonish. You hate him for being coldly, convincingly certain.

8. Seryu Ubiquitous

Anime: Akame ga Kill!

Seryu believes she is a hero, and that is what makes her so hard to stomach. Convinced her twisted version of justice forgives any atrocity, she carries out brutal acts with a righteous smile and never once doubts herself. Akame ga Kill uses her to show how an ideology can hollow a person out from the inside. She is genuinely unsettling, less a villain who knows she is evil than one who is certain she is good.

9. Rachel

Anime: Tower of God

Rachel's betrayal at the end of Tower of God's first season landed like a slap nobody saw coming. After a whole season of Bam climbing the Tower for her sake, she repaid that devotion with a choice so cold it defined the fandom's opinion of her overnight. Her quiet, unassuming surface is exactly what makes the turn cut so deep. Fans do not simply dislike Rachel. They feel personally betrayed on Bam's behalf, which is a strange kind of proof that the writing worked.

10. Kyubey

Anime: Puella Magi Madoka Magica

The cutest face on this list belongs to its most quietly monstrous mind. Kyubey offers wishes with the flat, blinking calm of something that cannot grasp why humans object to the fine print, and the fine print is horrifying. It never raises its voice or shows a flicker of malice, which is worse, because it means the cruelty is not personal at all. Madoka turns a mascot design into the stuff of nightmares, and the hate Kyubey earns comes laced with genuine dread.

11. Aizen Sosuke

Anime: Bleach

Aizen spent most of Bleach making everyone, the audience included, feel like a pawn in a plan he finished writing years earlier. The betrayal of the Soul Society, the endless reveals that he was five steps ahead, the smug certainty of all of it, they add up to a villain fans love to hate in equal measure. The twist with Aizen is that the loathing and the admiration are the same feeling. Our Aizen Quotes collection captures exactly why he lives rent-free in so many heads.

12. Minoru Mineta

Anime: My Hero Academia

Here is where the hate changes flavor. Mineta is not evil, he is exhausting, the comic-relief classmate whose perverted bits land worse every year the fandom grows up. Plenty of viewers wish My Hero Academia had quietly written him out, and the arguments about him get loud. In fairness, he is doing exactly the job an old shonen archetype was built to do. It is just an archetype that has aged badly. Somebody out there still laughs at his gags, but a lot more reach for the mute button.

13. Gabi Braun

Anime: Attack on Titan

Gabi might be the most unfairly hated character of the last decade. Yes, she pulled a trigger that broke a lot of hearts, and plenty of fans have never forgiven her for it. Look closer, though, and she is a child raised inside the exact propaganda machine the series spends its whole runtime dissecting, doing the same thing the hero she mirrors once did. Attack on Titan built her to make you uncomfortable on purpose. If Eren's side of that mirror is where your loyalty sits, our Eren Yeager Quotes collection is worth revisiting with Gabi in mind.

14. Sakura Haruno

Anime: Naruto

No character sparks a more tired argument than Sakura. The complaints are familiar: she cried when the plot needed tension, she got sidelined beside Naruto and Sasuke, her growth arrived late and quiet. Some of that is fair criticism of how she was written. A lot of it is the fandom asking one kunoichi to shoulder the blame for the whole series' habit of underserving its women. She deserves a better conversation than the one she usually gets.

15. Suzaku Kururugi

Anime: Code Geass

Suzaku spent years as the single most hated character in his fandom, and time has been kind enough to show why that was mostly a compliment to his design. He exists as Lelouch's foil, the idealist who insists on fixing a broken system from the inside while our antihero burns it down from the outside. Rooting against Suzaku was the entire point. Watched today, he reads as a tragic, deeply human counterweight, and our Lelouch Quotes collection makes the case for both sides of that war.

A word on the overhated

Not every one of these hated anime characters deserves the pile-on, and the pattern is worth naming out loud. The internet has a habit of turning discomfort into a villain, then handing that villain a single character to carry all of it. Shinji Ikari caught years of scorn for being a frightened, traumatized teenager written more honestly than fans wanted to sit with. Gabi, Sakura, and Suzaku all took heat for serving their stories exactly as designed. Hating a well-built antagonist is a compliment to the writer. Hating a character for being human, or for exposing something we would rather not face, usually says more about the audience than the writing.

Hate, earned and otherwise

The most hated anime characters share a stage without sharing a reason. Shou Tucker and Sugou are here because the writing wanted you to recoil. Aizen and Griffith are here because the writing made you care first. Mineta is here because he grates, and Gabi, Sakura, and Suzaku are here because a fandom needed somewhere to point. Sorting the venom by its source is the only way to keep a list like this honest instead of cruel.

FAQs

Who is the most hated anime character of all time?

Shou Tucker from Fullmetal Alchemist tops nearly every fan vote, and by a wide margin. What he did to his daughter Nina is treated as one of the medium's true points of no return, which is why his name comes up first almost every time the question is asked.

Why is Shou Tucker so hated?

His cruelty is personal, quiet, and aimed at a child who trusted him, delivered early enough in Fullmetal Alchemist to shape the tone of everything that follows. Fans revile him not for spectacle but for how believable the betrayal feels.

Are hated villains a sign of good writing?

Often, yes. When a character like Griffith, Aizen, or Akainu generates real anger, it usually means the story made you care about their victims first. The strongest hatred tends to be a backhanded compliment to the writer.

Which anime character is unfairly hated?

Gabi Braun, Sakura Haruno, and Suzaku Kururugi are the usual examples, each blamed for doing exactly what their story required of them. Shinji Ikari is the classic case of a character hated for being written a little too honestly.

Why do fans hate Sakura?

The common complaints are about late character development and being sidelined next to Naruto and Sasuke. A fair chunk of the hate, though, reflects how the series underserved its female cast, a burden Sakura ends up carrying almost by herself.
