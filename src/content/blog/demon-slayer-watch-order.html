Mugen Train: the movie or the TV arc?

The one real choice in Demon Slayer is how to watch Mugen Train. The arc came out first as a 2020 theatrical film that broke box-office records, then returned in 2021 as the first seven episodes of Season 2, recut for TV with one extra prologue episode about Rengoku. They tell the same story, so you watch one, not both.

For a first watch, either works. The movie is the stunning, uninterrupted theatrical version, while the TV arc adds a little more Rengoku backstory and keeps everything in episode form. Pick the TV arc if you want the bonus content, or the movie for the big-screen cut. One thing to remember: if you watch the movie, skip Season 2's first seven episodes and start at episode 8, where the Entertainment District arc begins. That single overlap is the whole reason the Demon Slayer watch order looks confusing.

Where the Infinity Castle films fit

Demon Slayer does not end with a Season 5. The final arc, Infinity Castle, is being told as a trilogy of films instead. The first, Infinity Castle Part 1, arrived in 2025 and opens the last battle against Muzan. It is essential rather than optional, the true beginning of the ending. Part 1 is where the released story currently stops, with the remaining two films still to come, so the finale is the one stretch you cannot binge all at once yet. Watch it right after the Hashira Training arc.

FAQ

What is the Demon Slayer watch order?

The Demon Slayer watch order is Season 1, then Mugen Train (the movie or the TV arc), then the Entertainment District, Swordsmith Village, and Hashira Training arcs, and finally the Infinity Castle films. Follow the Demon Slayer show in order from Season 1 and you get the complete story, since there are no time skips.

Should I watch the Mugen Train movie or the TV arc?

Either one, since they cover the same story. The 2020 movie is the theatrical version, while the 2021 TV arc splits it across seven episodes and adds a prologue about Rengoku. Watch the TV arc for the extra content or the movie for the big-screen cut, but not both.

What comes after the Mugen Train arc?

The Entertainment District arc comes next. It is the second half of Season 2, starting at episode 8 right after Mugen Train. If you watched the Mugen Train movie instead of the TV episodes, jump straight to that episode 8 to continue.

What's after the Swordsmith Village arc?

The Hashira Training arc follows the Swordsmith Village arc, making up Season 4. After Hashira Training comes the Infinity Castle film trilogy, which adapts the story's final arc and brings the anime to its conclusion.

Where can I watch Demon Slayer?

Crunchyroll carries the complete franchise, including all four seasons, the Mugen Train movie, and Infinity Castle Part 1, in sub and dub. Netflix has the TV seasons in many regions. Availability varies by region, so check what is streaming near you before you start.
