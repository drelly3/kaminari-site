The anime most like Vinland Saga, ranked

Here are the anime like Vinland Saga that genuinely share its build, ranked from the closest match to the loosest. The Similarity Rating is our read on how near each one gets to Vinland Saga specifically, not how good the show is overall, and we kept the stars to whole numbers. The build we are measuring against is strict: a grounded, realistic epic with brutal violence, deep character study, grey morality, and a reckoning with the cost of revenge, with no fantasy allowed.

1. 91 Days

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: During Prohibition-era America, Angelo Lagusa watches his entire family murdered by the mafia, then vanishes. Years later, a letter draws him back under a false name, and he worms his way into the very family that destroyed his, plotting an intricate revenge from the inside over the course of ninety-one days. Every bond he forms is a weapon and a wound. It is a tight, grounded crime tragedy with no wasted motion.

For Vinland Saga's revenge core, this is the closest match on the list. It is a completely grounded, fantasy-free story about a man who loses his family to violence and devotes himself to vengeance, only to be hollowed out by what it costs him, which is Thorfinn's early arc in a different century. The mature tone, the moral greyness, the real historical backdrop, and the hard look at where revenge actually leads make it feel like a cousin cut from the same cloth.

2. Golden Kamuy

Similarity Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Sugimoto, a haunted veteran of the Russo-Japanese War, teams up with a young Ainu girl named Asirpa to hunt a hidden cache of gold in the frozen wilds of early-1900s Hokkaido. Standing between them and the treasure are escaped convicts, rogue soldiers, and a one-eyed warlord, each as dangerous as the last. The violence is brutal and the history is meticulously researched. It balances all of that with hunting, cooking, and genuine warmth.

The build match is the grounded historical seinen with real brutality and real heart: a story rooted in actual history, carried by damaged, morally complicated people, that never flinches from violence but refuses to be only about it. Like Vinland Saga, it treats its period and its cultures with care, and it finds humanity in the bloodshed. It is more of an adventure with an ensemble than a single-character study, which is the main difference, but the grounded, brutal, humane build is a direct hit.

3. Kingdom

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: In the Warring States era of ancient China, a war orphan named Xin dreams of becoming a Great General of the Heavens, rising from the lowest rung through sheer will and skill on the battlefield. His path tangles with a young king determined to unify the land, and together they wade through massive, brutal campaigns. The war is depicted on an enormous scale, with real strategy and real cost. It is a sprawling historical epic.

The connection is the grounded historical war epic built on an underdog's growth: a fantasy-free story of ambition, loyalty, and survival across sweeping campaigns, where battles are won with tactics and paid for in blood. Xin's climb and the political weight around him echo the scope and the realism of Vinland Saga's wars. It is more rousing and heroic than Vinland's somber meditation on violence, which keeps it a step below, but the historical-war-epic build is a strong fit.

4. Rurouni Kenshin

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Himura Kenshin was once the deadliest assassin of the war that birthed Japan's Meiji era, and now he wanders the land with a reversed-edge sword, vowing never to kill again. Haunted by the blood on his hands, he protects the people he meets while his violent past keeps catching up with him. The recent remake brings his story to modern animation. The darker prequel, Trust and Betrayal, is a brutal, grounded tragedy in its own right.

The build link is the arc at the heart of Vinland Saga: a man forged by violence who renounces killing and spends his life trying to atone. Kenshin's vow never to take another life is the same turn Thorfinn makes, the warrior deciding that true strength means refusing to use it, in a grounded historical setting. The main series runs lighter and more action-driven than Vinland, but the atonement-from-a-life-of-violence core is a near-perfect thematic match.

5. Monster

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 5/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Prime Video

Synopsis: Dr. Kenzo Tenma is a gifted neurosurgeon who defies his hospital to save a young boy's life, a choice that ruins his career and, years later, unleashes something far worse, as the boy grows into a brilliant, remorseless killer. Tenma sets out across Europe to stop the monster he saved. It is a slow-burning, grounded suspense story with no powers and no action, only dread and moral weight. Johan is one of anime's greatest villains.

The connection is the grounded, mature seinen that treats its characters and its moral questions with total seriousness: no fantasy, no spectacle, just real people wrestling with guilt, violence, and what a person owes the world. Like Vinland Saga, it is a prestige character study that asks whether a good person can stay good in a brutal world. It is a modern thriller rather than a historical war epic, but the grounded depth and the moral gravity are squarely Vinland territory.

6. Berserk

Similarity Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Guts is a lone mercenary with a monstrous sword and a body covered in scars, hunting the demonic forces and the friend who betrayed him. His story reaches back to a brotherhood of warriors and a horror that shattered it, setting him on a lifetime of vengeance. The medieval world is merciless and the violence is relentless. It is one of the most influential dark tales ever told.

The build match is the brutal medieval saga of violence, betrayal, and a warrior consumed by revenge, carried by one of the genre's deepest character studies. Guts and Thorfinn are kindred spirits, men defined by a loss that sent them down a path of blood, and the Golden Age arc in particular shares Vinland's grounded, war-torn weight. The one real caveat is the supernatural, since Berserk is dark fantasy with demons and curses where Vinland Saga stays strictly grounded, so the realism is where they part ways.

7. Jormungand

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Jonah is a child soldier who hates weapons, yet ends up working as a bodyguard for Koko, a sharp and unsettling arms dealer who travels the globe selling death with a smile. As he follows her through deals, ambushes, and moral minefields, he tries to make sense of a woman who claims her trade will bring peace. The series is a grounded, globe-trotting crime thriller. Its politics are as sharp as its gunfights.

The build connection is the grounded, fantasy-free world of violence and the characters trying to find meaning inside it: no magic, no chosen ones, just mercenaries, dealers, and the real cost of the trade. Jonah's hatred of the very violence he is surrounded by echoes Vinland Saga's central question about whether peace is possible in a brutal world. It is slicker and more modern than Vinland, but the grounded, morally grey, character-driven build fits.

8. Angolmois: Record of Mongol Invasion

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: Exiles dumped on the island of Tsushima find themselves on the front line when the Mongol Empire launches its massive invasion of Japan in 1274. A disgraced warrior named Kuchii Jinzaburou rallies the island's defenders in a desperate, bloody stand against overwhelming odds. The history is real and the fighting is grim. It is a lean, underrated war story.

The build match is the grounded historical war saga: a real invasion, a brutal defensive fight, and warriors reckoning with survival and loss, all without a shred of fantasy. Like Vinland Saga, it dramatizes an actual historical conflict and finds its drama in the human cost of battle. It is shorter and rougher around the edges, and less philosophically ambitious, but the realistic-historical-war build is a genuine fit for Vinland fans hungry for more of that era-accurate brutality.

9. The Heike Story

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: A young orphan with an eye that can see the future is taken in by the powerful Taira clan at the height of its glory, just as the clan's slow, tragic fall begins. Through her eyes, the Genpei War unfolds, carrying the proud Heike toward their doom. It is a grounded historical tragedy drawn from one of Japan's great classical epics. The art is gorgeous and the melancholy runs deep.

The connection is the grounded historical epic about war, pride, and the inevitability of loss, told with real weight and real beauty. Like Vinland Saga, it treats a genuine era of history with care and uses war to meditate on impermanence and the cost of ambition. The single supernatural touch, the girl's foresight, is minor, and the story stays rooted in reality. It is quieter and more elegiac than Vinland's brutality, but the historical-tragedy build is a strong match.

10. Banana Fish

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Prime Video

Synopsis: Ash Lynx is a brilliant, traumatized teenage gang leader on the streets of New York, chasing the truth behind a mysterious drug called Banana Fish that destroyed his brother's mind. His path crosses with a gentle Japanese visitor, and the bond between them becomes his one fragile anchor in a world of violence. The series is a grounded, brutal crime tragedy. It pulls no punches about trauma or its cost.

The build link is the grounded, fantasy-free story of a young man shaped by violence and loss, fighting through a brutal world while a quiet relationship offers a glimpse of something better. Ash's arc, forged by abuse and vengeance yet reaching for peace, rhymes with Thorfinn's, and the series shares Vinland Saga's unflinching maturity. It is a modern crime drama rather than a historical saga, but the grounded brutality and the deep character study put it in the same family.

11. Samurai Champloo

Similarity Rating: 3/5 ⭐⭐⭐

Anime Overall Rating: 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Watch on: Crunchyroll

Synopsis: In an Edo-period Japan remixed with hip-hop style, a wild swordsman named Mugen and a stoic ronin named Jin are saved from execution by a girl named Fuu, on the condition that they help her find a mysterious samurai. The unlikely trio travels the country, trading barbs and crossing blades with anyone who gets in the way. The sword fights are grounded and ferociously good. It is cool, funny, and quietly poignant.

The connection is the grounded historical setting and the realistic, weighty swordplay, with no fantasy in sight, just skilled fighters on a long road through a dangerous era. Like Vinland Saga, it roots its action in a real period and lets its characters grow across the journey. It is far lighter and more stylish than Vinland's grim saga, so it ranks lower, but the grounded-historical-sword-journey build is a solid, fantasy-free fit.

A couple that get recommended but break the build

Search around for anime similar to Vinland Saga and two names come up constantly that do not quite fit the grounded, fantasy-free build. One is worth your time on screen, and one you will have to read.

Attack on Titan is the most common recommendation, and it shares Vinland Saga's revenge-for-family spark, its epic scope, and its heavy themes of war and hatred, but its Titans and its sci-fi mystery put it firmly in fantasy territory, which breaks Vinland's strict grounded realism. Vagabond, Takehiko Inoue's masterpiece about a legendary swordsman's journey from bloodlust toward peace, is arguably the closest thing in spirit to Vinland Saga ever made, grounded, brutal, and philosophical, but it has never been animated, so for now it lives only on the page. Both are essential in their own way. Just know that one trades grounded for fantasy, and the other is a manga rather than an anime.

FAQs

Is there an anime exactly like Vinland Saga?

Not a perfect twin, because its grounded, philosophical brutality is rare. The closest are 91 Days, a completely fantasy-free revenge tragedy, and Golden Kamuy, a brutal, meticulously historical seinen with real heart. Each nails a core piece of what makes Vinland Saga great.

What to watch after Vinland Saga

If you want the smoothest landing, start at the top of this list of anime like Vinland Saga: 91 Days, Golden Kamuy, Kingdom, Rurouni Kenshin, or Monster. Each one carries over the grounded realism, the brutal maturity, or the deep character study that made Thorfinn's story so powerful.

Is there a grounded anime like Vinland Saga with no fantasy?

Yes, and that is the whole point of this list. 91 Days, Golden Kamuy, Kingdom, Monster, Jormungand, Angolmois, and Banana Fish all stay completely grounded, with brutal, realistic violence and no supernatural elements, so you get Vinland Saga's weight without any magic.

Is Vinland Saga getting a Season 3?

As of now, no release date has been confirmed. A third season is widely expected and would adapt Thorfinn's Eastern Expedition arc, continuing his journey after Season 2, but neither MAPPA nor the production committee has announced a premiere window yet.

Where can you watch anime like Vinland Saga?

Most of these stream on Crunchyroll, including Vinland Saga itself, while Monster and Banana Fish sit on Prime Video. Streaming rights shift by region and over time, so double-check what is available where you are before you settle in.
