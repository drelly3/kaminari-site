Purple might be the most quietly iconic hair color in anime. It rarely lands on the loudest hero, but it shows up on a striking number of the medium's coolest side characters, mysterious fighters, and scene-stealing geniuses. We rounded up twenty of the best anime characters with purple hair, male and female, and ranked them on the personalities that make the color unforgettable.

Purple hair anime characters tend to carry a certain mystique, which makes sense given the color's long association with royalty, magic, and mystery. Some wear it in deep dark violet, some in soft lavender, and a rare few blend it with black or blue toward the tips. Purple hair characters run the gamut from deadly assassins to deadpan mages to actual princes.

Roughly half this list is female and half is not, spanning classic shonen, modern hits, and a few cult favorites. Every pick earns its place on character, not just the color, and we kept the main list to characters who are purple all the way through. Let's get into it.

The 20 best purple hair anime characters

Gathered from every corner of anime and ranked loosely by how unforgettable they are, across every shade from soft lavender to near-black violet.

1. Kyoka Jiro

Anime: My Hero Academia

Jiro is Class 1-A's resident cool kid, all punk-rock attitude and secret soft spots. Her dark purple bob suits a personality that would rather die than admit she cares, even as she absolutely does. Sharp, musical, and quietly one of the most likable heroes in the class. Do not let the deadpan fool you.

2. Fern

Anime: Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

Fern is deadpan devastation in mage form. Frieren's diligent apprentice pairs enormous magical talent with a flat, unimpressed stare that became an instant fandom favorite. Her purple hair suits a character who is disciplined, quietly emotional, and completely done with everyone's nonsense. A modern classic in the making.

3. Motoko Kusanagi

Anime: Ghost in the Shell

The Major is one of the most influential characters anime has ever produced. A cyborg commander with a philosopher's mind, she leads Section 9 with cool precision while quietly wrestling with what it means to be human. Her purple hair in Stand Alone Complex became a signature. Brilliant, formidable, and endlessly copied.

4. Faye Valentine

Anime: Cowboy Bebop

Faye is a con artist, a gambler, and one of the most grounded, human characters in all of anime. Brash and self-interested on the surface, she slowly reveals real vulnerability and a painful past. Her purple hair suits a woman who trusts no one and needs everyone. Cowboy Bebop would not be the same without her.

5. Yoruichi Shihouin

Anime: Bleach

Yoruichi is speed and swagger in equal measure. The Flash Goddess moves faster than the eye can follow and treats life-or-death combat like a game she has already won. Her sleek purple hair fits a character who is playful, brilliant, and completely in control. One of Bleach's coolest, full stop.

6. Shinoa Hiiragi

Anime: Seraph of the End

Shinoa hides real sharpness behind a teasing, mischievous grin. A young commander who mercilessly needles her allies while quietly carrying the weight of leadership, she is far more capable than her jokes let on. Her light purple hair matches a personality that keeps everyone guessing. Snark with a soul underneath.

7. Touka Kirishima

Anime: Tokyo Ghoul

Touka is all sharp edges protecting a soft center. Fierce, quick-tempered, and fiercely loyal, she fights to hold onto some scrap of a normal life in a brutal world. Her purple hair suits a character constantly torn between rage and tenderness. One of Tokyo Ghoul's most compelling hearts.

8. Saeko Busujima

Anime: Highschool of the Dead

Saeko is calm, composed, and absolutely lethal with a blade. A kendo prodigy who keeps her cool while the world falls apart, she reveals a darker, fiercer side the deeper the chaos gets. Her long purple hair suits a warrior who is graceful right up until she is terrifying. Poised and dangerous.

9. Sheele

Anime: Akame ga Kill

Sheele is clumsy, forgetful, and hopeless at everyday life, right up until she picks up her weapon. A member of Night Raid who finds her one true talent in combat, she is gentle and self-doubting in a way that makes her story hit hard. Her purple hair suits a character far kinder than her job description. A soft-hearted assassin.

10. Yugao Uzuki

Anime: Naruto

Yugao is one of the Hidden Leaf's most disciplined kunoichi, an ANBU operative and gifted swordswoman who carries herself with quiet, steely resolve. Beneath the mask is a woman shaped by real loss, devoted to her duty and to the memory of the person she loved. Her long purple hair suits a character defined by calm precision and unspoken grief. Understated, loyal, and quietly formidable.

11. Hitagi Senjougahara

Anime: Monogatari

Hitagi is razor-sharp and gloriously prickly, wielding sarcasm like the office supplies she famously keeps on hand. Cold and guarded after a painful past, she slowly softens without ever losing her edge. Her long purple hair suits one of anime's most memorably sharp-tongued heroines. Do not start a fight with her you cannot finish.

12. Trunks

Anime: Dragon Ball Z

Trunks is the classic purple-haired pick, sword on his back and a mission to change a doomed future. The time-traveling Saiyan brought real stakes and real heart to Dragon Ball, fighting for a world that had already lost everything. His lavender bowl cut is pure nostalgia. A hero shaped by equal parts tragedy and hope.

13. Jinshi

Anime: The Apothecary Diaries

Jinshi is charm, mischief, and hidden depth wrapped in court intrigue. Playfully manipulative on the surface, he hides a serious role and real loneliness underneath, and his fixation on the clever Maomao drives half the fun. His purple hair suits a character who is never quite showing his whole hand. A big modern fan favorite.

14. Hitoshi Shinso

Anime: My Hero Academia

Shinso is the underdog with a villain's Quirk and a hero's heart. Sharp-tongued and a little jaded, he claws toward the hero course through pure determination, refusing to let a scary-sounding power define him. His messy purple hair suits a character who is far kinder than he first appears. Endlessly easy to root for.

15. Atsushi Murasakibara

Anime: Kuroko's Basketball

Murasakibara is a lazy giant with monstrous natural talent, and his name literally means purple. Towering over everyone and treating basketball like a chore until something finally lights his fire, he is equal parts comedy and genuine threat. His long purple hair fits a character who is gentle until you make him try. A sleepy powerhouse.

16. Sinbad

Anime: Magi

Sinbad is charisma given a crown. A legendary adventurer turned king, he charms everyone he meets while chasing ambitions far larger than they appear on the surface. His purple hair suits a character who is dazzling, cunning, and just morally grey enough to stay interesting. The kind of leader people follow anywhere.

17. Shuu Tsukiyama

Anime: Tokyo Ghoul

Tsukiyama is theatrical, obsessive, and gloriously over the top. A gourmet ghoul who treats everything, including people, as an aesthetic experience, he swings between hilarious and genuinely unsettling. His purple hair suits a character who turns every scene into a performance. Unhinged in the most entertaining way.

18. Chrome Dokuro

Anime: Katekyo Hitman Reborn!

Chrome is the shy, soft-spoken illusionist who slowly discovers her own strength. Timid and unsure of herself at first, she grows into a devoted, quietly brave member of the Vongola family, holding herself together through sheer will and a little help from her illusions. Her purple hair suits a character whose gentleness hides real resolve. An underdog well worth rooting for.

19. Nozomi Tojo

Anime: Love Live!

Nozomi is the warm, teasing spiritual heart of Love Live's idol group. Fond of tarot cards and a little playful mischief, she looks after the other girls with quiet, motherly care underneath all the ribbing. Her long purple hair suits a character who sees more than she lets on and holds the whole group together. Steady, wise, and secretly the glue.

20. Tooru Ishikawa

Anime: Horimiya

Ishikawa is the sweet, awkward everyman quietly nursing a crush through most of Horimiya. Kind, a little insecure, and genuinely trying to be a good friend even when it stings, he is the relatable heart of the cast. His purple hair suits a character whose whole charm is how normal and decent he is. The nice guy who actually turns out fine.

Honorable mentions

A few more worth a mention, starting with the ones whose purple is not the whole story: Shinobu Kocho, whose famous hair fades from black into purple rather than running purple throughout, and Revy, whose Black Lagoon locks read more dark burgundy than true violet. Add Homura Akemi's near-black violet in Madoka Magica, Rize Kamishiro over in Tokyo Ghoul, and Kyoko Kirigiri's lavender-grey in Danganronpa, and the list runs deep.

Purple, the color of the scene-stealer

Assassin, cyborg, prince, or deadpan mage, anime characters with purple hair have a way of walking off with scenes they were never supposed to own. The color reads as regal and mysterious, which is exactly why writers keep handing it to the coolest, sharpest, most quietly powerful members of the cast. Pick a favorite and you are really picking a character who was probably too interesting to stay in the background.

FAQs

Who is the most iconic purple haired anime character?

Kyoka Jiro and Fern consistently top fan rankings of anime characters with purple hair, with classics like Trunks and Motoko Kusanagi close behind. Each one pairs an unforgettable look with the kind of personality that carries a scene on its own.

Who are the best female purple hair characters?

Some of the best female purple hair characters are Fern, Faye Valentine, Yoruichi, Motoko Kusanagi, and Nozomi Tojo. They range from deadly assassins to philosophical cyborgs, which shows just how versatile the color really is.

What shades of purple appear in anime?

Anime purple spans a huge range: deep dark purple like Kyoka Jiro, soft lavender and light purple like Shinoa and Trunks, the occasional magenta lean, and even black-and-purple gradients like Shinobu Kocho's famous look. If it falls anywhere between blue and pink, anime has drawn it.

Are there male anime characters with purple hair?

Plenty. Trunks, Jinshi, Hitoshi Shinso, Sinbad, and Atsushi Murasakibara are some of the most popular, ranging from time-traveling Saiyans to scheming princes to sleepy basketball giants. Purple is far from a female-only color.

What does purple hair usually mean in anime?

Purple has long been tied to royalty, magic, mystery, and individuality, so writers often hand it to characters who are regal, magically gifted, or simply a cut above the ordinary. It quietly signals someone worth watching, which is why so many purple-haired characters end up stealing the show.
