INFJs, the Advocates, are the rarest personality type in the whole MBTI lineup, and one of the most compelling to watch on screen. Introverted, intuitive, and quietly driven by a private vision of a better world, they pair deep empathy with an unshakable sense of mission. Anime loves them, because a gentle idealist willing to carry an impossible burden for the greater good makes for unforgettable television. We gathered twenty of the most INFJ anime characters and the reasons each one fits.

The Advocate runs on introverted intuition, a knack for seeing patterns and futures nobody else can, paired with genuine care for people as a whole. That combination makes INFJs visionaries and quiet protectors, the ones plotting three moves ahead entirely for someone else's sake. They tend to hide their real feelings, and they will sacrifice almost anything for a cause they truly believe in.

Expect masterminds with soft hearts, healers with steel spines, and more than one character carrying a secret too heavy to share. Because MBTI typing gets argued over hard, especially the INFJ-versus-INTJ line, we kept the main list to broadly agreed Advocates and parked the contested picks in the honorable mentions. Let's get into it.

The 20 most INFJ anime characters

Each pick comes with the trait that seals it: the vision, the empathy, the mission, or the quiet sacrifice. These are the anime characters who read as Advocates through and through.

1. Itachi Uchiha

Anime: Naruto

Itachi is the INFJ blueprint. He sacrificed everything, his clan, his reputation, his bond with the brother he loved, all for a vision of peace only he could see. Every cruel choice was a calculated move toward a better future, carried in total silence. The self-sacrificing visionary with a secret burden is the Advocate at its most tragic and pure.

2. Armin Arlert

Anime: Attack on Titan

Armin thinks in possibilities nobody else can picture, which is textbook introverted intuition. Diplomatic, imaginative, and forever chasing a peaceful solution in a world that rarely allows one, he becomes the moral compass of the whole cast. His genius is quiet and his conscience is loud. Peak Advocate under impossible pressure.

3. Yuta Okkotsu

Anime: Jujutsu Kaisen

Yuta is a reserved sorcerer who takes on everyone's pain as his own. Rather than exorcise the spirit haunting him, he chooses to protect her, a decision that says everything about how an INFJ values connection and peace over the easy answer. Gentle, powerful, and driven entirely by the wish to help others rest.

4. Kenshin Himura

Anime: Rurouni Kenshin

Kenshin is an Advocate spending his whole life atoning. A former assassin who swore never to kill again, he wanders the country protecting strangers, guided by an ironclad personal code and a vision of a gentler era. Warm on the surface, haunted underneath, and completely unbending about his ideals. Classic INFJ resolve.

5. Izuku Midoriya

Anime: My Hero Academia

Deku's real superpower is empathy. He feels other people's pain so intensely that saving them becomes reflexive, and his mind is always racing to find the way. That mix of deep compassion and analytical vision, aimed squarely at a better future, is what makes him such a widely typed Advocate. A hero built out of heart and strategy.

6. Yuki Sohma

Anime: Fruits Basket

Yuki looks like the perfect prince and feels like a prisoner. Quiet, principled, and slowly resistant to the family that tries to control him, he refuses to stay anyone's pawn and painstakingly builds a self worth defending. His inner struggle between duty and identity is a deeply Advocate arc. Gentle steel.

7. Kaworu Nagisa

Anime: Neon Genesis Evangelion

Kaworu drifts into Evangelion like a philosophical riddle, all serene kindness and unreadable depth. He understands Shinji instantly and offers the unconditional acceptance the boy has never had, acting on a compassion that seems to come from somewhere beyond the story. Mysterious, empathetic, and unmistakably Advocate.

8. Koshi Sugawara

Anime: Haikyuu!!

Sugawara is the heart of Karasuno, nicknamed “Mom” for good reason. Even after losing his starting spot, he keeps lifting his teammates, reading their emotions and making sure no one feels left behind. That nurturing, emotionally attuned leadership is INFJ energy on a volleyball court. Quiet influence, enormous impact.

9. Haku

Anime: Spirited Away

Haku is gentle guidance wrapped in mystery. He protects Chihiro in a spirit world built to swallow her, steering her with quiet care while carrying a lost identity of his own. Reserved, self-sacrificing, and deeply kind beneath a cool exterior, he is a small but perfect Advocate. Ghibli at its most quietly moving.

10. Jellal Fernandes

Anime: Fairy Tail

Jellal is an Advocate consumed by the need to atone. Once manipulated into villainy, he devotes the rest of his life to making up for it, chasing a vision of justice while refusing to forgive himself. Idealistic, tormented, and endlessly self-sacrificing, he pours all that guilt into protecting the world. Very INFJ, for better and worse.

11. Canute

Anime: Vinland Saga

Canute transforms from a timid prince into a king with a genuine vision for a better world, all without shedding his underlying gentleness. His ideals harden into a mission he will pursue at almost any cost, which is the Advocate's blessing and curse both. Watching a soft-spoken boy become a purposeful leader is peak INFJ growth.

12. Ashitaka

Anime: Princess Mononoke

Ashitaka wants only one thing: for everyone to find a way to live together. Cursed and caught between warring sides, he refuses to choose hatred, seeking harmony where everyone else sees only enemies. Calm, principled, and quietly immovable in his ideals, he is the Advocate as peacemaker. A hero who fights for understanding itself.

13. Akane Tsunemori

Anime: Psycho-Pass

Akane is one of anime's finest INFJ leads. Empathetic, serious, and gifted with a deep read on people, she holds onto her own sense of justice even inside a system built to erode it. Where others break or comply, she stays genuine and mentally unbreakable. A quiet idealist with a spine of iron.

14. Lady Tamayo

Anime: Demon Slayer

Lady Tamayo is a demon who refuses to be what her nature demands. Rejecting the easy path of preying on humans, she devotes centuries to compassion and a cure, guided by a private moral code stronger than her own hunger. That fierce, self-directed sense of justice is pure Advocate. Kindness as an act of rebellion.

15. Hotaru Tomoe

Anime: Sailor Moon

Better known as Sailor Saturn, Hotaru carries the weight of destruction and rebirth in her small frame. Lonely, gentle, and quietly willing to sacrifice herself for the world, she embodies the Advocate's habit of shouldering enormous burdens without complaint. A tragic, tender heroine hiding immense power.

16. Anthy Himemiya

Anime: Revolutionary Girl Utena

Anthy is the private, mysterious INFJ taken to its extreme. Softly cheerful and endlessly polite on the surface, she guards a hidden inner world so carefully that even the audience takes ages to understand her. Reserved to the point of enigma, she is one of anime's most complex Advocates. Never quite what she seems.

17. Naomi Misora

Anime: Death Note

Naomi is sharp, principled, and one of the very few characters to nearly derail Light entirely. A former FBI agent with a strong intuitive read on people and an unshakable sense of right, she trusts her gut and it is almost always correct. Quietly brilliant and deeply moral, she is an Advocate the story tragically underused.

18. Kurenai Yuhi

Anime: Naruto

Kurenai leads with care. A genjutsu specialist and devoted teacher, she looks after her students like family and fights for the people she loves rather than for glory. Her calm, nurturing strength and quiet conviction make her a gentle Advocate in a world full of loud ninja. Soft-spoken, steel-willed.

19. Melody

Anime: Hunter x Hunter

Melody is calm turned into a superpower. A musician whose gentle presence soothes everyone around her, she reads emotions with uncanny accuracy and offers steady support without ever chasing the spotlight. That quiet empathy and hard-won inner peace are deeply Advocate. Proof that strength does not have to be loud.

20. Emilia

Anime: Re:Zero

Emilia leads with kindness in a world that gives her every reason not to. A candidate for the throne carrying centuries of prejudice, she holds onto her ideals and her compassion with quiet stubbornness. Some fans read her as a Mediator, but that mission-driven, everyone-deserves-fairness core leans Advocate. Gentle, principled, and quietly determined.

Honorable mentions

A few more who land on INFJ lists but whose type the community argues over, usually pulled toward INTJ: Lelouch vi Britannia and Kurapika, both master strategists with a cause, Norman from The Promised Neverland, Nico Robin, and Homura Akemi. All brilliant, all Advocate-adjacent, all worth the debate.

The rarest type, the deepest characters

For the rarest personality type of all, INFJ anime characters turn up in a startling number of the medium's most unforgettable roles. Itachi's sacrifice, Akane's conviction, Yuta's gentleness, it all flows from the same place: a private vision of a better world and the will to carry it quietly. That blend of empathy and mission is exactly what makes Advocates so easy to love and so hard to forget. Pick a favorite and you have probably picked one of the most layered characters in their whole series.

FAQs

What is an INFJ personality type?

INFJ stands for Introverted, Intuitive, Feeling, and Judging, nicknamed the Advocate. It is the rarest of the sixteen types, marked by deep empathy, a vivid sense of vision, strong personal principles, and a drive to make the world better. Any anime character with INFJ personality traits tends to lead with quiet conviction rather than noise.

Which anime characters are INFJ?

Some of the clearest, least-debated examples are Itachi Uchiha, Armin Arlert, Yuta Okkotsu, Akane Tsunemori, and Kenshin Himura. Each one pairs sharp intuition with deep empathy and a cause worth sacrificing for, which is the Advocate in a nutshell.

Who is the most INFJ anime character?

Itachi Uchiha is the most-cited example, and it is easy to see why. His entire life was a chain of quiet sacrifices for a vision of peace only he could see, carried alone and in silence. He is the Advocate at its most tragic and complete.

Are these INFJ-T anime characters or INFJ-A?

Most of the INFJ-T anime characters here lean turbulent, meaning self-questioning and emotionally intense, rather than the calmer, more confident INFJ-A. Characters like Itachi, Kenshin, and Jellal carry exactly the kind of inner doubt and guilt that defines the turbulent Advocate.

What is the difference between INFJ and INFP anime characters?

Both are gentle idealists, but INFJ anime characters lead with a vision for the wider world and a plan to reach it, while INFPs lead with personal values and authenticity. An INFJ like Itachi will sacrifice himself for a cause, whereas an INFP like Mob just wants to stay true to himself. The Advocate looks outward, the Mediator looks inward.
