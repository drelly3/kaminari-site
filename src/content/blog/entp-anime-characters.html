ENTPs, the Debaters, are anime's most dangerous minds and biggest troublemakers: the quick-witted schemers, chaotic geniuses, and silver-tongued provocateurs who would always rather out-think you than fight you. Brilliant, restless, and allergic to boredom, they poke holes in every rule, argue for fun, and improvise their way through situations that should be impossible. Anime cannot get enough of them. We gathered twenty of the most convincing ENTP anime characters and the trait that seals each one.

The Debater runs on wide-open imagination fused with cold, playful logic, forever chasing the most interesting possibility over the safest one. That is what separates the ENTP from the very similar ENFP: both dream big, but where the Campaigner is powered by feeling and connection, the Debater is powered by ideas and argument. It also makes the type impossible to pin as hero or villain, since the exact same brilliance builds a beloved mentor or a nightmare mastermind.

One note before we start: these are community typings, not creator-confirmed labels, and the type skews heavily male in anime, though we surfaced every clear Debater we could find. Half the fun of the ENTP anime canon is that you are never quite sure whose side they are on. Here are the ENTP anime characters who prove the sharpest weapon in any story is usually a mind that will not sit still.

The 20 most ENTP anime characters, ranked

Each pick comes with the Debater trait that seals it: the love of the argument, the chaotic genius, or the grin that means they are already three steps ahead. These are the ENTP anime characters fans name first, heroes and villains alike.

1. Satoru Gojo

Anime: Jujutsu Kaisen

The strongest sorcerer alive is also its biggest instigator. Gojo treats world-ending battles like games, mocks the entire cursed establishment he is supposed to serve, and reshapes the system from the inside purely because the old way bores him. Playful, brilliant, and constitutionally incapable of taking authority seriously, he weaponizes charisma as effectively as his six eyes. Our Gojo Quotes collection has all the swagger a Debater can carry.

2. Osamu Dazai

Anime: Bungo Stray Dogs

If the Debater needs a masterpiece, it is Dazai. He wins conflicts not by being stronger but by having anticipated everyone's moves three steps ago, hiding a razor mind and a bleak past behind faked suicide attempts and relentless teasing. He is charming, theatrical, and impossible to read, a schemer who reveals his real hand only when it is far too late. Few characters embody the ENTP's chaotic genius as completely as he does.

3. Hisoka Morow

Anime: Hunter x Hunter

Hisoka approaches every person as a puzzle to probe and every fight as an experiment. He will actively help an enemy grow stronger just to make the eventual clash more interesting, chasing the most thrilling possibility over the easy win. Coldly logical beneath the clown makeup and unsettling charm, he lives entirely for the next fascinating challenge. He is the Debater's curiosity taken somewhere genuinely disturbing.

4. Izaya Orihara

Anime: Durarara!!

Izaya is an information broker who loves humanity the way a scientist loves lab rats, engineering chaos across a whole city just to watch what people do. Quick, gleeful, and endlessly manipulative, he treats every scheme as a grand experiment and every person as a variable. He is not cruel for its own sake so much as incapable of resisting an interesting outcome. The Debater as a god of mischief in a fur-trimmed coat.

5. Karma Akabane

Anime: Assassination Classroom

Karma is the gifted troublemaker who is bored by everything except a worthy challenge. Sharp, sadistic, and wildly creative in a fight, he improvises deadly plans on the fly and delivers them with a lazy grin. Beneath the delinquent act sits a genuinely brilliant strategist who respects only the people who can keep up with him. He is the Debater's mischief and intellect rolled into one dangerous student.

6. Koro-sensei

Anime: Assassination Classroom

The tentacled alien threatening to destroy the Earth is also the best teacher his students ever had. Koro-sensei runs on boundless curiosity and inventive problem-solving, tailoring a wild new lesson to each kid and turning his own assassination into the ultimate teaching game. Playful, brilliant, and always three ideas ahead, he hides deep care behind endless mischief. A Debater who chose to build people up instead of tearing them down.

7. Ryuk

Anime: Death Note

Ryuk drops a god-killing notebook into the human world for one reason: he was bored. The shinigami watches the ensuing carnage with the detached delight of someone running an experiment, cheerfully refusing to take sides as long as the show stays interesting. Amoral, curious, and endlessly amused, he treats chaos as a spectator sport. Our Light Yagami Quotes collection tracks the human he handed the match to.

8. Kuroo Tetsuro

Anime: Haikyuu!!

Kuroo is the provocateur of the volleyball court, needling opponents, baiting rivals, and running mind games with a sly grin the whole time. A clever middle blocker and a natural schemer, he reads the game two plays ahead and enjoys getting under people's skin almost as much as winning. Charismatic and analytical in equal measure, he is the Debater as a scheming captain who turns every match into a chess problem.

9. Hawks

Anime: My Hero Academia

Hawks solves problems faster than anyone can follow, improvising his way through impossible situations with easy charm and a mind that never stops calculating. The number-two hero plays a dangerous double game, talking his way into and out of trouble while keeping his real intentions hidden behind a relaxed grin. Quick, unconventional, and always working an angle, he is a Debater built for the long con. Our My Hero Academia Quotes collection has more from his world.

10. Reigen Arataka

Anime: Mob Psycho 100

Reigen has no psychic powers whatsoever and runs a thriving spirits-and-consultation business anyway, purely on quick thinking and an unshakable gift for talking his way through anything. He improvises solutions on the spot, reframes every crisis into an opportunity, and somehow ends up dispensing real wisdom by accident. A con artist with a surprisingly good heart, he is the Debater's silver tongue given a business card.

11. Kisuke Urahara

Anime: Bleach

Urahara hides a brilliant, restless inventor behind a lazy shopkeeper's grin and a striped hat. He designs impossible-looking training scenarios that always have a hidden solution, engineers plans years in advance, and reveals only as much as he wants you to know. Playful on the surface and coldly strategic underneath, he out-thinks every problem rather than overpowering it. Our Bleach Filler List keeps your rewatch clean.

12. Joseph Joestar

Anime: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

Joseph wins fights by cheating cleverly, trash-talking, and predicting exactly what his enemy will say next. Where other JoJos overpower their foes, he out-thinks them, improvising ridiculous plans that somehow always work. Loud, quick-witted, and allergic to doing anything the straightforward way, he is the franchise's purest Debater. Our JoJo Filler List sorts the whole saga out.

13. Shigure Sohma

Anime: Fruits Basket

Shigure plays the easygoing novelist while quietly orchestrating events years in the making, always several moves ahead of everyone who thinks they have him figured out. Charming, teasing, and genuinely difficult to trust, he pursues his goals through subtle manipulation and a maddening refusal to ever be straight with anyone. Beneath the flippancy is a sharp, calculating mind. The Debater at his most quietly dangerous.

14. Edward Elric

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist

Edward is a hot-headed genius who breaks the laws of alchemy and society with equal enthusiasm. He argues with everyone, rejects authority on principle, and finds solutions nobody else considered by refusing to accept that a problem has no answer. Quick-tempered, inventive, and relentlessly curious, he thinks his way out of corners that should be hopeless. Our Edward Elric Quotes collection has more of that stubborn brilliance.

15. Bulma

Anime: Dragon Ball

Bulma is the brains that kickstarts the entire franchise, a teenage inventor who builds world-changing technology out of sheer curiosity and stubborn confidence. Quick-tongued, fearless, and endlessly inventive, she talks back to gods and tinkers her way through every crisis the fighters cannot punch. She keeps the whole saga running on brainpower and nerve. The Debater as the brightest mind in a cast full of warriors.

16. Haruhi Suzumiya

Anime: The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya

Haruhi is a whirlwind of ideas who bends reality to her whims and bulldozes anyone too slow to keep up. Demanding, inventive, and permanently bored by the ordinary, she launches wild schemes just to see what happens and drags a whole club along for the ride. She wants the extraordinary so badly she accidentally creates it. A Debater whose restless imagination is literally a force of nature.

17. Hange Zoe

Anime: Attack on Titan

Hange turns relentless curiosity into a personality, chasing Titan research with a manic glee that treats even mortal danger as a fascinating experiment. Brilliant, unconventional, and forever asking the next question, they bounce between wild theories with obvious delight while quietly carrying the weight of leadership. That hunger to understand everything is peak Debater. Few characters make raw intellectual excitement this infectious.

18. Mei Hatsume

Anime: My Hero Academia

Mei is an inventor with zero social filter and a thousand ideas a minute, cheerfully hijacking any situation to show off her latest gadget. Boundlessly enthusiastic and completely undeterred by failure, she treats every setback as data for the next wild prototype. Her brain simply never stops generating possibilities. The Debater as a gremlin genius who would absolutely blow up her own workshop for science.

19. Yumeko Jabami

Anime: Kakegurui

Yumeko lives for the thrill of a high-stakes gamble, reading opponents with unnerving precision and improvising her way through impossible odds. Sharp, unpredictable, and energized by exactly the risks that terrify everyone else, she treats each match as a delicious intellectual puzzle. Behind the excitement is a mind coldly calculating every angle. A Debater who found her perfect arena at the gambling table.

20. Konata Izumi

Anime: Lucky Star

Konata is the quick-witted slacker who could ace anything if she cared to, but would rather spend her genius on games, anime, and winding up her friends. Clever, playful, and gleefully contrarian, she turns everyday conversations into rapid-fire comedic sparring and dodges effort with impressive creativity. Her mischief is the whole charm. The Debater at its most low-stakes and delightfully lazy.

Honorable mentions

A couple of names get typed ENTP often but lean warmer than the Debater, running on feeling more than cold logic: Himiko Toga and Obito Uchiha both fit the Campaigner mold better. On the genuine ENTP bench, Kabuto Yakushi experiments his way through Naruto with unsettling curiosity, Mayuri Kurotsuchi brings mad-scientist chaos to Bleach, and Kimblee treats destruction like a fascinating theory in Fullmetal Alchemist. All of them keep the debate lively.

Why the Debaters keep us guessing

Line them up and the pattern is clear: ENTP anime characters do not win by hitting hardest, they win by thinking fastest. Gojo's mockery, Dazai's schemes, Hisoka's experiments, it all runs on the same engine, a restless mind that treats the whole world as a puzzle worth poking. That is why the Debater is impossible to pin down and impossible to look away from, whether they are saving the day or setting the whole board on fire.

FAQs

What is an ENTP personality type?

ENTP stands for Extroverted, Intuitive, Thinking, and Perceiving, nicknamed the Debater. ENTPs are quick-witted, inventive, and endlessly curious, chasing interesting possibilities, questioning every rule, and arguing for the sheer fun of it. In fiction the type lands on clever tricksters, chaotic geniuses, and silver-tongued schemers.

Which anime characters are ENTP?

The clearest ENTP anime characters include Satoru Gojo, Osamu Dazai, Hisoka Morow, Izaya Orihara, and Ryuk. Each one out-thinks rather than overpowers, treats the world as a puzzle to solve, and refuses to sit still or play by anyone's rules, which is the Debater in a nutshell.

Who is the most ENTP anime character?

Satoru Gojo and Osamu Dazai top most fan rankings, both leading entirely with wit and strategy. Gojo is the playful, rule-breaking genius who reshapes his world for fun, while Dazai is the master schemer who has already won three moves before anyone notices the game has started.

Are ENTP anime characters usually heroes or villains?

Both, which is the whole appeal. The same restless brilliance that makes a beloved mentor like Koro-sensei also makes a nightmare like Hisoka, so ENTP anime villains show up just as often as the heroes. The type is defined by how it thinks, not which side it lands on.

What is the difference between ENTP and ENFP anime characters?

Both are imaginative, spontaneous extroverts, but their fuel differs. An ENTP is driven by logic and ideas, debating and experimenting to see what is true, while an ENFP is driven by feeling and values, inspiring people toward a better future.
