Anime has a gift for making us care about a character and then absolutely destroying us. The saddest anime characters are the ones whose pain lingers long after the episode ends, whether through a devastating loss, a cruel childhood, or a fate they never deserved. We gathered twenty of the most heartbreaking, ranked by how hard their stories hit.

This list runs on tragic backstories and gut-punch arcs. Some of these are the most depressing anime characters ever written, carrying grief most people could not survive, while others break your heart quietly, through a single loss or a slow goodbye. Anime characters with tragic backstories tend to become the ones we love most, because their suffering makes their strength mean something.

Fair warning: keep tissues nearby. Cursed swordsmen, war orphans, and demons who were once human, these are the sad anime characters all but guaranteed to wreck you. Let's get into it, and try not to cry.

The saddest anime characters and their tragic backstories

Ranked loosely by how thoroughly they broke us. Every one of these sad anime characters earned their spot the hard way, through a story that hurts to remember.

1. Guts

Anime: Berserk

Guts was born into horror and raised on cruelty, and the world has spent every day since trying to break him. Just when he finally found a family and something like happiness, the Eclipse took nearly everyone he loved in a single unthinkable night. What makes Guts the saddest is that he keeps getting back up anyway, dragging his grief behind him. Endless suffering met with endless defiance.

2. Itachi Uchiha

Anime: Naruto

Itachi's whole life was a quiet sacrifice nobody ever thanked him for. Forced to choose between his family and his village, he shouldered an unbearable burden and let the brother he loved grow up hating him for it. He died still protecting Sasuke, the truth of his devotion revealed far too late. Few characters have ever paid a higher price for peace.

3. Hyakkimaru

Anime: Dororo

Hyakkimaru's own father traded pieces of his newborn body to demons in exchange for power, leaving him without eyes, ears, limbs, or a voice. He spends the series reclaiming his stolen humanity one brutal fight at a time, feeling pain for the very first time as he does. A story about earning the right to simply exist. Devastating and strangely beautiful.

4. Homura Akemi

Anime: Puella Magi Madoka Magica

Homura is trapped in a nightmare built out of her own love. Reliving the same stretch of time over and over, she watches her dearest friend die in every single timeline, growing colder and more desperate with each loop. Her whole arc is one girl breaking herself to save someone who may never even know she tried. Heartbreak on an infinite loop.

5. Violet Evergarden

Anime: Violet Evergarden

Violet was raised as a weapon, a child soldier who barely understood her own feelings until the one person who cared for her was suddenly gone. The series follows her learning what love and loss actually mean, one letter at a time, always a step behind her own grief. Watching her slowly become human is as beautiful as it is quietly shattering.

6. Kaneki Ken

Anime: Tokyo Ghoul

Kaneki was a gentle, book-loving college student until a single cruel twist of fate turned his life into a nightmare. Tortured and forced to abandon everything he was, he loses his humanity by inches while trying desperately to protect what little he has left. He never deserved any of it, which is exactly what makes his story so hard to watch.

7. Nico Robin

Anime: One Piece

Robin has been running since she was a child, the sole survivor of a home destroyed for the crime of remembering history. Hunted, betrayed, and alone for most of her life, she learned to trust no one because everyone she loved was taken from her. Her tearful cry of “I want to live” is one of the most cathartic moments in all of anime.

8. Thorfinn

Anime: Vinland Saga

Thorfinn watched his father die as a boy and then poured years of his youth into revenge, letting hatred hollow him out from the inside. When that revenge finally slips away, he is left with nothing, a broken young man forced to figure out who he even is without it. His slow climb back toward meaning is one of anime's most moving redemptions.

9. Alphonse Elric

Anime: Fullmetal Alchemist

Al lost his entire body as a child, his soul bound to a hollow suit of armor after one desperate mistake. He can no longer eat, sleep, or feel a human touch, and he spends the series fighting for the chance to simply be a boy again. His unbroken gentleness in the face of that loss is what makes his story quietly gutting.

10. Crona

Anime: Soul Eater

Crona was raised in total isolation by a mother who treated them as an experiment rather than a child. Starved of kindness and taught only fear, they grow up unable to handle even the smallest bit of human connection. Every glimpse of the frightened, lonely kid underneath makes Crona one of the most tragic figures in the series. A child failed by everyone.

11. Denji

Anime: Chainsaw Man

Life never gave Denji a break. He lost his parents young, spent his childhood buried under a debt that was never his, and sold pieces of himself just to survive one more day. All he ever wanted was a normal life with enough food to eat, and the world keeps finding new ways to take even that. Heartbreak dressed up as a chainsaw.

12. Nezuko Kamado

Anime: Demon Slayer

Nezuko's story opens on the worst day imaginable: her entire family slaughtered, her own humanity stolen, her body twisted into the very thing that killed them. She spends the series fighting to hold onto her heart while the world sees only a monster. Her fierce, wordless love for her brother, buried under a curse, makes her quietly one of anime's saddest hearts.

13. Menma

Anime: Anohana: The Flower We Saw That Day

Menma, whose real name is Meiko Honma, died in an accident as a child and lingers as a gentle presence years later, unable to move on. Her old friends drifted apart under the weight of their guilt and grief, and she returns hoping to bring them back together so she can finally rest. Her innocent wish to see everyone happy one last time makes Anohana's ending an absolute flood of tears. Heartbreak in its purest, kindest form.

14. Eri

Anime: My Hero Academia

Eri is a small girl who spent her earliest years in isolation and mistreatment, used as a tool because of a power she never asked for and could not control. Her fear runs so deep that her rescue becomes one of the most emotionally charged arcs in the series. Every step toward her first real smile feels like a small miracle. Pure heart, quietly devastating.

15. Meliodas

Anime: The Seven Deadly Sins

Meliodas hides centuries of grief behind a lazy grin. Cursed with immortality, he is doomed to watch the woman he loves die and reincarnate over and over, remembering every single loss while she begins again fresh each time. Thousands of years of the same heartbreak, carried with a smile. One of anime's quietest and longest-running tragedies.

16. Lucy

Anime: Elfen Lied

Lucy was born different in a world that punished her for it, bullied and experimented on until cruelty was all she had ever known. Torn between an innocent child's heart and unimaginable rage, she becomes both victim and monster, shaped entirely by the pain others inflicted on her. Her real tragedy is that she was never once given the chance to be anything else.

17. Tomura Shigaraki

Anime: My Hero Academia

Before he was a villain, Tomura was a little boy named Tenko who only ever wanted to be a hero. Raised in a cruel home that ended in one horrific accident, he was left alone and broken, then slowly shaped into a weapon by the worst possible person. His story is a gut-wrenching reminder that villains are so often made, not born.

18. Miyo Saimori

Anime: My Happy Marriage

Miyo grew up neglected and mistreated by the family that should have protected her, made to feel worthless in her own home. By the time she is sent away to marry a stranger, she has all but given up on the idea that anyone could ever truly care for her. Watching her slowly learn to accept kindness is as tender as her past is painful.

19. Kaori Miyazono

Anime: Your Lie in April

Kaori lit up every scene she was in, a free-spirited violinist who pulled a grieving pianist back toward the music. But behind that bright smile was an illness she hid from nearly everyone, determined to burn as brightly as she could for as long as she had left. Her story is a bittersweet reminder to live fully, and it leaves almost no one dry-eyed.

20. Nagisa Furukawa

Anime: Clannad

Nagisa is gentle, frail, and endlessly kind, the emotional heart of one of anime's most famously devastating stories. Her struggles and the heartbreak that follows in Clannad: After Story have been reducing grown adults to tears for years now. Few characters embody the beauty and the cruelty of an ordinary life quite as fully as she does.

Honorable mentions

The feels train has plenty more stops: Gaara enduring one of the loneliest childhoods in Naruto, Tanjiro Kamado carrying his sister and his grief across Demon Slayer, Rem loving a Subaru who keeps forgetting her in Re:Zero, Isla saying an impossible goodbye in Plastic Memories, and Lucy from Cyberpunk: Edgerunners left to chase a shared dream alone after one of anime's most devastating endings. Bring tissues for those too.

Why the feels train never stops

The saddest anime characters stick with us precisely because their pain feels real. Whether it is Guts refusing to fall, Itachi carrying an impossible secret, or Violet learning what love means far too late, these are the arcs that turn a good show into one you never forget. Sadness, done right, is just another word for depth. Keep the tissues close.

FAQs

Who is the saddest anime character?

It is hotly debated, but Guts from Berserk, Itachi Uchiha, and Hyakkimaru from Dororo are the names that come up most often. Each has endured a lifetime of loss that would break almost anyone. If you want a single answer to who is the saddest anime character, Guts is the most common pick.

Who has the saddest backstory in anime?

Guts, Hyakkimaru, and Nico Robin are frequent answers to who has the saddest backstory in anime, each shaped by childhood tragedy and years of loss. Demon Slayer's cast, from Nezuko to the Hashira, also carries some of the most heartbreaking pasts in the whole medium.

Why are sad anime characters so popular?

Sadness creates depth. A sad anime character we have watched suffer and endure becomes far more meaningful than one who breezes through life untouched. Tragic backstories make the triumphs land harder and the losses hit deeper, which is why fans return to these characters again and again.

What are the most depressing anime backstories?

Sad anime characters do not get much heavier than this. Some of the most depressing anime characters include Crona from Soul Eater, Lucy from Elfen Lied, and Tomura Shigaraki, all shaped by isolation and cruelty as children. Their stories are heavy, but that weight is exactly what gives their series so much of its power.

Which sad anime characters get the happiest endings?

Not every tragic story stays tragic. Nezuko, Thorfinn, and Miyo Saimori all endure devastating pasts and slowly find their way toward peace, proving that even the saddest anime characters can earn a hopeful ending. Those redemption arcs are often the most rewarding payoffs of all.
